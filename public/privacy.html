Volyume Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 May 2026
Last updated: 25 May 2026

This policy explains what Volyume collects, why, and how to delete it. Volyume is a training and nutrition self-coaching tool. We are private by design: no social feed, no community, no targeted advertising, no sale of your data.

1. Who we are

Volyume is operated by the developer of the Volyume mobile app (Android, with iOS planned). Contact for privacy queries:

  • Email: support@volyume.app

This policy applies to the Volyume mobile app (app.volyume) and the marketing site at volyume.app.

2. What data we collect

Account essentials

  • Email address and password hash (managed by Supabase Auth)
  • A randomly-generated user ID (Supabase Auth)
  • First name if you choose to enter one
  • Sign-in metadata (provider, last-seen device, session timestamps)
  • Authentication tokens (encrypted in device secure storage)

Special-category health and nutrition data (UK GDPR Article 9)

The categories below are subject to explicit consent via the in-app Article 9 consent screen at onboarding (see Section 3). Without that consent the engine cannot run; the app cannot function without these categories. You can withdraw consent at any time, which triggers account deletion.

  • Body weight and how it changes over time
  • Body fat percentage, fat-free mass, fat mass when you enter them
  • Everything you log to your food diary (food entries, calories, macros, fibre, sodium, sugar, water)
  • Energy and recovery scores from weekly check-ins
  • Adherence to calorie targets (under, hit, over)
  • Eating-disorder pattern detection signals and flag state
  • SCOFF screener responses at onboarding
  • Menstrual cycle phase, if and when you enter it (future)

Training data

  • Workouts, exercises, sets, reps, weight lifted, rest times, dates
  • Training notes you choose to add to specific exercises or sessions
  • Personal records, volume by muscle, training-block progress

On-device only (never leaves your device)

  • App preferences (units, accessibility settings)
  • Progress photos you take in the camera flow (never synced to cloud at any version)
  • An error ring buffer used for crash diagnostics, redacted before storage. You can view, export, or clear it via Settings → Debug Logs.

Crash and performance telemetry

Crash stack traces and error events sent to Sentry, used to fix bugs. Sensitive fields (weight, intake, body fat percent, fat-free mass, eating-disorder flag state, photos, all field names matching the protected database tables) are redacted before transmission by a server-side audit-tested scrubber. We do not use Sentry for product analytics or behavioural tracking.

Camera and photo library

We access the camera or photo library only when you explicitly scan a barcode, scan a nutrition label, or save a workout share card. These permissions are requested at the moment of use. Photos you take remain on your device.

Health data (iOS HealthKit / Android Health Connect)

If you opt in via Settings → Health, Volyume can:

  • Read your most recent body weight so the morning-weight card picks up entries from your smart scale or wearable
  • Write completed workouts so the Health app shows your training alongside other activity

We do not read heart rate, sleep, HRV, or step data. All health-app access is opt-in and revocable from the OS Settings app at any time.

Subscription and payment data

  • Receipt and transaction tokens from Google Play (and, when iOS launches, Apple App Store)
  • Current tier, trial state, locked-in price, and tier change history

We do not handle card details. The store handles payment.

Data we do not collect

  • Heart rate, HRV, sleep stages
  • Precise location (we do not use GPS)
  • Browsing history outside the app
  • Contacts or address book
  • Social-graph data (no friends, followers, leaderboards)

4. Where data is stored

  • Cloud data: Supabase (EU region), a named sub-processor in Section 6. Hosted in the EU; never leaves the UK/EU at v1.
  • Local data: on your device in encrypted secure storage (EncryptedSharedPreferences on Android, Keychain on iOS) for tokens, and SQLite for training history, food diary, and rollups.

5. Who can see it

Only:

  • You, on your devices, and from any other device signed in to the same account
  • The Volyume support team, under strict access controls, only when investigating a support ticket you have raised, and never the raw eating-disorder pattern flag data
  • The authorised sub-processors named in Section 6, each with a contractual obligation to process the data only for the purpose described

7. How long we keep your data

  • Active accounts: indefinitely while your account remains active.
  • Deleted accounts: all cloud-stored personal data is wiped within 30 days of your deletion request. Local data is wiped immediately on the device that initiated the deletion.
  • Anonymised engine metrics (no link back to you) are kept indefinitely for product improvement.
  • Crash and error logs: 90 days in Sentry, then aged out.
  • Notification logs: 30 days, then aged out.

8. Your rights

Under UK and EU GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold on you
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erase your account and all associated data (Settings → Account → Delete account)
  • Port your data in a machine-readable format (Settings → Privacy → Download my data, emails you a CSV bundle)
  • Restrict processing, or object to particular processing activities
  • Withdraw consent for the Article 9 health and nutrition processing at any time, which deletes your account because the engine cannot run without that data
  • Lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or your local EU supervisory authority

To exercise any of these rights, email support@volyume.app. We respond to verifiable requests within 30 days.

9. International transfers

At v1, your data does not leave the UK or EU. Supabase hosts the Volyume database in an EU region. If we ever need to add a sub-processor outside the UK/EU we will update this policy and notify users.

10. Children

Volyume is for users 16 and over. We do not knowingly serve accounts under 16.

If you are under 16 and want something like Volyume, we recommend chatting with a parent and a GP about how to track training and food in a way that's right for you.

If we discover an account belongs to someone under 16, we delete the account and refund any active subscription.

11. Breach notification

If Volyume discovers a breach of unsecured health information affecting 500 or more people, we will notify affected individuals and the U.S. Federal Trade Commission at the same time, without unreasonable delay and no later than 60 calendar days after discovery. We will also notify the UK ICO under the UK GDPR timeline (72 hours) where applicable. The most sensitive table in our system is the eating-disorder pattern flags; any incident affecting that table is treated as highest priority.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy materially we will update the "Last updated" date at the top and notify signed-in users via in-app push. Email notification is added at v1.1. Minor clarifications may be made without notice. The current version is always available in the app under Settings → Privacy and at volyume.app/privacy.
