Getting started

Download Volyume from the App Store on iPhone or from Google Play on Android, then sign in. The first time the app opens, it asks for your agreement to hold your health-related data. The app cannot work without it.

Next you choose how to begin. Whichever you pick, you can change your mind later.

  • Set up coachingA short guided setup of your details, how you train and your goal. It builds your first plan and your targets.
  • Pick a ready-made planChoose from 58 plans and start training. You can set up coaching later from the Coach tab.
  • Build your ownWrite your own plan by hand, day by day.

Signing in

You sign in with Apple, with Google on Android, or with an email address and a password. There is no guest mode, because your training and food logs are backed up to your account.

To add or remove a way to sign in, open Settings → Account → Sign-in methods. If you signed up with Apple or Google, you can add an email address and password there as another way in.

If you forget your password

On the sign-in screen, type your email address, then tap Forgot your password? If that address has an account, we send a link to get back in. Open the link on the phone you use for Volyume, then choose a new password of at least 8 characters. If the email is not in your inbox, look in your spam or junk folder.

Moving to a new phone

Install Volyume on the new phone and sign in with the same account. Your training and food logs are backed up to your account, so they come back when you sign in.

Your progress photos and workout videos stay on the phone they were taken on. They do not move with your account.

Before you stop using the old phone, open Settings → Your data. Cloud sync shows when your data was last saved online, and you can tap it to save again now.

Your data

You can take a copy of your data from Settings → Your data.

  • Export workout logYour logged sets, as a spreadsheet file.
  • Coach handover reportYour training, trend, targets and coaching decisions, as a PDF for your coach.
  • Back up app dataA backup file you can restore later. Your progress photo files stay on your phone unless you choose to share or export them, so they are not in it.

Deleting your account

Open Settings → Account → Delete account. Your account, your data in the cloud, your Community profile and the data on your phone are removed. Cloud removal starts straight away, and backup copies are purged within 30 days. If you are offline when you confirm, Volyume tells you and finishes the job when you reconnect.

The full detail is in the privacy policy.

A note on healthy training

Volyume includes safety limits that protect you from under-fuelling: it holds your calorie target steady if it sees a concerning pattern, and never pushes you below safe minimums. If you are struggling with food or your relationship with eating, support is available in the UK from Beat: beateatingdisorders.org.uk.
