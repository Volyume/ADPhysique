Why Volyume works differently

A general chatbot has to make its answer from the information available to it. If something important is missing, it can still give you an answer that sounds convincing, and it may answer differently if you word the question another way. That is not how Volyume decides what you should change.

Volyume already has the history you have been building as you train.

You are already logging it

So you never have to explain your training again. Volyume follows what actually happens over time: the sessions you complete, the exercises, sets, reps and weights, how your lifts are progressing, how your bodyweight is moving, what you have eaten and what you tell it about recovery.

At your weekly check-in it puts those together. If something genuinely needs changing, it tells you what and why. If the right answer is to stay the course, it says that instead.

  • Based on what you actually did.Uses what you have logged rather than filling in missing history.
  • Consistent advice.The same situation will not suddenly produce different coaching because you asked again.
  • It knows when not to change things.Without enough to go on it can stay the course, instead of inventing a reason to adjust.
  • You are always in control.It explains what it recommends, and its weekly suggestions only take effect when you apply them.
