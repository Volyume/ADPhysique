Not an AI fitness chatbot

AI can guess. Volyume doesn't.

Ask a general AI chatbot what to change about your training and it has to come up with an answer from whatever you have just told it. Word the question differently, leave something out, or ask again tomorrow, and the advice can change. It can sound completely convincing even when it does not really have enough to go on.

Volyume works differently.

You never have to explain your training to it, because you are already logging it as you go.

It follows what you actually do week to week: the sessions you finish, how your lifts are moving, the way your weight is trending, what you have eaten and how recovered you have been feeling.

At your weekly check-in it puts all of that together and works out one thing. Are you progressing the way you should be, does something genuinely need changing, or is the right answer simply to carry on as you are. Then it tells you why.

  • No invented answers. What you are told comes from what you have actually logged, not from a chatbot filling in the gaps.
  • No random changes. If nothing has really changed in your training, the advice does not suddenly change either, just because you asked again.
  • No pretending to know. When there is not enough there yet to justify changing something, it says so and keeps you on course instead of guessing.
  • You stay in control. It explains what it suggests and why, and nothing changes until you say so. It never rebuilds your training behind your back.
