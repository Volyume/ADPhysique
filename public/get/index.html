Get Volyume

Volyume is on the App Store for iPhone and on Google Play for Android. It plans your training, logs your workouts and food, and once a week tells you what changes and why.

For iPhoneApp Store For AndroidGoogle Play

What you need

A phone

An iPhone, or a phone that runs Android.

An account

On iPhone, sign in with Apple or with an email address. On Android, sign in with Apple, Google or an email address. There is no guest mode. Your training and food logs are backed up to your account, so they come with you to a new phone.

Signal, some of the time

Logging workouts, the rest timer, your plan and your food diary all work without signal. The foods that come with the app search without signal once they have loaded the first time. Looking further for a food, Community and backing up need a connection.

Your agreement

Before the app opens, it asks for your agreement to hold your health-related data. You can withdraw it later in Settings.

Ways to start

You choose when you first open the app. Whichever you pick, you can set up coaching or change plan later.

  • Set up coachingA short guided setup of your details, how you train and your goal. It builds your first plan and your targets.
  • Pick a ready-made planChoose from 58 plans and start training. You can set up coaching later from the Coach tab.
  • Build your ownWrite your own plan by hand, day by day.

Your data

Your progress photos and workout videos stay on this phone, unless you choose to share them. Your data on the phone is held in an encrypted database, and you can delete your account and its data in Settings.

Read the privacy policy or get help.

Training, food and a weekly check-in, with a coach that shows its reasons. On iPhone and Android.
