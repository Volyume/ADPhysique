The eating-disorder safety messages (verbatim)

Hold (lockout)

"We've held your calorie cut. We've noticed a few signals together: your weight has been dropping faster than your intake suggests, your energy scores have been low, and your food log shows you eating less than your target for a few weeks running. Even when a cut is going well in numbers, sustained low energy is a safety signal. We'd rather pause than push. Once your fuelling and energy recover for two weeks, we'll suggest new targets."

Plus a "Read more" explainer about relative energy deficiency, a locale-specific support link (Beat in the UK), and "You can keep using Volyume normally… Only the calorie target stops shifting."

Hold lifted

"The signals that triggered the hold have settled for two weeks. We're back to the standard coach output… Take this gently. Energy recovery beats rushing back into a deep cut."

Acted immediately (rapid loss)

"Your weight dropped more than 1.5% this week and your energy is low. We're not waiting two weeks to react; we've bumped your daily target up immediately. This isn't a punishment for hitting your goal too fast. It's a safety call. Steady is the goal."

Triggers: rapid loss (≥1.5%/week), low energy (≥2 weeks), under-eating (2 of 3 weeks), weight-only check-ins. Fires on 2 stacked signals (or 3 if the user set an advanced goal-lock).

6. Onboarding, paywall & settings

Welcome screen

Pro "The coach who writes back." Free "The logbook a coach would write in." Pro bullets: a plan around your schedule/goals/level; Precision Coaching that adjusts training and nutrition; personalised calorie and protein targets; a written reason for every change.

Pro onboarding (the option lists)

Experience: Beginner (<18 months) · Intermediate (18mo–3yr) · Advanced (3–5yr) · Competitive (5yr+). Session length: 45 / 60 / 75 / 90 min. Days/week: 3 / 4 / 5 / 6. Equipment: Full Gym · Machines & Cables · Dumbbells Only · Barbell & Plates · Home Gym · Bodyweight. Recovery: Poor · Average · Good.

How it explains itself: "Each morning you weigh in. Once a week you check in. Your coach reads the trend and adjusts your calories and training. Logging your food makes each call more accurate, and your weight trend carries the rest."

Health-data consent (Article 9)

Lists what it uses (weight, body fat & lean mass, food diary, weekly check-ins, eating-habits screening), what it never does ("Never sell it" / "Never share it with advertisers" / "Never use it to train a public AI model"), and where it lives ("On your phone, in encrypted local storage" / UK servers with row-level security / "removed within 30 days" on deletion). Checkbox: "I agree to Volyume using my health and nutrition data to coach me."

Two flags from the audit live here: the "within 30 days" line (the code deletes immediately), and the eating-habits screening is named but the automated eating-disorder detection isn't.
