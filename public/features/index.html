Training

Your plan, the workout logger, and everything around a session. Plans are built from 923 exercises, and you can add your own.

Your plan

Today and the next workout
Today opens on the next session in your plan, with one button to start it. Options lets you look through it first, do a blank workout instead, or skip it just this once.
Start without a plan
You can start logging straight away without choosing a plan.
Train tab
Your active plan, where you are in it this week, the next workout and your saved workouts.
Plan library
58 ready-made plans, searchable and grouped into collections such as kettlebell, dumbbells only, bodyweight, bands, short sessions and beginner.
Adjust training plan
Rebuild your plan from your goal, 2 to 6 training days a week, session length, equipment, experience and the muscles you most want to bring up. You see the new plan beside your current one before anything changes, and if it cannot build one it says why.
Training styles
6 ways to share out your plan’s work if you are not competing. Balanced trains every muscle evenly. V-taper adds back and shoulder work for width. X-frame puts shoulders, back and legs first, for proportion top to bottom. Upper-body density trains chest and back more often. Powerbuilder keeps heavy squat, bench and deadlift-style lifts at the centre, with muscle-building work around them. Minimalist keeps each muscle at its weekly minimum, for shorter sessions.
Create your own plan
Build a plan by hand: pick the days and exercises, and add supersets and circuits. Change a workout’s exercises, rep range, rest and starting weight later.
Plan pages
Open any plan to see its days, workouts and weekly sets. Add a library plan to your own, make one active, edit it or archive it.
Plan folders
File plans into folders, archive old ones, restore them, or start a plan again from the beginning.
Training blocks
Your training runs in blocks of a few weeks that end with a lighter recovery week. See your current block, past blocks and your weekly load.
Recovery weeks
A planned lighter week at the end of a block, with fewer and easier sets. A longer break is only ever suggested, never started for you.
Choosing your next block
At the end of a block it shows what stays, what changes and what is new. Keep the plan, adjust it or pick something else, and look back at what the block showed.
Plan updates
When the plan builder could do better for you, it offers an update and changes nothing until you agree.
Injuries and limitations
Tell the app what shapes your training, and your plan is built around how you train.
Session length
Tell the app how long your sessions usually run, so plans and suggestions fit.
Quick full-body session
Say what equipment you have to hand and it fills a one-off full-body workout from that kit, without touching your plan.
Create a one-off workout
Build a workout from scratch, choose sets, reps and rest, and start it.
Exercise library
923 built-in exercises, each with written notes on how to set up, how to do it and what to watch for, searchable by muscle and equipment. Add your own exercises too.
Import from Hevy or Strong
Bring past workouts across from a Hevy or Strong export file, with a preview before anything is saved.

During a workout

The workout logger
Log the weight and reps of each set, or time or distance where that fits the exercise. Targets for each set come from your recent sessions, and a line under the entry tells you when a number would beat your best.
How are you feeling today?
An optional question before a session about sleep, soreness and energy. A rough night can make the session a little easier. A good one never adds work. You can switch the question off.
Welcome back
After a long gap it asks if you have still been training, then adjusts how hard it starts you back. It never changes which workout comes next.
Kinds of set
Mark each set as what it was: a working set, a warm-up, or a drop set, where the weight is lowered straight after. Myo-reps are a hard first set followed by short mini-sets after brief rests. Rest-pause means going as far as you can, resting 10 to 20 seconds, then squeezing out more reps. An AMRAP set is as many reps as you can manage. Warm-ups stay out of your weekly count of hard sets.
Warm-up sets
One tap adds a ladder of lighter sets up to today’s working weight. It only appears when you ask for it.
Supersets and circuits
Pair exercises back to back, or run a circuit with a rest between rounds.
One side at a time
For single-arm and single-leg moves it offers to log the first side, then the other, as one set.
Use last session
Fills this session’s waiting sets with what you did last time.
Rest timer
Starts when you log a set (you can turn that off), with a large countdown, buttons to add or take off time or skip, and a sound and a buzz at the end.
Rest for one exercise
Set how long you rest between sets for a single exercise.
Rest finished alert
A sound and a buzz when rest ends, even when the phone is locked.
Rest countdown on the lock screen Android only
On Android, while you rest with the app in the background, a live countdown stays on the lock screen, with buttons to add time or skip the rest.
Make rest alerts exact Android only
Lets Android sound the rest alert on time, instead of letting the battery saver delay it.
Swap an exercise
Replace an exercise mid-workout, for today or from now on, from the alternatives the plan lists or from the whole library.
I cannot do this
Tell it a movement is off the table, and that kind of exercise is left out of suggestions.
Shorten the session
Trims the rest of today’s session to fit the time you have left. You can undo it.
Change the order
Rearrange, add or drop exercises during a session.
History and records
Look up how you did on an exercise before, and your best set, reps and session, for all time or recent months.
Notes
A note on a set, a note on the session, and a note to yourself for next time.
Workout in progress
If you leave a session open, a bar on every tab takes you back to it.
Personal bests
Spots a new best for you: the heaviest weight, the best estimated one-rep max (the most you could lift once), the most reps at a weight, and for assisted lifts the least help used.
Workout summary
After you finish: the total weight lifted, working sets, time, any new bests, how the session felt, and a link to this week’s coaching decision.
Save a workout
Keep a finished workout to start again later.
Set videos
Record a silent video of a set, up to 3 in a workout. Videos are private on your device only, unless you choose to share them. They are kept for 7 days unless you choose longer, and one can sit behind a share image.

Food

A food diary, quick ways to add food, and a meal builder that plans to your targets. The app comes with over 29,000 foods, built around UK products, and searches them without signal once they have loaded the first time.

The food diary
One page a day: a calorie ring, bars for protein, carbs and fat, your meals, planned and eaten food shown apart, and water. Swipe to change day.
Meals a day
Choose how many meals you eat in a day and name them.
Search
Search foods or brands, with tabs for recent, suggested, favourite and often-eaten foods. Online, it can look further than the foods that come with the app.
Around this time
Offers the foods you usually eat at about this time of day.
Type what you ate
Type “two eggs, toast and a tea” and it splits that into foods for you to check before adding.
Scan a barcode
Point the camera at a barcode to find the food, or type the number in.
Scan the label
If a barcode is not found, point the camera at the nutrition label. Your phone reads the figures and fills in a new food for you to check.
Quick add
Log calories now, with protein, carbs and fat if you know them.
Your own foods
Make a food with its calories, protein, carbs, fat, fibre, sugars and salt, named portions, and vitamins and minerals if you have them.
Food details
Pick the amount and portion, switch between raw and cooked weight for staples that have both, add a note, set the time you ate it, and see vitamins and minerals where the food data has them.
Water
Add water a glass at a time, towards a daily target you set.
Copy a day
Repeat yesterday, or copy a whole day or one meal from an earlier day.
Select several
Move foods to another meal, copy them to today, save them as a meal or delete them together.
Saved meals
Save a go-to meal once and add it again in one tap.
Recipes
Build a recipe from its ingredients, with servings and an optional cooked weight, or bring one in from a web link. Log it by servings or by grams.
Meal builder
Plans today’s meals or the week ahead from 94 built-in meals and your own saved meals and recipes, aimed at your targets for calories, protein, carbs and fat. Nothing counts until you mark it eaten.
Meal builder choices
Set meals a day, how much variety you want, how many oily fish meals a week, and your dietary needs.
Swap a meal or a food
Swap one meal or one food for a close match, just this once or from now on, or tell it never to suggest a food again.
Add a day or a week
Add a planned day or week to the diary. It fills only the meals you have not logged yet.
Repeat a day
Copy a planned day onto other days.
Shopping list
A list for the week’s plan, in the weights you buy (raw or dry), with the cooked weight beside it, ready to share.
Higher-calorie day
Plan a bigger day, and the difference is spread across the rest of the week so the week’s total stays the same.
Your targets
Your daily calories and protein, carbs and fat are worked out from your details, your goal and how active you are, with the reasoning shown and the full working there to open. You can change the protein level and the number of meals in your diary.
See why
When your targets change, a note on the diary explains why.
Trends
Your calories, protein, how often you land within range of each target, and an average of vitamins and minerals, over periods from a week to three months. Save a report as a PDF or a spreadsheet file.
Dietary needs
Set your diet (vegan, vegetarian, pescatarian or anything), allergens to leave out and foods never to suggest. Meal plans and suggestions follow it. It filters suggestions; it is not a guarantee, so check the label.
Fibre, sugars and salt
Choose whether fibre, sugars and salt show on a food’s details.
Nutrition basics
A short guide to calories, protein, carbs and fat for anyone new to them.
Import a food diary
Bring a food history across from a MyFitnessPal or Cronometer export.
Share a label back
An optional switch to share a label you scanned with Open Food Facts, a public food database, so the next person finds it. Off until you turn it on.

Progress

Where your training stands, what each muscle has had this week, and your photos.

Progress tab
A top card on where you stand with training, your body and your photos, then recovery, your recent sessions and links to everything below.
Lifts
Every lift you have trained, ranked by how your best estimated lift is trending, with an overall strength level from Beginner to Elite and how each lift compares with your body weight.
Exercise pages
For one exercise: a strength trend chart, your records, recent sessions, similar exercises, and a target weight you can set and track. It notes when a lift has stopped moving.
Weight lifted
A weekly chart of the total weight you lifted, with a share image.
Consistency
Sessions each week, weeks in a row and session milestones, shown without judgement.
Muscle map
A body figure showing how many hard sets each muscle got this week, in plain bands. Tap through to any muscle.
Recovery
An estimate, for each muscle, of how recovered it is and when it should be ready again, based on what you logged.
Weekly sets review
The week’s sets for each muscle, against what your plan is aiming for.
Full history
Every past workout. Open its summary or its videos, and delete videos.
Recaps
A story of your month once you have logged enough sessions, and of your year once your first session is a year old.
Body measurements
Log tape measurements and your weight in kg, stone or pounds, and see them over time. Volyume does not measure body fat. If you have a figure from your own test, you can enter it and say how it was measured. Your weight is one of several things your coach reads.
Progress photos
Take photos with a faint outline of your last one over the camera, so you can line up the same pose, or import them. They stay on your phone, with location data removed. Browse by front, back or side.
Compare and trend
Put two sets of photos side by side, or look through the change across all of them.
Volyume Score
A score from your own photos, front and back, worked out on your phone and shown with a confidence level. It is not a body-fat measurement, and it can decline to score a photo it cannot read well.
Share images
Turn a session, a new best, a milestone or a weekly recap into an image or a video, in story, full-screen, feed, square or sticker shapes, light or dark, over a photo, a set video or a plain background. Save it or share it.
Set as wallpaper Android only
On Android, you can set a full-screen share image as your lock screen wallpaper.
Two-photo card
An image of two of your photos side by side with their dates. You start it, and nothing is posted for you.
Athlete profile
Your name and picture, sessions completed, strength level, how up to date your data is, and the starting points your coach works from.

Weekly coaching

The weekly check-in, the decision that follows it, and the settings that shape it. How the coach works explains the rules it follows.

Ways to start
Set up coaching, pick a ready-made plan, or build your own. You can set up coaching later from the Coach tab.
Coaching setup
A short guided setup: your details, how you train (experience, session length, days and equipment), your gym, your goal and training style, and a protein level. It then builds your first plan and sets up reminders.
Coach tab
Your latest decision, this week’s check-in, your week and your setup, in one place.
Weekly check-in
Once a week, a few quick questions about how you felt, your recovery and how training went, beside the week’s logs. A shorter version appears when your logs already answer most of it. You choose the day.
Coaching decision
Each week it says what it saw, what changes in your calories and training, what stays the same and why, and when it looks again. Apply a change, or keep things as they are.
It waits rather than guesses
When there is not enough to go on, or a change is too recent to judge, it keeps things as they are and tells you why in plain words.
How much it does on its own
Collaborative, the default: your coach suggests each change and you tap to apply it. Manual: you make the changes yourself. Coached: the week’s changes apply when you open the decision, and some always wait for you to confirm.
Coaching tone
Choose how your coach words things: Automatic, Supportive (plainer, with a little more explanation) or Precise (shorter, numbers first).
How it works, in the app
A page in the app on what your coach reads, why changes wait, why some things stay the same, how training changes, recovery weeks, and what it cannot do.
Show the science
Optional: the technical name appears in brackets beside the plain label.
Coaching history
Every past weekly decision, including the weeks it kept things the same.
Your week
One page with the week’s story: what you trained, what you ate and your coach’s decision.
Change your goal
Change what you are focused on, your protein target and your training setup. A summary then shows what changed.
Physique categories
If you compete, choose one of 8 physique categories, such as Men’s Physique, Classic Physique, Bikini or Wellness, and your plan’s emphasis shifts to match.
Muscles to bring up
Choose the muscles you most want to bring up, and your plan puts extra work into them.
Stalled lifts
A quiet note on Today when a lift you still train has stopped moving, with a tap through to it.
Today line
One line on Today with the most useful thing right now, such as a new decision, a lighter week or a check-in waiting.
Cycle tracking
Optional, and offered only on profiles set to female: one extra check-in question, so your coach can keep your targets steady around your period.
Coaching reminders
Reminders for the weekly check-in, a missed check-in, a morning weigh-in and confirming planned meals.

Community

An optional place to train alongside other people. You can look around before you join. Posting, following and messages need a profile.

Feed, People, Groups and You
The four parts of the Community tab: posts, people to follow, your groups, and your own profile.
Join
Make a profile with a username, a picture, your gym, what you train for, who can follow you and what you share, then agree to a few short rules.
Feed
Posts from people you follow, people at your gym, your groups or everyone, newest first or most respected first.
Posts
Write a short note, or post the session you just finished. A new best or a milestone can be posted from its share image.
Session posts
Once you join, each finished session can post for you. You choose who sees those posts before your profile is made, and you can turn them off.
Respect and comments
Give a post Respect, the app’s thumbs-up, or comment on it. One tap sends Respect to everyone in your gym or group who trained today.
Find people
Find people who train like you, at your gym, near you or in your discipline. Follow them, send a connection request with a reason, and see who trained today.
Search
Search Community for people and groups.
Messages
Once two people have accepted each other, they can send each other text messages, one to one.
Groups
Make or join a group, see each other’s weeks, and chat as a group in short text messages.
Boards
A ranked list for your gym, the people you follow, a group or everyone, by week, by month or by consistency. Smaller groups see an unranked list instead.
Training profile
Short summaries of how you train, such as your training days, times and main lifts, that you switch on one at a time.
Open to training together
Show that you are looking for someone to train with, at your gym if you like.
Training now
Optional: people who follow you, and your groups, can see that you are mid-session. It clears when you finish.
Gym directory
Choose your gym from a list, search by name or distance, or add one that is missing. Your location is used only if you ask, once, to list nearby gyms, and it is not stored.
Block, mute and report
Block or mute anyone. Report a person, post, comment or group from where you see it.
Community privacy
Choose who can follow you and who can send connection requests, show or hide your gym and your town, choose what you share of your training, and turn training now on or off. Leave Community whenever you like.
Activity
Follow requests, connection requests and other activity in one place.
Community on Today
A row on Today shows how many of the people you follow trained today.
Gym challenges
A gym can run a scored challenge for its members. If yours does, you join it with its link or code.
Friends on the widget Android only
On Android, the home-screen widgets can show how many of the people you follow trained today.

Privacy and your data

What happens to your data, and the controls you have over it. The privacy policy has the full detail.

Your agreement first
Before the app opens, it asks for your agreement to hold your health-related data. You can withdraw it later.
An account, no guest mode
You sign in with Apple, with Google on Android, or with an email address and password.
Works without signal
Logging works with no signal. Your data is saved on your phone and backed up to your account when you are online.
Encrypted on your phone
Your data on the phone is held in an encrypted database.
Photos and videos
Your progress photos and workout videos stay on this phone, unless you choose to share them.
App lock
Optional: ask for Face ID, your fingerprint or the phone’s passcode each time you open the app. Off unless you turn it on.
Usage data
A switch for sharing usage data: counts of which features get used and where the app is slow, never your training, food or body data. On unless you turn it off.
News by email
A switch for emails about new features and surveys. On unless you turn it off, and every email has a link to stop them.
Delete your account
Removes your account, your data on our servers and on your phone, and your Community profile, and withdraws your agreement.
No marketing notifications
Volyume does not send marketing notifications. Reminders are set on your phone, and you control them.
The policy in the app
The full privacy policy can be read inside the app.

Settings

How the app looks, the units it uses, reminders and your account.

Ways to sign in
Continue with Apple on any phone, Continue with Google on Android, or Continue with email, with a way to reset a forgotten password. Add or remove sign-in methods later.
Display
Dark, light or match the phone, larger text, higher contrast, a colour-blind safe palette, reduce motion, and a separate switch for vibration.
Units
Body weight in stone, kg or pounds, and food energy in kcal or kJ. Gym weights are always in kg.
Workout settings
Session length, default rest time, whether rest starts on its own, the rest finished alert and rest sounds.
Notifications
Training reminders, getting-started notes, a welcome-back note, Community alerts with their own quiet hours, meal reminders at times you pick (off unless you turn them on), and quiet hours.
Calmer coaching
A switch for quieter prompts. Your plan and your numbers stay the same.
Dietary needs
Your diet, allergens to leave out and foods to avoid.
Profile
Name, sex, height, date of birth and diet.
Account
See how you sign in, sign out once your data is backed up, or delete your account.
Backing up
See when your data last backed up, back it up now, and refresh the food library.
Save and restore
Save your app data to a file and restore it later, or restore the copy made before each app update. Photos stay on the phone.
Export
Your workout log as a spreadsheet file, and a PDF report for your coach with your training, trends, targets and coaching decisions.
Import
Workouts from Hevy or Strong, and food from MyFitnessPal or Cronometer.
Video storage
See how many set videos the phone holds and the space they use, choose how long they are kept, or delete them all.
Clear workout history
Delete your logged sessions and the bests worked out from them.
Home-screen widgets Android only
Next session and Weekly consistency. They show a plan name and session counts, never weight, calories or body data.
Shortcuts on the app icon
Press and hold the app icon for Start workout and Log food.
Shake to send feedback
Shake the phone firmly and a feedback sheet opens.
Help
Common questions answered inside the app, with no signal needed.
Feedback and ratings
Send a quick rating with an optional note, or rate Volyume in the store.
Credits
Credits for the food data the app uses, including Open Food Facts and the UK government’s CoFID tables.
What’s new
A short note on what changed in your version.
