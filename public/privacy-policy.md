# Volyume Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 26 September 2026
**Last updated:** 21 October 2026

This policy explains what Volyume collects, why, how it is stored, and how to delete it. Volyume is a training and nutrition self-coaching tool. Your health data stays private: it is never shown to other people, used for advertising or sold. Community, where you can follow other lifters and share your workouts, is optional, and section 5 explains what it shares.

## 1. Who we are

Volyume is operated by the developer of the Volyume mobile app. For privacy queries, contact support@volyume.app.

## 2. Data we collect

When you create an account we collect your email address, a user ID, sign-in metadata, and any first name you choose to enter.

To run the app and coach you, Volyume may use:

- Profile details such as age, sex, height, training goal, schedule and equipment
- Workouts, exercises, sets, reps, weight lifted, routines, programmes and training notes
- Body weight, body measurements, body-fat percentage and lean mass when you enter them
- Food diary entries, recipes, saved meals, water, calories and macro targets
- Weekly check-ins, recovery, energy, adherence and eating-habits screening responses
- Progress photo metadata and Volyume Score analysis metadata, including photo quality, result confidence, leanness band, visual score and progress change
- Short workout videos you choose to record (up to 15 seconds, no sound), kept on your device only
- Usage events that tell us which app surfaces are used and where the app is slow
- Crash and diagnostic events, scrubbed before they leave the device
- If you join Community: your Community profile, posts, comments, reactions, messages, groups and the people you follow (section 5)

Progress photo image files stay on your device unless you choose to share or export them. Workout video files stay on your device too: they are never uploaded, are not included in backups, and are deleted when you delete the workout or your account. To save space, a video is removed from your device after 7 days unless you tap Keep on it in Your videos; you can choose a longer time, or to keep every video, in Settings under Your data. Volyume Score is a visual progress feature, not an exact body-fat percentage, DEXA scan, diagnosis, or medical assessment.

## 3. Why we collect this data

Volyume uses your data to run your account, sync your own records, calculate training and nutrition targets, show progress, run the deterministic coaching system, and protect against unsafe under-fuelling patterns. We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it to train a public AI model.

Under UK and EU GDPR, core account operation is processed under contract, and health/nutrition data is processed with explicit Article 9 consent. Crash diagnostics and first-party product telemetry are processed under legitimate interest. Community is processed under contract, because it is a feature you choose to use, and keeping it safe is our legitimate interest. You can switch usage telemetry off in Settings > Privacy & legal. Usage data records which screens open and for how long, when something fails or takes too long, and counts of what happens in the app, such as how many sets a finished workout had or whether you logged food that day. It never includes the content: no weights, foods, amounts, notes, names or messages, and nothing about injuries and limitations or Volyume's wellbeing checks. Before you agree to the health data terms, the only usage data recorded is whether the app was slow or failed. Usage data is kept for six months and then deleted, and it is used only to improve the app.

## 4. Where data is stored

Local data is stored on your device. Auth tokens use secure device storage. Training, nutrition, body metrics, progress metadata and settings live in SQLite.

Cloud-backed account data is stored in Supabase in the EU region, protected by row-level security so only you, and the team supporting your account when needed, can see it. Progress photo, scan image and workout video files are not cloud-synced by Volyume.

## 5. Community

Community is optional. Nothing you log is shown to other people unless you join it.

**Your profile.** When you join, you choose a handle and can add a display name, a short bio, your training styles and goal, and your area and gym. Your profile is public unless you set it to followers only, and you can hide your area and gym at any time. It also shows how many sessions you usually do a week, the lifts you focus on and your experience level, and you can hide any of these. You can choose to show more: the days and times you usually train, your age band, and your training consistency, such as how many sessions you have done this week.

**Your workouts are shared by default.** Once you have joined, each workout you finish is posted to Community automatically. The post shows the workout's name and date, how long it took, how many sets you did, the total weight you lifted, the exercises, your best set and how many personal records you set, and up to three of those records are posted alongside it. It never includes your body weight, measurements, food diary, photos or check-ins. Everyone in Community can see these posts, except that if you are under 18 only your followers can. The switch is shown, already on, before you create your profile.

**Turning it off.** You can stop sharing at any time by turning off "Share what I did" in Community, under Training profile, and you can remove what you have already shared at the same time. Automatic sharing also pauses on its own while calm mode is on, or while Volyume's safety check has paused your calorie changes.

**Links outside the app.** A post, profile or training plan shared with everyone can be opened from a link by people who do not use Volyume. Nothing from anyone under 18 is ever shown this way.

**Posts, messages and groups.** You can also write posts, comment, give Respect, share training plans, join groups and message people you are connected with. Messages are private to the people in the conversation. If someone reports a message, our moderators can see it.

**Location.** If you tap "Use my location" in the gym finder, your phone's approximate location is used for that one search and then discarded. It is never stored.

**Notifications.** Community can send you notifications, for example when someone follows you, replies to you or messages you. You can turn them off in Settings, under Notifications.

**Keeping Community safe.** You can block, mute and report people, posts, comments, plans, messages and groups. The Volyume team reviews reports, and a post, comment or plan reported by three different people is hidden automatically.

**Leaving.** You can leave Community at any time in Community, under Privacy. Leaving deletes your profile, posts and follows; your training, plans and food diary are not touched. Deleting your account deletes everything you shared in Community too. Reports you made are kept for moderation, with your account removed from them.

## 6. Third parties

We use a small number of processors to run the app:

| Provider | Purpose | Data |
|---|---|---|
| Supabase | Database, authentication and cloud backup | Cloud-backed account data, never progress-photo image files |
| Sentry | Crash and performance reporting | Scrubbed diagnostic events |
| Expo push | Push notification delivery | Device push token |
| Resend (sends from Ireland, records kept in the United States under the standard contract clauses) | Sending the news emails | Your email address and the email itself |
| Open Food Facts / USDA FoodData Central | Food lookup data | Search terms and barcode values only |

We do not pass data to advertising networks or third-party analytics providers.

## 7. News emails

When you create an account you are told that Volyume will email you about new features, short surveys and a weekly summary. These are the news emails.

**Your choice.** You can turn them off at any time, in the app under Settings with the "News by email" switch, or with the Unsubscribe link in every email. Turning them off does not change anything else in the app, and you can turn them back on in Settings.

**When we do not send.** We do not send news emails while Volyume's safety check has paused your calorie changes.

**Who sends them.** Resend sends these emails for us from Ireland and keeps its email records in the United States under the standard contract clauses. It receives your email address and the email itself, and only for that purpose. We measure whether each news email is opened and which of its links are clicked.

**What the team can see.** To choose who to email, the team can see the last day you opened the app, your app version and how many workouts you logged recently, never what was in them.

**What we keep.** Your email address, a record of each time you turned them on or off and the wording you saw, a log of which news emails were sent to you, and your answers to any survey you take. A survey opened from one of our emails links your answers to your account, so a second answer replaces the first.

**Deleting.** Deleting your account deletes all of this.

## 8. Gym challenges

A gym challenge is optional and runs at one gym for up to a month. You only take part if you join it yourself, and only adults can join.

To score you, the challenge reads your training, your check-ins and Community posts, whether you logged food, whether you weighed in, and whether calm mode is on or the safety check has paused your calorie changes, so it never asks you to log food or weigh in at those times. It never reads what you ate or what you weigh.

If you are in the top 10, the name you choose and your score are shown to the other people in that challenge. Nobody else sees them. Winners are picked by a recorded computer draw. If you win, we use your email address to tell you, and your name and county are published unless you object.

You can leave a challenge at any time. After you leave, your records are not read for it again and you are taken off its board. We use this data with your explicit consent, which you give when you join. Completing a challenge with a final score of 50 or more earns free for life. Deleting your account deletes all of it, except the record of each prize draw, which is kept so the draw can be checked and no longer links to you once your account is gone.

## 9. Health apps

Volyume does not connect to Apple Health or Health Connect. It does not read from or write to any health app on your phone.

## 10. Deletion and retention

While your account is active, we keep your data so the app can work and sync across devices.

If you delete your account from Settings > Account > Delete account, cloud removal starts immediately and local data is wiped on the device that initiated deletion. If final sign-in removal cannot finish while offline, Volyume tells you and completes it when you reconnect. Backup copies are purged within 30 days. This includes your Community profile and everything you shared there (section 5).

Crash and diagnostic events age out according to the retention settings of our Sentry project.

## 11. Your rights

Under UK and EU GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, and port your personal data. In Volyume, Settings > Your data lets you export workout sets as CSV and create a JSON app-data backup. The JSON backup contains database records, including progress photo metadata and Volyume Score analysis metadata, but not private photo image files.

To make a request, use the in-app controls or email support@volyume.app. We respond to verifiable requests within 30 days.

## 12. Children

Volyume is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If we discover an account belongs to someone under 13, we delete it.

In Community, anyone under 18 shares with their followers only and never appears on pages shown outside the app.

## 13. Changes

If we materially change this policy we update the date above and notify signed-in users in the app.

## 14. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: support@volyume.app. You can also contact the UK Information Commissioner's Office at https://ico.org.uk/.
