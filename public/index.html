Training, food and a weekly check-in, with a coach that explains every change.

Volyume plans your training and logs your workouts and food. Once a week it works out whether your plan should change, and tells you why in plain words. By default you tap to apply any change.

Log a workout set by set

Each set is a weight and a number of reps. Targets for each set come from your recent sessions, and a line under the set tells you when the numbers would beat your best.

  • A rest timer starts when you log a set, with a sound and a buzz when rest is over, even with the phone locked.
  • Warm-ups, drop sets and other kinds of set are marked as what they are. Warm-ups stay out of your weekly count of hard sets.
  • Swap an exercise for today or for good, or shorten the session to fit the time you have left.
  • New personal bests are spotted for you: the heaviest weight, the most reps at a weight, and more.
  • Film a set. The video is private on your device only, unless you choose to share it.
Everything the logger does

Plans built around you

Start with one of 58 ready-made plans, or let Volyume build one from your goal, the days you can train, how long your sessions run and the equipment you have. Your training runs in blocks of a few weeks, and each block ends with a lighter recovery week.

  • Choose from 6 training styles if you are not competing in a physique category. Balanced trains every muscle evenly. V-taper puts more work into your back and shoulders for width. Minimalist keeps each muscle at its weekly minimum, for shorter sessions.
  • Tell the app what shapes your training, and your plan is built around how you train.
  • You can also build a plan by hand, with supersets and circuits.
  • When your plan needs a decision from you, Today offers an Update your plan card, and changes nothing until you agree.

Food that fits your day

Find food by searching, scanning a barcode or typing what you ate, such as “two eggs, toast and a tea”. The app comes with over 29,000 foods, built around UK products, and searches them without signal once they have loaded the first time.

  • If a barcode is not found, scan the nutrition label. Your phone reads the figures and fills in a new food for you to check.
  • The meal builder plans a day or a week from 94 built-in meals and your own recipes, aimed at your targets for calories, protein, carbs and fat. Nothing counts until you mark it eaten.
  • A shopping list for the week, in the weights you buy, with the cooked weight beside it.
  • Your targets are worked out from your details and your goal, with the reasoning shown.
All the food features

Once a week, a decision with its reasons

Each week you answer a few quick questions about your energy, sleep, soreness and how training went. Your coach reads them alongside the sessions you did and the food you logged. It then tells you what changes, what stays the same, and why.

  • When there is not enough to go on, or the last change is too recent to judge, it keeps things as they are and tells you the reason.
  • By default your coach suggests each change and you tap to apply it. You can also make every change yourself, or have changes apply when you open the decision.
How the coach works

See what is working

Progress shows how your lifts are moving, how many hard sets each muscle got this week, and an estimate of how recovered each muscle is.

  • Each exercise has its own page, with a strength trend, your records and a target you can set.
  • Progress photos show a faint outline of your last photo, so you can line up the same pose. They stay on your phone unless you choose to share them, and location data is removed.
  • The Volyume Score is a score from your own photos, worked out on your phone and shown with a confidence level. It is not a body-fat measurement, and it can decline to score a photo it cannot read well.
  • Recaps of your month and your year, and share images for new bests.
All the progress features

Train alongside other people

Community is optional. Follow people, see their sessions and new bests, give Respect (the app’s thumbs-up) and comment.

All the Community features
  • Find people who train at your gym or train like you, and send each other messages once you have both accepted.
  • Start or join a group, see each other’s weeks and chat as a group.
  • Once you join, each finished session can post for you. You choose who sees those posts before your profile is made, and you can turn them off.
  • Choose who can follow you, whether your gym shows and what you share. Block, mute or report anyone.

Your data

Works without signal

Your workouts and food logs are saved on your phone first, and backed up to your account when you are online.

Photos and videos stay with you

Your progress photos and workout videos stay on this phone, unless you choose to share them.

A coach that follows fixed rules

Your coach isn’t an AI chatbot. It follows fixed rules. The same information gives the same decision.

Yours to delete

Delete your account in Settings. That removes your data from your phone and from our servers, along with your Community profile. We keep a one-way hash of your email address and the record of any prize draw, which no longer links to you.
