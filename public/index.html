Volyume

A training and food log for people who lift to change how they look.

Log your sets and your meals. Volyume keeps your volume per muscle, your calories and macros, your weight and your lifts in one place, then works out what to change each week. No feed, no followers, no ads.

Get it on Google Play

Android. iPhone later.

What it does

  • Workout logging, with volume per muscle and your personal records.
  • A food diary with a barcode scanner and macro targets.
  • A weekly check-in that adjusts your training and your calories.
  • Your weight and progress, kept over time.

Private by design. Your data is yours, it stays in the UK and EU, and you can delete all of it whenever you want. We do not sell it and we do not run ads.
