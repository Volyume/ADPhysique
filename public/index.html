How it works

Volyume delivers something useful before your first workout, then keeps the loop tight from there.

STEP 1

Tell it about you

Your goal, your history, your numbers. No defaults filled in for you, and nothing assumed.

STEP 2

It builds your plan and targets

A training plan and calorie and macro targets, with the rationale for each target shown, plus your first check-in date.

STEP 3

You train and log

Log your sessions and, on Pro, your food. The local database on your device is the source of truth.

STEP 4

The weekly check-in decides

Change or hold, with the reasons written down. When the data is not enough yet, it holds rather than guesses.

Volyume Pro begins with 14 days of full access and no card required, so you can see the whole system before deciding. If you choose to continue, you opt into the subscription through Google Play, and Google Play's introductory offer adds a further 7 free days before your first payment is taken. The price Google Play shows at the point of purchase is the one that applies.

Deterministic coaching, not generative coaching

Volyume's weekly decision is rule-based and explainable. The same inputs give the same decision, every time, and the reason is written down so you can check it. It is not a chat assistant and it does not build a private model of you. It applies clear rules to your own numbers and tells you what it did.
