How the coach works

Once a week, Volyume looks at how your training and eating went and decides whether your plan should change. It tells you what it saw, what changes, what stays the same, and why.

Your coach isn’t an AI chatbot. It follows fixed rules. In the app, it is called Precision Coaching.

The weekly check-in

Once a week, on a day you choose, the check-in asks how your week was: energy, sleep, soreness, stress, joint pain, illness, travel, and anything else that affected it.

It fills in what it can from what you have logged, and you correct anything that does not match your week. When your logs already answer most of it, a shorter check-in asks you to confirm your energy and recovery, and that is all.

What your coach looks at

It reads all of these together. One unusual number on its own should not change your plan.

  • The sessions you completed, and how they felt.
  • How many hard sets each muscle got in the week.
  • Your calorie target, and your food diary if you use it.
  • Your morning weigh-ins, as one input among several.
  • Your answers to the weekly check-in.

What can happen each week

A week with no changes is your coach working, not asleep.

Something changes
Your calorie target or your training changes, and the reason sits beside it. For training, that means a few more or fewer hard sets for a muscle in the week, based on your energy, soreness and the sessions you completed.
Things stay as they are
When your progress is on course, your coach keeps your calorie target where it is. When your recovery is low, your training stays the same or eases back. The decision lists what stayed the same, and why.
It waits for more information
When there is not enough to go on, such as too few days of food logged, or the last change is too recent to show yet, your coach waits rather than guesses, and says so in plain words.
Changes wait long enough to show
Your calorie target does not change every week. It waits until there has been time to see what the last change did, so it is not reacting to normal day-to-day ups and downs.

You choose how much it does on its own

Switch between these in the app’s coaching settings whenever you like.

Collaborative
Your coach suggests each change, and you tap to apply it. This is how Volyume starts.
Manual
Your coach shows each change and the reason. You make the change yourself.
Coached
Your coach applies the week’s changes when you open the decision. Some changes always wait for you to confirm.

Every change comes with its reason

The coaching decision says what your coach saw, what changes in your calories and your training, what stays the same and why, and when it will look again. “How this decision was made” opens the detail behind it.

You choose how it words things. Supportive is plainer, with a little more explanation. Precise is shorter, with the numbers first. If you want the technical terms as well, Show the science adds them in brackets beside the plain ones.

Every past decision stays in your coaching history, including the weeks your coach kept things as they were.

Fixed rules, not an AI chatbot

Your coach follows fixed rules. Given the same information, it makes the same decision, and no language model is involved in it.

Two features do use recognition on your phone: reading a nutrition label when you scan one, and working out the Volyume Score from your photos. Both run on the phone itself.

What it cannot do

Your coach cannot see food you have not logged. It cannot know how a set felt unless you log it or say so at your check-in.

It cannot tell what is wrong if you are injured or ill, and it cannot measure how much of you is muscle or fat.
