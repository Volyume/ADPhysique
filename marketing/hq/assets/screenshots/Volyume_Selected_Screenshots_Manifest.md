## Crop applied

Each image was cropped from `(0, 60)` to `(709, 1448)`.

This removes the Android status bar and Android system-navigation bar while
preserving the complete Volyume interface and its own bottom navigation.

## Recommended replacement

The readiness image is the strongest current substitute for a coaching image.
Replace it later with a clean screenshot of a completed, valid weekly Precision
Coaching output showing what changed, what stayed the same and why.

## Excluded from this final eight

The remaining screenshots are retained as supporting material for onboarding
explainers, feature posts, help content and future composite assets. They were
not selected because the final store sequence needed less repetition and a
clearer balance across setup, daily use, training, nutrition and progress.

- Screens 01 to 07: free to use across site, social, articles and store

- Screen 08 (Progress Photos, shows a physique photo): store listing
