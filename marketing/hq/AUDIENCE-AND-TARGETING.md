# Audience and Targeting — Volyume Marketing HQ

*Governing document. British English. Calm, evidence-led, no hype.*
*Last updated: 2026-07-12.*

Volyume is the connected system for self-coached lifters. The customer
problem, stated in their words: **"I have the data, but I still have to
decide what to do next."** Everything below serves that one sentence.

The differentiator is **deterministic, explainable coaching** — the same
inputs always produce the same answer, and the app shows its reasoning. Not
generative AI, not a black box. Every segment hook ties back to this.

UK-first throughout: UK communities, UK terminology, and price anchoring in
pounds (**Pro £2.99/mo or £19.99/yr** — cheaper than most single competitor
apps, and one subscription instead of several).

---

## Priority order (build effort in this sequence)

1. **The self-coached lifter (core)** — the centre of gravity.
2. **The tool-juggler / consolidator** — nearest adjacent, easiest switch.
3. **The evidence-based / AI-sceptic lifter** — where our differentiator
   lands hardest.
4. **The privacy-conscious user** — a reinforcing overlay on all of the above.
5. **The physique competitor** — niche, high value, handled with care.

Segments 1–3 carry the growth. Segment 4 is a trust multiplier layered onto
the others. Segment 5 is a small high-value niche we serve honestly, never a
headline claim.

---

## 1. The self-coached lifter (core)

**Who they are.** Trains 3–6x/week, seriously. Tracks lifts *and* macros.
Already juggles a logger plus a nutrition app plus a spreadsheet or notes.
They are not looking to be motivated — they show up. They are looking to make
the right *next* decision with the data they already collect.

**Where they are (UK).** r/weightroom, r/naturalbodybuilding, r/fitness UK
threads, UK-heavy lifting Discords, gym-progress corners of Instagram and
TikTok, and searching Google for specific programming and nutrition questions
(the SEO surface). They read; they don't want a pep talk.

**The hook.** *The app makes the weekly decision — and shows you its
reasoning.* You bring the data; Volyume tells you what to change next week and
exactly why.

**Proof points to use.** Deterministic weekly coaching (same inputs, same
answer). Explainable output — the reasoning is shown, not hidden. Real free
training product (logging, plans, history, progress) so they can trust it
before paying. MEV/MRV volume landmarks and evidence-based nutrition maths
under the hood.

**What would repel them.** Hype, transformation promises, "AI coach" black-box
language, motivational fluff, anything that treats them like a beginner.

---

## 2. The tool-juggler / consolidator

**Who they are.** Pays for, or tolerates, two or three apps — a logger, a
macro tracker, maybe a separate planner — and stitches them together
manually. Feels the friction and the combined cost.

**Where they are (UK).** Same communities as the core, plus comparison and
"best app for…" searches, and threads complaining about subscription stacking.

**The hook.** *One connected system, one subscription — cheaper than most
single competitors.* Stop exporting between apps; the logging, nutrition and
coaching talk to each other.

**Proof points to use.** £2.99/mo or £19.99/yr for the connected whole vs
paying separately for a logger and a macro app. Connected data flow: what you
log feeds what you're coached to do. Free tier proves the training half before
they consolidate.

**What would repel them.** Any hint it's a shallow bundle rather than a
genuinely connected system; hidden costs; feeling nickel-and-dimed across
tiers.

---

## 3. The evidence-based / AI-sceptic lifter

**Who they are.** Reads studies, distrusts black-box AI coaching, has been
burned by apps that spit out confident nonsense. Wants to understand *why* a
recommendation was made before trusting it.

**Where they are (UK).** Evidence-based fitness communities, natural
bodybuilding spaces, comment sections pushing back on "AI coach" apps, and
searches for the reasoning behind programming/nutrition rules.

**The hook.** *Deterministic and explainable: same inputs, same answer, and
it shows its working.* No generative model deciding your training on a whim.

**Proof points to use.** The engine is deterministic pure logic — no LLM, no
randomness. The reasoning is surfaced in the app. Transparent, auditable
maths (BMR/TDEE/macros, volume landmarks). This is our strongest, most
defensible message — lead with it here.

**What would repel them.** Overclaiming, any "AI magic" framing, unsourced
assertions, marketing that talks down to them.

---

## 4. The privacy-conscious user (overlay segment)

**Who they are.** Cares where their health data lives and who profits from it.
Often overlaps with segments 1–3 rather than existing alone. A reason to
*choose us* and a reason to *stay*.

**Where they are (UK).** Privacy-minded tech and fitness communities, threads
about apps selling health data, users specifically avoiding ad-funded apps.

**The hook.** *Encrypted on your device, EU data residency, no ads, nothing
sold — ever.*

**Proof points to use.** SQLCipher-encrypted local storage (device is the
source of truth). EU-Dublin data residency, absolute. No advertising in the
product. No selling of user data. Health-data consent handled properly under
UK/EU rules.

**What would repel them.** Vague privacy language, any whiff of data
monetisation, tracking that contradicts the claim. Every privacy statement
must be literally true — this segment checks.

---

## 5. The physique competitor (niche, high value)

**Who they are.** Preps for shows, thinks in divisions, times contest phases.
Small in number, high in value and word-of-mouth influence.

**Where they are (UK).** UKBFF / PCA / federation communities, competitor
Instagram, prep-focused Discords and forums.

**The hook.** *Division-aware structure without a £150/mo human coach.*
Programming that understands divisions and contest timing.

**Proof points to use.** Division-specific programming structure. Contest-
phase awareness. Serious, structured tooling at £19.99/yr.

**What would repel them — and the hard rule.** **Never claim to replace a
coach.** Volyume is a *tool for the self-coached*, not a substitute for a human
coach who is choosing to work with one. Overstating this loses their trust
instantly and is off-positioning. Frame as structure for those *already*
self-coaching, never "fire your coach".

## Who we do NOT target

Stating this protects the brand and keeps us the right side of advertising
rules.

- **Beginners needing motivation or hand-holding.** Volyume assumes you
  already train and already track. Motivation marketing attracts users who
  churn and mis-sets expectations. Not our audience.
- **Under-18s. Never.** No targeting, no creative aimed at minors —
  advertising-rule requirement, non-negotiable.
- **Anyone seeking weight-loss promises or transformation marketing.** No
  before/after hooks, no "lose X in Y", no body-transformation persuasion.
  This is both a positioning choice and an ED-safety boundary — the product
  refuses to make those promises and so does the marketing.
- **Coaches / B2B.** Out of scope entirely. Volyume is for the individual
  self-coached lifter, not for coaches to run clients through.

When a campaign idea drifts toward any of these, it stops and goes back to the
founder rather than being softened through.

---

## Cross-segment guardrails

- **Every claim is literally true and, where possible, sourced.** The
  evidence-based audience is the whole market's quality bar.
- **No transformation, weight-loss-promise, or coach-replacement claims,
  ever** — positioning and safety both.
- **Pounds, UK communities, UK terminology** as the default frame until unit
  economics justify a new market.
- **Free tier is a proof point, not a giveaway to apologise for** — it lets
  every segment trust the training product before paying for the connected
  whole.
