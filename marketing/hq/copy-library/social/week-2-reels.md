# Week 2 Social Reels (product-first, cold-audience)

**What this is.** Two short-form pieces for a cold UK audience scrolling
TikTok / Reels / Shorts who have never heard of Volyume. Each piece is a ~18s
reel script of seven scenes, a matching carousel adaptation, a post caption, and
lowercase hashtags. Built on the proven Week 1 v3 spine: name a concrete problem
the viewer feels, name and explain Volyume by scene 2, show the change-or-hold
mechanism, make the reason the selling point, show the real app, show the one
verified HOLD card, then the CTA. Two fresh angles, not reused from Week 1.

**Channel:** faceless social (IG Reels / TikTok / YT Shorts), 9:16, ~18s, seven
scenes. Each is also a seven-slide 4:5 carousel using the same copy.

**Brand and visuals:** `MARKETING-VISUAL-IDENTITY-LOCKED.md`. Amber `#F5A623` on
near-black `#0D0D0D`, one accent. Display grotesk for words, IBM Plex Mono for
numbers. The one verified decision card is the HOLD card. Every concept carries a
real branded app screenshot via `appShot` (Addendum A2, demo-account data,
manifest usage rules). No bodies, no stock, no gradients.

**Voice:** British English, no em dashes, no exclamation marks, no jargon, no
insider compression, the say-it-out-loud test (CLAIMS-STANDARDS §9.10, §9.11).

**Status:** DRAFT. Not publish-ready until a compliance-reviewer PASS and a
cold-viewer clarity PASS are on record. Founder-tap lane.

## Gate notes (for the compliance-reviewer)

- Both pieces name Volyume and say what it is by scene 2. Scene 1 opens on a
  problem the viewer feels, in their own words.
- Say-it-out-loud: every line is a sentence a lifter would say to a mate. No
  "same data in same answer out", no "one flat number can be water", no engine
  terms before the plain explanation.
- The only decision card shown is the verified in-app HOLD output. No invented
  card content, verdict, number, or reason line.
- No claim about what other apps do. The two fresh angles are built on the
  viewer's own instinct (the Sunday guess; the over-adjuster), not on a
  competitor comparison.
- Every "Free to start on Google Play" is paired with "The weekly coaching is
  Pro." No trial-length or price number appears.
- British English, no em dashes, no exclamation marks, no hype adjectives, no
  banned §9 patterns.
