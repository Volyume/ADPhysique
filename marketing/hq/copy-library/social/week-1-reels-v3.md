# Week 1 Social Reels — v3 (product-first plain rewrite)

**Why v3 exists.** v1 assumed the viewer knew Volyume. v2 fixed the claims but
still opened on mood and withheld what the app is until scene 3, and it carried
an unverifiable competitor claim the founder caught ("most fitness apps push a
change every week" — false; most trackers make no decision at all). v3 is the
plain rewrite: **the first breath says what Volyume is and does**, in words a
person would say out loud. Every concept then shows the real app, shows the one
verified decision card, and ends with where to get it.

**Channel:** faceless social (IG Reels / TikTok / YT Shorts), 9:16, ~18s, six
scenes. Each is also a six-slide 4:5 carousel using the same copy.

**Brand and visuals:** `MARKETING-VISUAL-IDENTITY-LOCKED.md`. Amber `#F5A623`
on near-black `#0D0D0D`, one accent. Display grotesk for words, IBM Plex Mono
for numbers. The one verified decision card is the HOLD card (§5); the four
diagram components are founder-approved (Addendum A1); every concept carries a
real branded app screenshot via `appShot` (Addendum A2, demo-account data,
manifest usage rules). No bodies, no stock, no gradients (§7).

**Voice:** British English, no em dashes, no exclamation marks, no jargon, no
insider compression, the say-it-out-loud test (CLAIMS-STANDARDS §9.10, §9.11).

**Status:** DRAFT. Not publish-ready until a compliance-reviewer PASS and a
cold-viewer clarity PASS are on record. Founder-tap lane.

## Concept A — what it is

**Scene 1 (~3s).** Volyume is a workout and food-tracking app that tells you
what to change each week. [Claim 1]
**Scene 2 (~3s).** You log your training and your meals. Every week it reads
them and makes one call: change something, or hold. [Claim 1]
**Scene 3 (~3s).** And it shows you the reason behind the call, in plain
English. [Claim 1]
**Scene 4 (~3s, real app).** appShot `02_know_what_to_do_today.jpg`. Label:
"This is the app. The Today screen."
**Scene 5 (~3s, proof).** Some weeks the honest answer is hold, and it says so
instead of inventing a change. [Claim 3] miniCard: HOLD, "Need at least 3
morning weights for a reliable trend." [Claim 5]
**Scene 6 (~3s, CTA).** Volyume. / Free to start on Google Play. [Claim 11,
Claim 10] / The weekly coaching is Pro. [Claim 9]

**Caption.** Volyume is a workout and food-tracking app that does one thing the
trackers do not: it tells you what to change each week. You log your training
and meals, it reads them, and it makes the weekly call, change or hold, with the
reason shown. The weekly coaching is part of Pro. Free to start, on Google Play.
**Hashtags.** #selfcoached #hypertrophy #gymuk #traininglog #weeklycheckin

## Concept B — the flat scale

**Scene 1 (~3s).** You train hard for weeks and the scale will not move.
**Scene 2 (~3s).** Volyume is an app that tells you whether that really means
you have stalled, or whether to hold. [Claim 1, Claim 4]
**Scene 3 (~3s).** It reads your weekly trend, not one morning, because weight
swings with food, sleep and water. [Claim 4]
**Scene 4 (~3s, real app).** appShot `02_know_what_to_do_today.jpg`. Label:
"This is the app. Your morning weight, logged."
**Scene 5 (~3s, proof).** With fewer than three weigh-ins in a week it holds and
tells you why, instead of guessing. [Claim 5, Claim 3] miniCard: HOLD, "Need at
least 3 morning weights for a reliable trend." [Claim 5]
**Scene 6 (~3s, CTA).** Volyume. / Free to start on Google Play. [Claim 11,
Claim 10] / The weekly coaching is Pro. [Claim 9]

**Caption.** A flat scale after a few hard weeks is the moment most people reach
for the calorie calculator. Volyume reads your weekly trend, not one morning,
and needs at least three weigh-ins before it acts, so it will not have you
cutting on a week you did not actually stall. The weekly coaching is part of
Pro. Free to start, on Google Play.
**Hashtags.** #selfcoached #cuttingphase #macrotracking #gymuk #naturalbodybuilding

## Concept D — the honest hold

**Scene 1 (~3s).** When progress stalls, the urge is to change something
straight away.
**Scene 2 (~3s).** But change too much, too often, and you cannot tell what
actually worked.
**Scene 3 (~3s).** Volyume checks your trend first, and some weeks it tells you
to hold, because it will not invent a change the numbers do not support. [Claim
3, Claim 4]
**Scene 4 (~3s, real app).** appShot `03_plan_that_moves_with_you.jpg`. Label:
"This is the app. Your plan, changed only with a reason."
**Scene 5 (~3s, proof).** It holds and shows you why, in plain English. [Claim
1, Claim 3] miniCard: HOLD, "Need at least 3 morning weights for a reliable
trend." [Claim 5]
**Scene 6 (~3s, CTA).** Volyume. / Free to start on Google Play. [Claim 11,
Claim 10] / The weekly coaching is Pro. [Claim 9]

**Caption.** Plenty of training weeks are mostly noise, and changing things on a
noisy week makes real progress harder to read later. Volyume checks the trend
first and holds the call when the data does not back a change, and it tells you
why. The weekly coaching is part of Pro. Free to start, on Google Play.
**Hashtags.** #selfcoached #naturalbodybuilding #gymuk #trainingscience #progressiveoverload

## What changed from v2 (for the gate)

- Every concept now names Volyume and says what it is by scene 1 or 2, never
  scene 3. No mood-only opens.
- The false competitor claim ("most fitness apps push a change every week") is
  gone. Concept D is reframed on the viewer's own instinct and the app's honest
  hold behaviour; the only claim about other apps is the verified Claim 6 (they
  log and leave the decision to you).
- Plain-language sweep: no "deterministic", no "same data in same answer out",
  no "connected system", no "shows its working", no "one flat number can be
  water". Every line is written to be said out loud.
- Each concept shows the real app (appShot) and the one verified HOLD card.
- The reason behind the decision is the through-line, per the founder.
