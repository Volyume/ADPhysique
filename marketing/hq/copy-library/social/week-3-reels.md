# Week 3 Social Reels (product-first, cold-audience)

**What this is.** Two short-form pieces for a cold UK audience scrolling
TikTok / Reels / Shorts who have never heard of Volyume. Each piece is a ~18s
reel script of seven scenes, a matching carousel adaptation, a post caption, and
lowercase hashtags. Built on the proven Week 1 v3 / Week 2 spine: name a concrete
problem the viewer feels, name and explain Volyume by scene 2, show the
change-or-hold mechanism, make the reason the selling point, show the real app,
show the HOLD verdict (weight not foregrounded), then the CTA. Two fresh angles,
not reused from Week 1 or Week 2 (Week 2 was the Sunday-night guess and the
over-adjuster; those are not repeated here).

**Channel:** faceless social (IG Reels / TikTok / YT Shorts), 9:16, ~18s, seven
scenes. Each is also a seven-slide 4:5 carousel using the same copy.

**Brand and visuals:** `MARKETING-VISUAL-IDENTITY-LOCKED.md`. Amber `#F5A623` on
near-black `#0D0D0D`, one accent. Display grotesk for words, IBM Plex Mono for
numbers. The decision card shows the HOLD verdict only (no reason string; weight
not foregrounded, ED-safety). Every concept carries a real branded app screenshot
via `appShot` (Addendum A2, demo-account data, manifest usage rules). No bodies,
no stock, no gradients.

**Voice:** British English, no em dashes, no exclamation marks, no jargon, no
insider compression, the say-it-out-loud test (CLAIMS-STANDARDS §9.10, §9.11).

**Status:** DRAFT. Not publish-ready until a compliance-reviewer PASS, a
cold-viewer clarity PASS, and a principles PASS are on record. Founder-tap lane.

## Gate notes (for the compliance-reviewer)

- Both pieces name Volyume and say what it is by scene 2. Scene 1 opens on a
  problem the viewer feels, in their own words (the conflicting online advice;
  the static plan that never reacts). Neither is reused from Week 1 or Week 2.
- Claim-mechanism consistency (AD-STRUCTURE rule 9): both call Volyume "a workout
  and food-tracking app", and the scene 3 mechanism in both names reading your
  training AND your food. Self-description and shown mechanism match.
- Free/Pro accuracy (rule 10): the only free action shown is training logging
  (Piece 1 scene 5 "You log your sets here"). Food and the weekly decision are
  attributed to the Pro coaching. No line implies food logging or weight logging
  is free. Every "Free to start on Google Play" is paired with "The weekly
  coaching, with its food diary, is Pro."
- ED-safety (§5): weight is not foregrounded anywhere. No scale hook, no
  weigh-in count as a proof beat, no weight-trend card. The appShot chosen for
  each piece avoids the morning-weight screen (`02` not used). The only decision
  card shown is the verified in-app HOLD output, verdict only, no reason string,
  no number.
- Say-it-out-loud (§9.10, §9.11): every line is a sentence a lifter would say to
  a mate. No engine terms, no "same data in same answer out", no insider
  compression, no feature name before the plain explanation.
- No claim about what other apps do. Both angles are built on the viewer's own
  instinct, not a competitor comparison.
- British English, no em dashes, no exclamation marks, no hype adjectives, no
  banned §9 patterns. No trial-length or price number appears.
