# Week 1 Social Reels — v2 (cold-audience rewrite)

**Why v2 exists.** The v1 batch (`week-1-carousels.md`) was rejected by the
founder for assuming the viewer already knew Volyume. These concepts are
written for a COLD viewer who has never heard of the app. Scene 1 is always a
problem the viewer recognises, with no product name. Volyume is named and
explained in plain words by scene 3 at the latest.

**Channel:** Faceless social (IG Reels / TikTok / YT Shorts), 9:16 vertical,
~21 seconds, six scenes each. Each concept is also specified as a six-slide
4:5 carousel using the same copy, slide 1 = hook, final slide = CTA card.

**Brand and visuals:** all assets follow `MARKETING-VISUAL-IDENTITY-LOCKED.md`.
Amber `#F5A623` accent on near-black `#0D0D0D`, one accent only. Two typefaces:
display grotesk for headings and sentences, monospace (IBM Plex Mono) for every
number and stat. Left-aligned, asymmetric, one idea per scene. Every proof beat
uses a component from the approved renderer set: the decision card (§5 of the
visual identity doc) plus the four diagram components (weekly trend line, fork,
lone dot, filling dots) founder-approved as production standard with carousel 1
on 2026-07-12 (ruling recorded in the identity doc addendum). No bodies, no
stock, no before/after, no gradients (§7 of the visual identity doc, hard stop).

**Voice check:** British English, no em dashes, no exclamation marks, no hype
adjectives, no negation-pivots, no decorative triads, no outcome promises. See
the self-check notes at the foot of this file.

**Status:** DRAFT. Not publish-ready until a compliance-reviewer PASS is on
record (CLAIMS-STANDARDS §10). This batch is founder-tap.

  is §5 of the visual identity doc; the four diagrams are founder-approved

  one-line promise + "Free to start, on Google Play." No trial length is stated
  anywhere; §3 is not engaged.
- **Negation-pivot pattern (§9.3):** used zero times. Scene sub-lines such as
  "Not busywork, a decision" (D5) and "not only when to push" (D6) are
  contrastive on concrete content, not the empty "more than a tracker" /
  "it's not X, it's Y" shape. Flagged for the reviewer to confirm.
- **Triads (§9.4):** the three-item lines (C1 "training log, food app, notes";
  B2 "water, sleep or a heavy meal") carry distinct, load-bearing content, not
  rhythm. No decorative triad anywhere.
- **Question openers (§9.8):** Concept A Scene 2 is a question, but it is not
  answered by the product on the next beat; Scene 3 introduces the product a
  beat later as the resolution of the whole hook, not as an immediate
  question-answer pair. No "Tired of X? Volyume does Y." construction is used.
- **No hype adjectives, no scene-setting openers, no exclamation marks, no em
  dashes, no emoji in body copy.** No emoji used at all in this batch.
- **British English throughout.**
- **No outcome or timeline promises, no before/after, no shame framing, no
  guaranteed check-in count, no medical or body-fat claim, no "replaces a
  coach", barcode never called free.** Precision Coaching is stated as Pro in
  Concept C (claim 9). "Free to start" refers only to the real free tier
