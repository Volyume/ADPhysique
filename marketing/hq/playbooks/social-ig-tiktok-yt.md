# Playbook: Faceless Social — Instagram, TikTok, YouTube

**Owner agents:** content-writer (opus, scripts/copy), creative-designer
(sonnet, Canva visuals), compliance-reviewer (opus, gate), growth-analyst
(sonnet, measurement). Publishing lane per OPERATING-CHARTER §4.

---

## Purpose

Give Volyume a visual, scroll-native presence that explains the product's
actual value (deciding what to do next, explainably) without requiring the
founder's face, voice, or time — consistent with the ~15-minutes/week
founder-time ceiling (OPERATING-CHARTER §1).

## How this channel works for Volyume specifically

- No founder face or voice is ever required for this channel. All formats
  are text-forward or narrated by design/motion, not a talking head.
- Brand: amber **#F5A623** on near-black **#0D0D0D**, applied consistently
  across all Canva assets so the account is recognisable before a viewer
  reads a word.
- Content teaches the "what do I do next" decision problem directly —
  that is Volyume's actual differentiator and translates well to short,
  concrete, visual explainers.
- CLAIMS-STANDARDS §5 bans before/after transformation advertising outright
  — this is doubly binding on this channel because transformation imagery is
  the default format for fitness social content elsewhere. Volyume's visual
  identity here deliberately does not do what the category normally does.

## What the agents do, step by step

1. **content-writer scripts/copy** each piece against one of the approved
   formats:
   - Text-forward carousels and short explainer clips.
   - Decision-explainers (e.g. "when should you actually change your
     calories?").
   - Myth-calm-downs (correcting a common fitness myth, calmly, evidence-led).
   - UK lifting content (culturally specific references, UK food/gym
     context).
2. **compliance-reviewer gates** the script/copy exactly as any other public
   artefact — Claim Rule, prohibited list (no before/after, no guaranteed
   outcomes, no invented stats), qualified-claims framing, tone.
3. **creative-designer builds the visual asset in Canva** on brand
   (amber/near-black), only from a PASSed script — design never precedes
   the compliance gate, since a visual built around a failed claim is
   wasted work and a temptation to publish it anyway.
4. **Founder-tap lane until proven, then autonomous per channel:** the
   first batch of content for a given platform (e.g. the first Instagram
   batch) is FOUNDER-TAP — the founder approves that batch before anything
   posts. Once that first batch is approved **and** that platform's API is
   connected via the scheduler, subsequent scheduled posts on that platform
   move to the AUTONOMOUS lane (OPERATING-CHARTER §4). Each platform earns
   its own autonomy independently — Instagram going autonomous does not
   make TikTok or YouTube autonomous.
5. **growth-analyst logs** every publish and reads performance, treating
   follower growth as context, never the KPI (see Measurement).

## Visual identity

`marketing/hq/MARKETING-VISUAL-IDENTITY-LOCKED.md` is binding for every
piece of social imagery and video on this channel, in full — not just the
amber/near-black summary above. Two recurring approaches carry most of this
channel's output:

- **The decision-card format** (locked spec §5) — a real number, a
  change/hold verdict pill, one plain-English reason line, built from the
  Volyume Canva brand kit, not a stock template. This is Volyume's own
  recurring visual unit and the one no competitor can replicate, because it
  comes from the product's real weekly coaching decision.
- **Category contrast** (locked spec §1) — this channel's competitors are
  bright, hyped, and photo/transformation-led; Volyume's feed stays calm,
  dark and editorial deliberately, as the strategic differentiator, not a
  style preference.

## Cadence

Consistency over volume. Working target once accounts exist and are
approved: 3–4 posts per week per platform. This is deliberately modest —
a thin, sporadic account looks worse than a small consistent one, and
quality/compliance review time is the real constraint, not ideas.

## Success / failure — measurable

- **Success:** store-listing taps or installs attributable to social
  referral (where platform attribution allows); consistent posting
  cadence maintained without compliance FAILs; content that performs (saves,
  shares, watch-through) without relying on transformation or hype framing.
- **Failure:** any post reaching publish without a compliance PASS; any
  before/after or transformation-framed content anywhere in the pipeline
  (this is a hard stop, not a metric); cadence collapsing to sporadic
  posting; chasing follower count as if it were the KPI instead of reading
  it as context (OPERATING-CHARTER §6 — installs/trials/retention are the
  ladder, not likes or followers).

## Founder actions required

- Create the accounts (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube) — agents never create
  accounts (OPERATING-CHARTER §5 and §4, FOUNDER-ONLY).
- Start Meta and YouTube developer/API app approvals early — these take
  weeks, so this is a founder to-do that should begin well before the first
  content batch is ready, not after.
- Approve each platform's first content batch (founder-tap).
- Approve/connect each platform's API to the scheduler once available.

## Hard boundaries

- Never before/after imagery or transformation framing, in any format, on
  any platform, ever (CLAIMS-STANDARDS §5).
- No founder face or voice requirement — if a format needs one, it's the
  wrong format for this channel.
- No account created, or API connected, without the founder doing it
  personally.
- No platform moves to the autonomous lane until both conditions are met
  (first batch approved AND API connected) — meeting only one is not
  enough.
- No claim on any asset that isn't traceable to PRODUCT-FACTS; no
  "best/most accurate" superiority claims (CLAIMS-STANDARDS §5); no
  "learns you" / AI-coach framing (CLAIMS-STANDARDS §6).
- Follower growth is never reported as the primary success metric in any
  digest or ledger entry.
