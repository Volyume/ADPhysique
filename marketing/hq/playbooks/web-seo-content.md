# Playbook: Web / SEO Content — volyume.app

**Owner agent:** content-writer (opus) drafts; compliance-reviewer (opus)
gates; growth-analyst (sonnet) reads performance. Publishing per
OPERATING-CHARTER §4 autonomous lane.

---

## Purpose

Build a small, genuinely useful body of content on volyume.app that compounds
in search over time (OPERATING-CHARTER §1 mission: "compounding beats
spikes"), and that earns organic links and trust because it is actually
useful standalone — not because it is optimised to rank. This is the
lowest-risk, most durable channel in the plan.

## How this channel works for Volyume specifically

- Google penalises **scaled, manipulative, low-value content** — including
  thin AI-generated content produced at volume to game rankings — not
  AI-assisted writing itself. The verified research finding governs this
  playbook: quality, usefulness, and evidence, not authorship method, is
  what protects rankings. Cite the verified research report in `reports/`
  internally; this justifies content-writer (an AI agent) producing the
  articles, provided each one clears the bar below.
- Volyume's actual differentiator — deterministic, explainable coaching
  answering "what do I do next" — gives genuinely distinct pillar topics
  that generic fitness content sites don't cover well. That distinctiveness
  is the content strategy, not keyword volume.
- Every factual claim in an article must trace to PRODUCT-FACTS
  (CLAIMS-STANDARDS §2). Where an article makes a general training/nutrition
  claim not specific to Volyume (e.g. an evidence-based statement about
  progressive overload), it must be genuinely evidence-cited to a real
  source — not attributed to Volyume's engine unless it is.

## What the agents do, step by step

1. **content-writer selects a topic** from the pillar list (below), checks
   it hasn't been covered in the last 90 days, and drafts a standalone
   article: useful on its own even to a reader who never installs the app.
2. **Evidence citation.** Any general fitness/nutrition claim carries a
   real, checkable source. Any Volyume-specific claim traces to
   PRODUCT-FACTS by section.
3. **One contextual link to the app**, placed where it's earned (e.g. "if
   you'd rather this decision were made for you automatically, that's what
   Volyume's weekly check-in does") — never a hard sell, never more than one
   link, no keyword-stuffed anchor text.
4. **Tone check.** Calm, plain, no hype, no exclamation marks, no em dashes,
   British English (CLAIMS-STANDARDS §8).
5. **compliance-reviewer gates** the draft against CLAIMS-STANDARDS in full
   (Claim Rule, prohibited list, qualified-claims framing for
   "personalised"/"coach"/"deterministic", ASA/CAP basics) and records a
   PASS or FAIL with cited reasons.
6. **On PASS, the article publishes autonomously** (OPERATING-CHARTER §4 —
   volyume.app articles that have passed the compliance gate are the
   canonical autonomous-lane example). On FAIL, content-writer corrects and
   resubmits; it does not go around the gate.
7. **growth-analyst** logs the artefact and PASS record to the growth
   ledger and tracks its performance once Search Console is connected.

## Pillar topics

- Training volume and programming decisions — how to know when to change.
- Macro and nutrition decision-making.
- Weekly check-in methodology.
- UK-specific lifting nutrition (the UK food-data angle — Volyume's bundled
  UK database, PRODUCT-FACTS §D, is a genuine, defensible edge here).
- Explainable vs black-box coaching (the deterministic-engine story, framed
  per CLAIMS-STANDARDS §6: "deterministic coaching, not generative
  coaching," never a bare "no AI" slogan).

## Cadence

1–2 articles per week, maximum. This is a ceiling, not a target — a week
with zero publishes because nothing cleared the quality bar is a better
outcome than a week with two thin ones. No batch publishing to hit a
quota.

## Success / failure — measurable

- **Success:** search impressions and clicks trending upward once Search
  Console is connected (founder grant); waitlist or install referrals
  attributable to specific articles; zero compliance FAILs reaching publish;
  articles earning organic backlinks or shares without promotion.
- **Failure:** any article publishing without a PASS record (this is a hard
  stop, not a metric — OPERATING-CHARTER §3 makes the gate blocking);
  cadence exceeded (more than 2/week); an article that reads as thin,
  generic, or interchangeable with any other fitness site; declining search
  performance after a content batch (signal to re-check quality, not to
  publish more).

## Founder actions required

- Grant Google Search Console access.
- Approve the initial pillar list (this playbook proposes it; founder may
  revise).
- Spot-check published articles periodically against CLAIMS-STANDARDS as a
  sanity check on the gate, not as a required per-article approval (this
  lane is autonomous by design).

## Hard boundaries

- No programmatic or templated content sprawl (OPERATING-CHARTER §7:
  "never programmatic sprawl, never thin content farms").
- No article publishes without a logged compliance PASS — no exceptions,
  including under cadence pressure.
- No more than one contextual app link per article; never a landing-page
  style hard sell.
- No unverified numbers, no invented statistics, no claim not traceable to
  PRODUCT-FACTS or a real external source.
- No before/after or transformation framing anywhere in article content or
  imagery (CLAIMS-STANDARDS §5).
- No claim that Progress Scan measures body fat or is medical, ever
  (PRODUCT-FACTS §A, CLAIMS-STANDARDS §5).
