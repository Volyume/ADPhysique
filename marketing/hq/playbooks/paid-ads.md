## Playbook: Paid Ads and Creator Partnerships

**Owner agents (once unlocked):** marketing-director (opus, orchestration),
content-writer (opus), compliance-reviewer (opus), growth-analyst (sonnet,
CAC/LTV measurement). Spend and account creation remain FOUNDER-ONLY forever
regardless of unlock state (OPERATING-CHARTER §4, §5).

---

## Purpose

Paid acquisition is the only channel in this plan that costs money and can
scale spend badly before quality is proven. OPERATING-CHARTER §1 and §5 are
explicit: "Retention proof comes before spend," and paid advertising "does
not open until both" conditions below hold. This playbook exists to be
ready the day it's unlocked, not to be used before then.

## Unlock conditions (both required)

1. **Retention proven well above the category floor.** Working reference:
   D30 retention cohorts sitting well above the ~3% category floor cited in
   OPERATING-CHARTER §5. "Well above," not "at," the floor.
2. **Revenue threshold.** Roughly **£3,000–5,000 MRR**, working figure per
   OPERATING-CHARTER §5. The founder may revise this figure.

Both conditions, not either. Unlocking is a founder decision even once both
are numerically met — meeting the thresholds is necessary, not sufficient.

## How this channel will work for Volyume, once unlocked

The verified two-pattern approach:

- **(a) Retained micro-creator programme** — the verified Cal AI pattern:
  many small UK fitness creators on modest retainers producing native
  content, rather than one large sponsorship. Agent-drafted outreach;
  every individual creator engagement and every payment is founder-approved
  (account creation and spending are FOUNDER-ONLY absolutely,
  OPERATING-CHARTER §4).
- **(b) Paid social tests** — small, controlled budgets, strict CAC-vs-LTV
  measurement from day one, kill fast on any test that doesn't clear the
  bar. No open-ended always-on spend.

## What the agents will do, step by step (once unlocked)

1. marketing-director proposes a bounded test plan (channel, budget cap,
   creative, success threshold) as a founder decision before any spend.
2. content-writer drafts creative against PRODUCT-FACTS and
   CLAIMS-STANDARDS exactly as any other channel.
3. compliance-reviewer gates every piece of paid creative, with two
   additional non-negotiable checks specific to ads: **#ad** labelling
   present and prominent (CLAIMS-STANDARDS §7), and no under-18 targeting or
   under-18-reading creative anywhere in the plan (CLAIMS-STANDARDS §7).
4. Founder approves spend and the specific creator/platform account before
   anything goes live (FOUNDER-ONLY, no exception, no delegation).
5. growth-analyst tracks CAC against LTV per test; anything not clearing
   the pre-agreed threshold is killed, not extended "to see."
6. Micro-creator engagements: agent drafts outreach messages; founder
   approves every individual creator relationship and every payment
   individually — this is not a batch approval.

## Cadence

Not applicable while locked. Once unlocked: test cadence set per the
founder-approved test plan, never an ongoing always-on budget without a
kill-review point.

## Success / failure — measurable (once unlocked)

- **Success:** CAC held meaningfully below LTV on a live test; retention
  cohorts hold steady or improve under paid-driven users (paid traffic must
  not dilute retention quality); #ad compliance and under-18 exclusion
  verified on every piece of paid creative with zero incidents.
- **Failure:** any test running without a pre-agreed kill threshold; any ad
  live without visible #ad labelling; any targeting or creative reading as
  aimed at under-18s; CAC exceeding LTV without an immediate kill; spend
  occurring without founder sign-off (this is not a metric — it is a hard
  stop and a serious incident).

## Founder actions required

- Decide when both unlock conditions are genuinely met (this is a founder
  judgement call, not an automatic trigger even once the numbers clear).
- Approve every test plan, every budget, every creator relationship, and
  every payment individually.
- Create every ad account and every creator-payment mechanism personally.

## Hard boundaries

- **This channel does not activate until the founder explicitly says so,
  even if both numeric thresholds are met.**
- No agent creates an ad account, a payment method, or spends money, ever,
  under any condition (OPERATING-CHARTER §4, absolute).
- **#ad** on every piece of paid or creator content, always, no exception.
- No under-18 targeting, no under-18 talent, no creative reading as aimed at
  minors, ever.
- No weight-loss-promise creative, ever — this sits on top of the
  general CLAIMS-STANDARDS §5 ban on guaranteed outcomes and before/after
  transformation advertising, which apply here exactly as everywhere else.
- No test scales past its agreed budget cap without a fresh founder
  approval.
- If any founder-only condition above is unclear or contested, the correct
  action is to stop and ask — never to interpret ambiguity in favour of
  proceeding with spend.
