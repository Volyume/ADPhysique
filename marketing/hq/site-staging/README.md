must not be deployed or copied over `public/`. The live pages are in `public/`
(mirrored in `docs/web/`), and they are the only source of truth for what the
website says. Nothing in a workflow reads this folder.

It is kept only because `marketing/hq/playbooks/retention-email-loop.md` still
names `survey/index.html` here. Some of its styles are left over from an older
pricing page; Volyume has no tiers, prices or trial.
