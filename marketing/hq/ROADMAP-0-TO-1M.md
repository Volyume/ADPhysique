# Roadmap: 0 to 1M — Volyume Marketing HQ

*Governing document. British English. Calm, evidence-led, no hype.*
*Last updated: 2026-07-12.*

This roadmap governs how the Volyume AI marketing department grows the app
from zero users toward one million and beyond. It is staged deliberately:
each stage has entry criteria, measurable exit criteria, a channel focus,
what the agents do differently, the founder's involvement, and the failure
modes that could stall progress with the agreed response. No stage is
skipped. No stage is declared complete on vibes — the exit criteria are
numbers, and a stage that has not met them is not finished.

---

## Honesty box — the base rates

Read this before believing any projection in this file.

- **Most fitness apps never reach 100k users.** Reaching 1M is rare. This
  roadmap does not assume we will; it earns each stage or records in the
  ledger why we did not.
- **Hevy, our closest verified precedent, took roughly 3.5–4 years to reach
  1M users** on under $15k of lifetime ad spend — then went 1M→2M in about
  five months. The growth was back-loaded: years of slow compounding, then a
  sharp inflection. We should expect the same shape and not panic in the
  slow years or over-celebrate early.
- **The growth engine that worked for Hevy was an in-product social/viral
  loop plus word of mouth plus store-discovery algorithms — not paid
  acquisition.** Their founder says paid UA "never cracked" and early ASO was
  basic. Marketing's highest-leverage lever (share cards, partner invites) is
  product-side, outside this department's build scope; where the evidence
  supports it, we recommend it to the founder rather than build it ourselves.
- **No verified case study exists of a fully autonomous AI marketing
  department.** This build is pioneering. Early weeks run under close founder
  review; autonomy widens only as the system proves it can run a clean cycle.
- **Never judge a channel dead in year one.** SEO and social compound over
  quarters. A channel is retired only against evidence recorded in the
  ledger, never against impatience.

Everything below is a plan, not a promise.

## Stage 0 — Foundation (now)

Stand up the machine before pointing it at anyone.

**Entry criteria**
- Marketing HQ agent system live and able to run.
- App live on Google Play (`app.volyume`); zero users; no prior marketing.

**What the agents do differently here**
- Build, not broadcast. Landing page live on volyume.app; waitlist capture
  wired; measurement wired end to end (install attribution where lawful,
  store analytics, rating/review monitoring, content analytics).
- First content batch published — bounded, differentiated, evidence-cited
  articles. No programmatic sprawl (Google penalises scaled/manipulative
  content, not AI-assisted content per se).
- Every automated surface is dry-run reviewed by the founder before it can
  post autonomously.

**Founder involvement:** high. Founder reviews the landing page, the first
content batch, and the first full weekly-cycle output before autonomy widens.

**Exit criteria (measurable)**
- The system completes **one full autonomous weekly cycle cleanly**: plans,
  drafts, measures, and reports without a founder having to correct a
  mechanical failure. Content quality is still founder-reviewed; the exit
  bar is that the *machine* runs.

**What could kill progress + response**
- *Measurement not actually wired* (we grow blind). Response: no stage exit
  until a test event is confirmed end to end in analytics.
- *Content trips spam heuristics.* Response: keep volume low and evidence
  density high; every article cites sources; no templated mass-generation.

## Stage 1 — First hundreds

Turn on discovery. Protect the rating from install one.

**Entry criteria:** Stage 0 exit met.

**Channel focus:** Play Store / ASO; review replies; first SEO articles;
social accounts seeded.

**What the agents do differently**
- **ASO foundations:** iterate title and description *within the frozen
  screenshot set* (screenshots are not changed here). Test metadata, not
  creative.
- **Review replies from the first install**, and **rating protection as
  Tier-1 infrastructure.** Rationale from research: 4.0+ is effectively a
  store-visibility gate — apps under ~3.5 barely rank, and 85–90% of featured
  apps are 4.0+. A cluster of early 1-stars can sink discovery before it
  starts, so every review gets a calm, prompt, British-English reply.
- Seed IG / TikTok / YouTube (faceless, Canva-designed) with a **consistent
  cadence** — cadence over intensity.
- First SEO articles published against real self-coached-lifter queries.

**Founder involvement:** medium-high. Founder posts any community drafts
personally (founder-posted only). Reviews social cadence and the first
review-reply tone. Approves ASO metadata changes.

**Exit criteria (measurable)**
- **~500 installs.**
- **Rating ≥ 4.5** with early reviewers.
- **Waitlist growing week on week.**

**What could kill progress + response**
- *Early rating dips below the visibility gate.* Response: pause acquisition
  push; fix the driver (surface bugs to founder), reply to every review,
  invite satisfied users to rate. Rating is protected ahead of growth.
- *Social cadence slips* (algorithms punish inconsistency). Response: reduce
  scope to a cadence we can hold every week rather than a burst we cannot.

## Stage 2 — First thousands

Let content and cadence compound. Open the community lane properly.

**Entry criteria:** Stage 1 exit met.

**Channel focus:** SEO compounding; social via scheduler API; UK community
presence; waitlist activation.

**What the agents do differently**
- **Content compounding:** SEO articles begin ranking; social cadence runs
  through the scheduler API once platform APIs are approved (auto-posting
  only after approval — until then, founder-posted).
- **Community presence:** founder-posted drafts into UK lifting communities
  under a strict **90/10 value rule** — nine genuinely useful contributions
  for every one that mentions the product. Communities detect and reject
  marketing; we earn attention.
- **Waitlist activation** once the EU-residency-compliant sender is wired —
  no email send before that.

**Founder involvement:** medium. Founder still posts community drafts and
approves the activation email programme. Day-to-day content runs
semi-autonomously with weekly review.

**Exit criteria (measurable)**
- **~5,000 installs.**
- **D30 cohort measurable and above the category floor (~3%
  install-weighted).** Retention cohorts now lead installs as the headline
  KPI.
- **First organic keyword rankings** established.

**What could kill progress + response**
- *Installs grow but retention is at or below floor.* Response: do not scale
  acquisition. Flag to founder that the product's activation/retention needs
  work before more traffic; marketing holds spend and effort steady.
- *Community backlash / perceived astroturfing.* Response: tighten the 90/10
  rule, pause the lane, and let the founder re-establish trust personally.

## Stage 3 — Retention proof

Stop counting installs. Prove people stay.

**Entry criteria:** Stage 2 exit met.

**Channel focus:** unchanged organic mix, but the KPI lens shifts hard.

**What the agents do differently**
- KPI focus shifts to **D1 / D7 / D30 cohorts and trial→paid conversion**
  (14-day cardless trial → Play subscription with 7-day intro → first charge
  ~day 21; watch the day-21 charge cliff specifically).
- **Recommend in-product loop improvements to the founder with evidence** —
  share cards, partner invites — since research names in-product loops as the
  single highest-leverage growth surface. These are product-side; marketing
  recommends, the founder decides and the product team builds.
- **Store-listing experiments** (including screenshots) once traffic
  justifies statistically meaningful tests.

**Founder involvement:** medium, decision-heavy. Founder rules on in-product
loop recommendations and any listing experiments.

**Exit criteria (measurable)**
- **D30 well above the 3% floor, sustained** across cohorts (not a single
  lucky month).
- **MRR approaching £3–5k.**

**What could kill progress + response**
- *Trial→paid conversion is weak at the day-21 charge.* Response: surface to
  founder as a pricing/onboarding/value-communication question; marketing
  sharpens the value story but does not touch billing (founder-gated).
- *Retention will not clear the floor.* Response: the roadmap stalls here on
  purpose. We do not unlock paid spend on a leaky product. Record in the
  ledger; keep organic steady; support product fixes with evidence.

## Stage 4 — Paid unlock

Only now does money enter acquisition — and only in controlled tests.

**Entry criteria:** Stage 3 exit met — **retention proof + roughly £3–5k
MRR.** This gate is firm. Paid ads are locked until it is cleared, because
paid spend before retention proof burns money (Cal AI went paid only after
organic plateaued around $2M/mo; Hevy never cracked paid at all).

**Channel focus:** paid ads (small, controlled) + a retained-creator
programme.

**What the agents do differently**
- **Paid ads playbook activates with small controlled tests first** —
  measure LTV:CAC per channel before any scaling. Every spend decision is
  founder-approved.
- **Creator-programme playbook** on the verified Cal AI pattern: retained
  micro-creators producing native content, scaled via agent-drafted outreach
  the **founder approves** before it sends. No spammy blast; relationships.

**Founder involvement:** high on spend. Every budget and every creator
contract is a founder decision. Marketing drafts and measures; the founder
authorises money.

**Exit criteria (measurable)**
- **Paid channels demonstrating LTV > CAC at scale** (not just in a lucky
  test cell).

**What could kill progress + response**
- *CAC exceeds LTV once tests scale.* Response: cut the channel, return to
  organic-led growth, record the result. A channel that loses money at scale
  is paused, not subsidised.
- *Creator content misrepresents the product* (especially any coach-
  replacement or transformation claim). Response: hard stop — approval gate
  catches it before publish; brief creators explicitly on the banned claims.

## Stage 5 — Compounding to 1M

The flywheel does the work; we keep it turning and widen the map.

**Entry criteria:** Stage 4 exit met.

**Channel focus:** multi-channel flywheel (organic + paid + creator +
in-product loops), iOS App Store fully live, measured international
expansion.

**What the agents do differently**
- Run all proven channels together, reallocating toward whatever compounds.
- **iOS App Store fully live** — full ASO and review infrastructure mirrored
  from Android.
- **International expansion beyond the UK only when unit economics prove** in
  a new market; the UK-first positioning and pound anchoring stay until then.
- Expect the **Hevy shape**: the final stretch to 1M can be *faster* than the
  middle as word of mouth and store algorithms compound. Do not under-resource
  the inflection.

**Founder involvement:** strategic. Founder approves market expansion and
major budget shifts; the machine runs the rest.

**Exit criteria (measurable):** toward 1M+ installs with a healthy retained,
paying base and channels that pay for themselves.

**What could kill progress + response**
- *Over-extending internationally before economics prove.* Response: one
  market at a time, each earning its place against the same retention +
  LTV:CAC bar.
- *Complacency at the inflection.* Response: treat the acceleration as the
  moment to invest, not coast; keep rating protection and review replies as
  Tier-1 throughout — they never stop being infrastructure.

## Standing rules across every stage

- **Retention cohorts rank above installs** as the primary KPI from Stage 2
  on. Vanity install counts never justify a stage exit on their own.
- **Rating protection and review replies are Tier-1 from the first install
  to the millionth.** They are infrastructure, not a campaign.
- **The ledger is the memory.** Every channel judgement — kept, paused,
  retired — is recorded with evidence and date. Nothing is declared dead
  without it.
- **No paid spend before Stage 4**, and none within it without founder
  approval.
- **Everything the founder must post, the founder posts.** Community drafts
  are agent-written, founder-published.
