# Volyume Advertising Principles (evidence-graded rulebook)

**What this is.** The proven, sourced foundations the marketing agents write
from and are graded against, so an advert is judged on established principle,
not taste or guesswork. Built 2026-07-12 from a 105-agent deep-research pass
that adversarially fact-checked every claim (three independent verifier votes
each; anything that could not survive was cut). Each rule carries its evidence
grade and source. Where the evidence contradicts popular copywriting folklore,
that is stated plainly, because the contradictions are the most useful part.

**How to read the grades.**
- **LAW (high):** empirically grounded, replicated, or legally binding. Follow
  it.
- **CANON (high, not RCT-tested):** decades-proven direct-response doctrine,
  universally corroborated. Apply it.
- **PRACTITIONER (medium):** strong professional consensus, no primary
  documentation (the platforms do not publish their algorithms). Apply, but
  re-verify before a campaign.
- **DO NOT USE:** failed verification. Listed at the foot so no agent
  reintroduces it.

## Layer 1 — How brands actually grow (Ehrenberg-Bass / Byron Sharp)

The most data-grounded body here, and it reframes the whole job. It is about
brand growth, and it partly contradicts the message-level copy craft in Layer 2
(both are true at different levels; see the reconciliation note).

1. **Grow by penetration, not loyalty. [LAW]** Brands grow mainly by acquiring
   more buyers through broad reach, not by extracting more from existing ones.
   IPA analysis of ~880 cases: ~82% of growth came from penetration, ~2% from
   loyalty. → For Volyume: reach as many self-coached lifters as possible;
   do not over-target a narrow niche.
   *Caveat (honest):* Volyume's Pro tier is a subscription, so retention still
   drives revenue even if acquisition drives growth. The penetration evidence
   is FMCG-derived and does not map perfectly to subscription apps. Balance is
   an open question (see below), not settled.
   Source: marketingscience.info; brandgenetics.com (How Brands Grow).

2. **Reach broadly and stay always-on, not targeted bursts. [LAW]** The two
   proven growth levers are wide reach and continuous presence. → A steady
   weekly cadence beats occasional big pushes. Consistency is the strategy.
   Source: Byron Sharp, marketingscience.info / Mi3.

3. **Win on mental and physical availability, not persuasion. [LAW]** People
   buy what is easy to think of at the moment of need and easy to get. Mental
   availability is built by distinctive brand assets acting as retrieval cues.
   → Being remembered and easy to find matters more than a clever argument.
   Source: Ehrenberg-Bass; Building Distinctive Brand Assets (Romaniuk, OUP 2018).

4. **Meaningless distinctiveness beats meaningful differentiation. [LAW,
   contested at edges]** Sharp, verbatim: "Rather than striving for meaningful,
   perceived differentiation, marketers should seek meaningless distinctiveness."
   Consistent sensory assets (a colour, a logo, a signature device) do more work
   than a unique-selling-point argument. → **Volyume already has these: the
   amber #F5A623, the wordmark, the monospace numerals. The evidence says these
   are among the highest-leverage assets we own. Use them on every asset,
   unchanged, forever.** Measure each asset on Fame (share who link the cue to
   Volyume) and Uniqueness (how exclusively it points to us).
   Source: How Brands Grow ch.8; Ehrenberg-Bass DBA framework.
   *Contested:* Kantar/Hollis argue meaning still matters; the debate is about
   emphasis, not whether distinctiveness works.

**Reconciliation with Layer 2 (important, do not skip).** Layer 1 governs the
BRAND (be consistent, distinctive, broadly reaching, always-on). Layer 2 governs
the MESSAGE to a cold viewer (meet them where their awareness is). They are not
in conflict: the distinctive assets carry recognition; the message earns the
watch. Tonight's failures were Layer 2 failures (wrong awareness stage, unclear
copy) sitting on top of Layer 1 assets that are already right.

## Layer 2 — The message to a cold stranger (Schwartz, the verified canon)

5. **Match the message to the viewer's awareness stage. [CANON]** Schwartz's
   five stages (unaware → problem-aware → solution-aware → product-aware →
   most-aware). A cold scroller who has never heard of Volyume is **problem-
   aware at best**: they feel the problem, they do not know the solution or the
   product. → Lead with the PROBLEM they feel, in their words, then introduce
   the product as the answer. Feature-led product copy is for product-aware
   audiences we do not have yet. **This is exactly why the clever, product-first
   and mood-first drafts failed a cold viewer tonight — wrong awareness stage.**
   Source: Breakthrough Advertising (1966); Great Leads (2010); Copyhackers.
   *Note:* the five-stage message-match is verified canon. Schwartz's separate
   "market sophistication escalation" theory FAILED verification — see Do Not Use.

6. **Reason-why copy. [CANON]** Hopkins: give the concrete reason a claim is
   true; specifics sell, vague superlatives do not. → Volyume's honest "here is
   the reason for the weekly call" is textbook reason-why, and it is a genuine
   asset. Lead with the specific over the vague every time.
   Source: Scientific Advertising (Hopkins); Ogilvy on Advertising.

## Layer 3 — Google Play ASO (the concrete organic lever)

7. **The title carries the most keyword weight. [PRACTITIONER]** Keyword
   hierarchy: title > short description > long description. One study found
   84.2% of ranking gains correlated with short-description keywords, so the
   short description is underrated, not a contradiction. Ranking factors:
   metadata, engagement/retention, Android Vitals (crash < ~1.09%, ANR <
   ~0.47% or listing is suppressed), ratings/reviews, install volume, update
   frequency, localisation, category. **2025 rebalance toward retention and
   engagement over raw installs** — which suits Volyume's retention focus.
   Source: ApptTweak, MobileAction, AppRadar (practitioner consensus; Google
   does not publish weights — re-verify before acting).

## Layer 4 — UK ad law (ASA / CAP Code, legally binding)

8. **These are law, not guidance. [LAW]** Verified verbatim against the
   regulator:
   - **No precise weight loss in a stated period** (CAP 13.9). Never "lose X in
     Y weeks".
   - **Efficacy claims need rigorous human trials, not testimonials** (CAP 13.1).
   - **Objective health/slimming claims need trials on people** (CAP 12.1).
   - **Hold documentary evidence BEFORE publication; the burden is on us**
     (CAP 3.7).
   → Every weight, health or outcome line must clear these before it ships.
   Cross-referenced in CLAIMS-STANDARDS §7.
   Source: asa.org.uk CAP Code sections 3, 12, 13.
   *Note:* a specific food sub-rule (13.10.1) cited in early drafts FAILED
   verification — do not cite it; the four above are the verified ones.

## Layer 5 — Persuasion psychology (Cialdini, Kahneman, fluency, memory, emotion)

Added 2026-07-12 from a founder-sourced external research report, evidence-graded
against primary/academic sources. Fills the gap Layer 1-4 left open.

9. **Cialdini's principles, graded for a zero-review cold start. [CANON]**
   Usable now: reciprocity (give real value first — a "change or hold"
   explainer, a checklist), commitment/consistency (small first actions in
   onboarding), liking (sound like a lifter, not a wellness brand), authority
   (transparent mechanism substitutes for missing crowd proof), unity ("built
   for self-coached lifters" — an honest shared-identity frame). Usable only
   if literally true: scarcity (real founding pricing/beta capacity only —
   fake urgency backfires, see rule 22). **Not usable yet: social proof** —
   there is no honest crowd signal; do not fake it, switch it on once reviews
   exist. Source: Cialdini, *Influence* / *Pre-Suasion*.

10. **Framing and anchoring are the behavioural-economics survivors. [LAW]**
    Both replicate robustly (Tversky & Kahneman's original framing paper,
    Kühberger's review; Many Labs and large-scale anchoring replications). →
    Frame "hold" as protecting progress made, "change" as avoiding wasted
    effort — same fact, better frame.

11. **Loss aversion is real but weaker than the "2x" folklore, and priming
    does not replicate. [LAW, contested magnitude / DO NOT USE for priming]**
    A 2024 meta found strong loss aversion; a 2025 re-meta found it weakens
    sharply under symmetric designs — use sparingly and only when concrete
    ("changing too early can waste a good run"), never as a fixed multiplier
    or melodrama. Separately: social/subtle-cue priming failed replication
    hard (94% of close replications smaller than the original, 17%
    significant in the original direction) — never build a rule on it.
    Source: Brown et al. 2024; Yechiam 2025; Mac Giolla et al. 2024.

12. **Plain, concrete language is the evidence base for the say-it-out-loud
    rule, not just founder taste. [LAW]** Processing-fluency research: easy
    to read/parse feels truer and more trustworthy. This is why
    CLAIMS-STANDARDS §9.10 exists and why it stays absolute.
    Source: Schwarz et al.; Reber; Alter & Oppenheimer.

13. **Concreteness and picture superiority beat abstraction. [LAW]** Concrete
    words and real images are remembered far better than abstract ones. This
    is the evidence base for the mandatory real-app screenshot rule (identity
    Addendum A2) — showing the actual Today screen beats describing it.
    Source: Fliessbach et al.; Taylor et al.; Packard; Baadte et al.; Defeyter
    et al.; Mintzer & Snodgrass.

14. **One distinctive element per asset (von Restorff). [LAW]** A single
    standout beats the tenth generic one. Volyume's distinctive element is
    the change-or-hold mechanism itself, not "AI" as a buzzword — never lead
    with the category, lead with the mechanism. Source: Hunt; Schmidt; Chee
    & Goh.

15. **Emotion and mechanism both matter; roughly two-thirds
    problem/mechanism to one-third identity/emotion. [INDUSTRY DATA, case-
    study based, not RCT]** Binet & Field/IPA: emotional brand-building work
    pays back longer than pure rational activation, but the evidence skews to
    large-brand award data — treat as directional, not a law. High-arousal
    emotion (relief, mild surprise, recognition) drives sharing more than
    valence alone; keep it honest, not manufactured outrage. Negativity works
    only if resolved immediately in the same asset, never left hanging.
    Humour lifts attention and attitude but must not eclipse the mechanism.
    Source: Binet & Field/IPA; Berger & Milkman; Baumeister et al.; Eisend
    2009/2010.

16. **"You"-language, question hooks and precise numbers help, with real
    boundary conditions — not blank cheques. [LAW, mixed effects]** Use "you"
    to name the viewer's problem, never accusatorially. Use a question hook
    only when the implied answer is obvious (vague/clickbait questions can
    read as gimmicky and lower trust). Use precise numbers only when true and
    diagnostic (Volyume's real "3 morning weigh-ins" figure), never as
    decoration. Source: Cruz et al.; Serbun et al.; Packard et al.; Lai &
    Farbrot; Escher et al.; Schindler & Yalch.

17. **Fake scarcity, fake reviews and shame-based copy reliably backfire —
    hard constraints, not techniques. [LAW]** Scarcity/dishonest-proof
    backfire risk is well evidenced (reactance, distrust). Separately,
    controlling or moralising language triggers reactance and the boomerang
    effect — pushing the audience away from the ask — and self-directed
    audiences (exactly Volyume's self-coached lifters) are the most
    sensitive to it. This is the evidence base for the existing no-shame
    tone rule (CLAIMS-STANDARDS §8); it is a measurable risk, not brand
    style. Never ship "you're sabotaging your gains"-register copy. Source:
    Mukherjee et al.; Biraglia et al.; Aguirre-Rodriguez; Otero et al.;
    Walther et al.; Steindl et al.; Sprengholz et al.; Mann & Hill.

## Layer 6 — Cold-start distribution reality

Added 2026-07-12, same source as Layer 5. Closes the four gaps Layer 3 left
open.

18. **All three short-form platforms can reach a zero-follower account; none
    guarantees a number. [LAW, platform-documented]** TikTok states follower
    count and prior video performance are NOT direct ranking factors;
    completion is a strong signal. Instagram: public accounts can reach non-
    followers; watch time/likes/sends matter; low-res, watermarked, or
    minimally-edited reposts are down-ranked. YouTube: early Shorts views act
    as an "impressions" test, then retention/swipe-away decides further
    reach. → Post one clean, unwatermarked master to all three, caption/
    package per platform; no platform is categorically dead for a new
    account. Source: TikTok Newsroom; Instagram ranking/originality
    guidance; YouTube Blog/Help.

19. **Consistency beats cadence hacks; "account warm-up" is folklore.
    [PRACTITIONER, platform-stated]** Both TikTok/Instagram/YouTube's own
    creator guidance says: pick a schedule you can sustain. No official
    support for warm-up rituals or secret posting hours.

20. **Reviews are a real ranking/download input; no public featuring
    threshold exists. [LAW for the mechanism, DO NOT USE for any number]**
    Google's docs: search ranking factors in ratings/reviews/downloads; a
    peer-reviewed App Store panel study (341 new apps) found ratings and
    reviews have direct and interaction effects on downloads. → Reviews
    matter, genuinely — never plan around an invented threshold number.
    Source: Google Play ranking docs; Sällberg, Wang & Numminen.

21. **Fire the review prompt after real value has landed, never gated on a
    happy-path question. [LAW (policy) + PRACTITIONER (timing)]** Google's
    API guidance bans pre-qualifying gating. Vendor case studies (not a
    universal law) found prompting at the "aha moment" beats prompting at
    onboarding's end. → Fire it after a user has seen at least one, ideally
    two, interpretable weekly decisions. Source: Google Play In-App Review
    API docs; Appbot case studies.

22. **Reach-first growth (Layer 1) and subscription retention answer
    different questions, not competing ones. [Reconciliation, inference from
    RevenueCat 2026 benchmarks]** Health & Fitness: 37.7% median trial-to-
    paid, 2.9% median day-35 download-to-paid, 82.1% of trials start day 0. →
    A zero-user subscription app optimises, in order: activation → early
    retention → conversion → scale installs. Do not build hard targets on
    scattered D1/D7 fitness listicle numbers — unverified, see Do Not Use.

23. **Reddit's "90/10" rule is a norm, not a sitewide law; the subreddit's
    moderators are the real authority. [LAW, platform-stated]** Matches the
    existing founder-only Reddit rule exactly: be useful and answer
    questions far more than you link, disclose affiliation, link only where
    explicitly permitted. Source: Reddit Help/Reddiquette.

24. **Shareable in-product artefacts are a proven growth mechanism in
    fitness apps. [PRACTITIONER — verified as real mechanism, not causal
    size]** Strava (kudos, activity posts) and Hevy (shareable workout
    cards) both centre growth on this. → Product idea, flagged not built:
    a shareable weekly decision card ("Hold this week" / "Change this week"
    plus the reason) is Volyume's natural analogue — worth a founder look as
    a growth feature.

## The HOOK rubric (a critic grades the first line 0-5)

Score the opening scene/line against these; 4+ to pass:
1. **Awareness-matched:** does it open on a problem a cold, problem-aware
   stranger already feels, in their words (Layer 2.5)? [required]
2. **Specific, not vague:** a concrete situation or number, not a superlative
   (Layer 2.6)?
3. **Would it stop a scroll:** is there a real tension, gap, or recognition in
   the first beat?
4. **Plain enough to say out loud** (CLAIMS-STANDARDS §9.10)?
5. **No product jargon before the plain explanation** (§9.11)?

## The AD-STRUCTURE rubric (a critic grades the whole piece)

1. **One message,** not five. The single thing a stranger should leave with.
2. **Problem → product → reason → proof → where-to-get-it** (Schwartz-ordered).
3. **Benefit before feature** (FAB): what it does for them, then how.
4. **Distinctive assets present and unchanged:** amber, wordmark, mono numerals
   (Layer 1.4). [required]
5. **Names Volyume and says what it is by beat 2** (§ advertising register).
6. **Shows the real app** (identity Addendum A2). [required]
7. **One clear call to action:** free to start on Google Play; weekly coaching
   is Pro.
8. **ASA-clean:** no banned weight/health/outcome claim (Layer 4). [required]

A piece that fails any [required] line does not ship.

## DO NOT USE (failed adversarial verification)

These were plausible and widely repeated but could not survive fact-checking.
No agent may reintroduce them without fresh, sourced verification:
- **Platform engagement figures** (e.g. "TikTok 2.8% vs Reels 0.65% vs Shorts
  0.30%"). Unverified. Do not claim one platform out-engages another by number.
- **Exact zero-follower reach numbers for any platform** (e.g. "X views on
  average for a new account"). The MECHANISM is verified (Layer 6.18); no
  platform publishes a reach guarantee, and no third-party figure is trusted.
- **Exact hook-rate/hold-rate thresholds** (e.g. "you need 70% retention in
  the first 3 seconds"). Platforms name the signal families, never a number.
- **"85% of featured Play apps are rated 4.0+."** Refuted. No public
  featuring threshold exists for review count or rating (Layer 6.20).
- **"Account warm-up rituals" before posting.** No official support.
- **Schwartz's five-stage market-SOPHISTICATION escalation** (distinct from the
  awareness stages, which are verified). Do not use the sophistication theory.
- **CAP rule "13.10.1 food weight-loss rate".** Failed verification; cite 13.9
  instead.
- **"A single home page cannot serve all awareness stages."** Unverified.
- **Loss aversion as a fixed "losses hurt 2x as much as gains help" law.**
  Contested magnitude (Layer 5.11) — use loss framing sparingly, never as a
  quantified multiplier.
- **Social/subtle-cue priming as a reliable technique** ("prime with
  achievement words"). Failed replication hard (Layer 5.11).
- **Precise D1/D7 fitness-retention benchmark figures from growth-blog
  listicles.** RevenueCat's named-methodology funnel numbers (Layer 6.22) are
  trusted; scattered D1/D7 figures without visible methodology are not.
- **"You-language always converts better" / "question headlines always
  outperform statements" as blanket rules.** Both are real effects with real
  boundary conditions (Layer 5.16), not universal wins — judge case by case
  against the rule, not as a reflex.

## Open questions (honestly still unresolved)

1. Exact zero-follower reach magnitude on any platform — the mechanism is
   verified (Layer 6.18), the number is not, and no number should be quoted.
2. Precise D1/D7 fitness-retention benchmarks below the RevenueCat funnel
   numbers already trusted (Layer 6.22).
3. Real causal lift (not just existence) of in-product referral/share
   features in fitness apps specifically (Layer 6.24) — evidenced as a real
   mechanism, not yet as a quantified lever.
