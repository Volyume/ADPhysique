## Ruling 2 — Mid-session load adds: **overshoot only, AMENDED with a senior gate**

Founder's answer verbatim: "Overshoot only. No mid-session load ADD during
deload/recovery, re-entry easing or an active readiness reduction. Those are
senior."

no sub-band set today, never compounding — AND the add path is disabled entirely
whenever deload/recovery, re-entry easing, or an active (undismissed) readiness
reduction is in force. Under those states the resolver may hold or reduce, never
add. §12.1 amended accordingly; §20 pins the adversarial cases.

the opening load; the consecutive-miss rule remains the backstop. §10.1 stands.
