## FINDINGS

  "too many taps / menus mid-workout" is the top complaint. | CONFIDENCE PARTIAL | PROVENANCE
  AGGREGATOR (setgraph.app roundups characterising Reddit sentiment) | US-SKEWED — confirm for UK.
- **WS-F2** | The previous workout's numbers should be visible at the point of logging, without taps
  or scrolling. | CONFIDENCE PARTIAL | PROVENANCE AGGREGATOR (setgraph roundups) | US-SKEWED.
- **WS-F3** | Positioning split: Strong = minimalist/clean/uncluttered fast logging; Hevy =
  modern/feature-rich (muscle illustrations, animations, social) yet still fast to log. | CONFIDENCE
  PARTIAL | PROVENANCE AGGREGATOR (multiple Strong-vs-Hevy comparison blogs) | US-SKEWED.

  "The interface is clean and easy to navigate"; "incredibly intuitive". One concern: "The tracking
