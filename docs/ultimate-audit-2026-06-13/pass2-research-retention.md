# PASS 2 — RESEARCH: RETENTION (area code RE)

Method: direct, no agents, provenance-labelled. UX/retention-research area.

## FINDINGS
- **RE-F1** | Streaks are cited as the most powerful retention mechanism (loss-aversion → users keep
  showing up); BUT a broken streak triggers guilt-abandonment, so leading apps offer a "streak freeze"
  (~once/month) to protect it. | CONFIDENCE PARTIAL | PROVENANCE AGGREGATOR (productgrowth/recurrr) |
  US-SKEWED. → Volyume HAS streaks (WeeklyStreakStrip, StreakWeeksSection, milestones, Pass-1); a
  streak-FREEZE mechanic appears ABSENT (VALUE DEFERRED) → candidate improvement.
- **RE-F2** | Churn benchmark (aggregator-cited): ~80% abandon within 3 months, typically after a
  plateau or missed workouts; Day-7 retention 10-15%, Day-30 only 5-7%. | CONFIDENCE PARTIAL |
  PROVENANCE AGGREGATOR (enable3/productgrowth benchmarks) | US-SKEWED. ⚠ verify before quoting. →
  "plateau" as the #1 churn trigger → Volyume's detectPlateau + coaching (Pass-1) directly addresses it.
- **RE-F3** | Gamification (badges, progress bars, confetti, leaderboards) + social/community
  ("alone users quit, with others persist") drive retention. | CONFIDENCE PARTIAL | PROVENANCE
  AGGREGATOR | US-SKEWED. → Volyume has milestones/PRs/PRCelebration + training partners (Pass-1) —
  partial social coverage; no public leaderboards (deliberate, ED-safety-adjacent).
- **RE-F4** | Notifications: 72% find reminders helpful (not annoying) IF personalised/well-timed;
  personalised get ~40% better engagement; push can lift retention up to 5×. Generic/poorly-timed →
  mute/uninstall. | CONFIDENCE PARTIAL | PROVENANCE AGGREGATOR (glofox/finegym) | US-SKEWED. → Volyume
  has notification_preferences + scheduler + check-in reminders (Pass-1).
- **RE-F5** | Pattern: send a consistency reminder after ~3 completed workouts in a week. | CONFIDENCE
  PARTIAL | PROVENANCE AGGREGATOR | US-SKEWED. → Matches Volyume first_week milestone (hasThreeInSeven).
