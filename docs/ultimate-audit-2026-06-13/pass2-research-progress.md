## FINDINGS
- **PR-F1** | Progress charts/strength graphs are more motivating than raw numbers; an upward trend is
  the motivator. Users value total-volume, frequency, and exercise-specific progress charts. |
  CONFIDENCE PARTIAL | PROVENANCE AGGREGATOR (setgraph roundups) | US-SKEWED. → Volyume has progress
  stats + LiftProgress (Free, Pass-1); at parity on charts.
- **PR-F2** | Progress PHOTOS + body measurements are high-demand: MacroFactor shipped them after
  "more than 1,500 upvotes each" on its roadmap; valuable because weight can stay flat while the body
  recomposes. | CONFIDENCE PARTIAL | PROVENANCE AGGREGATOR (MacroFactor vendor) | US-SKEWED. →
  **Directly corroborates Volyume U-D-1**: progress photos are high-demand and Volyume has only the DB
  table, no UI (Pass-1 Section 4 PARTIAL/table-only). Real gap.
- **PR-F3** | Body-measurement granularity benchmark: Shapez tracks ~11 measurement points (neck,
  shoulders, chest, biceps, forearms, waist, abdomen, hips, buttocks, thighs, calves) + 3 photo angles
  (front/side/back). | CONFIDENCE PARTIAL | PROVENANCE AGGREGATOR (Shapez listing) | US-SKEWED.
- **PR-F4** | Retention link: "clients who watch their own numbers move stay in the programme longer";
  personal-best alerts, before/after photos, streak counters drive retention without manual effort. |
  CONFIDENCE PARTIAL | PROVENANCE AGGREGATOR (fitfocus coach-tool) | US-SKEWED. → Volyume HAS PRs +
  streaks (Pass-1 EXISTS) but lacks before/after photos UI — partial coverage of the retention triad.
