## Full description (Play allows 4,000 chars; this is ~1,700)

    Volyume plans your training, watches how your body responds, and
    adjusts your calories and your plan every week, with a written
    explanation of what changed and why.

    LOG YOUR TRAINING, FREE, FOREVER
    Unlimited workout logging. An exercise library with personal
    records, a plan library, a custom plan builder, training blocks
    and full progress stats. No ads, ever.

    A COACH, NOT A CALCULATOR
    Start with 14 days of the full app. Precision Coaching builds a
    plan around your schedule, goals and experience, sets personalised
    calorie and protein targets, then reviews your week from your
    check-in and adjusts. After every check-in it explains its
    decision in plain language.

    FOOD LOGGING WITHOUT THE PAYWALL GAMES
    Barcode scanning is part of the nutrition experience, not a
    ransom note. Search a UK-first food database offline, scan
    labels, save your own meals and recipes, and see sodium and
    sugar where you want them.

    BUILT CALM ON PURPOSE
    No streak pressure, no shame copy, no red numbers for eating.
    Safety floors that never move, whoever you are. Reduce Motion,
    larger text, higher contrast and a colour-blind-safe palette are
    first-class. TalkBack works in the two flows that matter most:
    logging a set and logging food.

    YOUR DATA IS YOURS
    Works fully offline. Everything exports whenever you want. Your
    data lives encrypted on your phone and in the UK/EU, is never
    sold, and never trains a public AI model.

    Volyume Pro (the coach, nutrition targets, the food diary and
    check-ins) is a monthly or annual subscription after the trial.
    Everything in the free list above stays free.
