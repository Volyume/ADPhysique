## The market shape (2025–2026)

The tracker market has **bifurcated**: cheap-or-free *loggers* (Hevy, Strong) vs
premium *coaching-algorithm* apps (MacroFactor, RP, Caliber). Three forces define
the moment, and Volyume is counter-positioned to all three:

1. **AI became the loud default.** Hevy Trainer, Fitbod, Cronometer Oracle, WHOOP
   AI, Arvo all shipped AI in the last ~12 months. Reviews frequently find the AI
   *generic and sometimes wrong* on progressive overload. → Volyume's
   "deterministic, no AI, ever" is now a **contrarian trust position**, not a gap.
   But it must be *marketed as a deliberate stance*, not left as an apparent
   absence.
2. **Paywall creep is the loudest resentment.** MFP moved barcode behind Premium;
   Strava moved segments/leaderboards behind Premium ahead of its 2026 IPO. Both
   drew sustained backlash. → Volyume's honest binary free/pro with a *generous*
   free logger is a **reputational asset to protect** — never migrate a free
   feature into Pro.
3. **Shame mechanics eject the majority.** Performance-based social features raise
   anxiety for ~68% of recreational users; leaderboards "motivate the already-fit
   while quietly ejecting everyone else." → Volyume's calm/no-shame/ED-safe brand
   is the counter, and the research validates it as a *retention* strategy, not
   just an ethical one.

## The competitors that matter most

- **MacroFactor — the closest philosophical rival.** Its dynamic-expenditure TDEE
  algorithm does for ~$6/mo what coaches charge hundreds for, and it is
  deliberately **"adherence-neutral"** — a missed day doesn't break progress or
  throw a guilt warning. This is a *calm, no-shame mechanic done well*, and O7's
  #2 lesson: **adopt adherence-neutral / no-guilt missed days as a named
  behaviour** (Volyume already does this in the partner streak and missed-week
  handling — extend and name it).
- **Hevy — the polish benchmark.** Clean, fast logging; unusually generous free
  tier drives word-of-mouth. Lesson: match Hevy's *polish*, not its social feed.
- **Strong — the cautionary tale.** A beautiful logger that stopped evolving and
  is being eaten. Lesson: keep shipping.
- **Finch (10M+, 4.9★) — the accountability proof.** Non-judgemental "your bird
  waits for you" beats punitive streaks for anxious users. Direct validation of
  the Partners direction (doc 06): relational, not evaluative.
- **Gentler Streak — the soul analogue.** O8 names it "the closest external
  analogue to Volyume's soul": rest as a first-class state, self-relative
  progress, the courage to say "enough." **Study it closely** — it is the design
  north star for the whole retention layer.
- **WHOOP — the weekly-ritual pattern.** A predictable weekly assessment
  (headline → trends → detail) retains more calmly than daily nagging. → Package
  `runWeeklyCoach` as an anticipated weekly moment (P2-2).

## The under-served gap Volyume can own

Both research agents reached this independently, and it is the strategic thesis of
the whole audit:

> **A calm, private, genuinely-deterministic, non-judgemental coaching app — no
> AI black box, no shame streaks, no photo cloud-upload, no paywall
> bait-and-switch, ED-safe by construction. Every competitor nails one or two of
> these; none combines trust + privacy + calm voice as a whole product.**

The concrete expression O7 proposes, which doc 06 adopts: **Partners as a 1:1
"witnessed effort" pairing** that captures Strava's *retaining* half (relational
recognition) while stripping its *anxiety* half (evaluative comparison) — a
combination no one in the set offers.

## Store-quality bar 2026 (table stakes for "elite")

- **Apple (Jun 2026) + Google (Apr 2026)** both tightened low-quality-app and
  health-data rules. Google's policy now **forbids unauthorised health-data
  sharing** — Volyume's on-device-only photos put it *ahead* of the bar.
- **Apple now rejects free-trial toggle paywalls** (Guideline 3.1.2). The
  compliant, higher-trust pattern is the **honest timeline paywall** ("Today: full
  access → reminder day → charge day") with visible price/term/trial and a Restore
  button. → Billing-adjacent; a founder decision with a written test plan (doc 08,
  P-avoid-4).
- Featuring rewards design quality, correct platform integration, and clear
  paywalls — all of which the elevation work directly improves.

## Pricing context

Annual nutrition/training apps cluster at **$70–96/yr**; loggers at £30–40/yr or
~$75 lifetime; premium human coaching far above. Volyume sits mid-market and O7's
read is it **should stay there** — the differentiation is trust/privacy/calm, not
price. Pricing/packaging is a founder decision; the audit makes no recommendation.

## The 15-lesson shortlist (O7) and 20-principle shortlist (O8), distilled

The patterns worth adopting, each already compatible with a lock or already
half-built:

1. **Market the no-AI determinism as a trust stance** (not silence about it).
2. **Adherence-neutral / no-guilt missed days**, named and extended.
3. **Never migrate a free feature into Pro** — protect the reputational asset.
4. **Partners = relational, never evaluative** (Finch/Strava-kudos, not leaderboards).
5. **Weaponise offline-first + on-device photos** as marketable privacy benefits.
6. **Rest as a first-class state; reward showing up** (Gentler Streak).
7. **Weekly ritual as the retention spine** (WHOOP).
8. **Ghost-overlay capture + comparison-as-hero + private-by-default** (already shipped).
9. **Honest timeline paywall** (Apple-compliant, billing-gated).
10. **One brand, two dialects** — the governing platform rule.
11. **Motion/haptics at moments of meaning, calm amplitude.**
12. **Latency masking** — exploit the offline-first speed edge.
13. **Empty/error/first-run craft** in the calm voice.
14. **Contextual per-action permissions** (Article 9 gate stays first, fail-closed).
15. **Self-relative progress, never peer comparison.**

## The one pattern to refuse

O8's single strongest "avoid": **manufactured streak-loss anxiety and
variable-reward slot-machine loops.** Loss aversion is the industry's default
retention engine, but for a shame-prone, ED-sensitive audience it is actively
harmful and conflicts head-on with the brand, the ED-safety mandate, and the
deterministic-trust stance. Volyume tracks *consistency/weekly rhythm with
automatic grace*, self-relative, predictably rewarded — never a fragile
perfect-streak counter or randomised dopamine. This is not a constraint; it is
the differentiator.
