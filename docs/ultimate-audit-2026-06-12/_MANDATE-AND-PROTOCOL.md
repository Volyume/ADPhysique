## The order, as given

Audit EVERY component, feature, page and flow we have — exactly what we have,
in serial. Then targeted research per area against the best ~50 apps for that
area's flows/desires: where are they better than us and WHY; what can we pick
up, learn, develop. Goal: rebuild ours in our own style so each component is
individually better than every other app's, and the whole is the all-inclusive
best app it can be — the ultimate coach-in-your-pocket for a small price.

Mandatory lenses on every area:
- Usability, feel, look; what drives retention and custom.
- Anything MISSING vs all other apps that we should implement.
- The scope has CHANGED: expanding userbase — gym newbies and light users AND
  athletes, perfectly balanced. How does what we have change to fit?
- Feature linkage + placement: is every feature in an understandable,
  easy-to-find place, optimal for a human using the app in real life?
  Consider the psychology.
- Welcoming, helpful, supportive — without losing the performance-and-results
  nature of the app.

Deliverable: deep research into everything, then composed proposals to hugely
elevate Volyume.

## VERIFICATION PROTOCOL — every turn, no exceptions (founder: "we can't
have a repeat of earlier")

1. Tooling is PROVEN before any research claim: this session verified live
   WebSearch, end-to-end WebFetch (ISSN stand quoted verbatim) and agent
   completion. Every research agent re-proves by quoting fetched content
   with its URL in its first output.
2. Every competitive claim carries a fetched-source URL. 2+ independent
   sources for load-bearing claims. UNVERIFIABLE is an acceptable verdict;
   plausible invention is not. Failed fetches are logged per-URL.
3. Research agents that cannot fetch STOP and report — never degrade
   silently (CLAUDE.md standing rule).
4. Each returning research report gets an adversarial spot-check (orchestrator
   opens a sample of its cited URLs) BEFORE its findings enter the corpus;
   each completed wave gets a citation audit in the 2026-06-08 style.
5. Agents are supervised: stale ~5 min → checked; hung ~25 min → killed and
   relaunched. Internal audits are code-verified (file:line evidence).

## Phases

- **Phase 1 — internal audit, SERIAL:** one area at a time, agents read the
  real code/screens/flows and document exactly what exists (file:line),
  including linkage + placement of every entry point. Output:
  audit/a-NN-<area>.md.
- **Phase 2 — external research, per area:** best-in-class apps for that
  area (top ~50 across the app universe; per area the relevant leaders),
  fetched sources, better-than-us analysis. Output: research/r-NN-<area>.md
  + validation/v-NN spot-check record.
- **Phase 3 — synthesis + proposals:** composed centrally (design judgement,
  dual-market balance, placement psychology). Output: proposals/p-NN +
  master elevation plan for founder sign-off. Nothing builds before its
  research passes audit (gate model, proven on the meal library today).
