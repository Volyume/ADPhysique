### 1.2 Structure engine, as observed (D222)

Header `src/lib/structure/engine.js:1-35`, constants `src/lib/structure/rulebook.js`.

Inputs (`buildWith` args, engine.js:310-321): `goal`, `days` (2 to 6, `DAY_COUNTS`), `lengthMinutes` (30/45/60/75/90, `TIME_MODEL.lengths`), `equipment` (kit; default full_gym), `experience` (beginner/intermediate/advanced), `focus` (rulebook muscles, max 3 picks, `FOCUS.maxPicks`), `library`, `loggedExerciseNames` (continuity), `startIndex` (cyclic shift), `strategy`. Output: `{ ok:true, plan, report }` or `{ ok:false, refusal }`.

Observed rules (rulebook.js): tiers P/S/M/I per muscle per division; weekly sets: P min 6/8/10/10/10 (by days 2..6), typical 14, max 20; S min 4/5/6, typical 10, max 14; M min 2/3/4, typical 6, max 8; I (indirect) max direct 4 (`VOLUME`, lines 134-139). Experience scaling beginner 0.7, intermediate 1, advanced 1.15 (`EXPERIENCE`, line 298). Session limits: hard 8 exercises, 24 working sets, 2 to 4 sets per exercise (`SESSION_LIMITS`). Focus: adds up to typical +4, cap 20 sets (`FOCUS`). Per muscle per session: 8 direct / 11 fractional (`PER_SESSION`). Progression: 6 weeks, peak week 5, deload week 6, week 1 = 70% of sets (floor 2), max step 1/week, deload 50%, RIR ladder `[3,2,2,1,1,4]`, no compound set below RIR 1 (`PROGRESSION`, lines 326-339). Rep ranges 6-10 heavy compound, 8-12 moderate, 8-15 machine, 10-20 isolation (`REP_RANGES`). Time model: 7.5 min overhead, 60 s per set, per-kit transition seconds; session may exceed chosen length by at most 5 minutes after rest is shortened, otherwise the person is asked for longer sessions or more days (`TIME_MODEL.toleranceMinutes`, Q1=C). Joint rules: men's physique zero overhead press sessions, lateral raise to 90 degrees (`JOINT_RULES`).

Determinism: pure, no I/O, "same inputs give the same plan" (engine.js header last line). `checkPlan` (checker.js:69) runs on the peak week; a step is kept only if it adds no new fault.

After-build changes (phase 7, `src/lib/structurePlanChange.js`, `structure/stored.js`): every edit (swap, add, remove, set count) is run through `applyChange`; a change beyond the checks asks the person to keep it ("it is their plan", R71). Swap list filtered by `structureSwapFilter`.

### 5.4 Training decision matrix (OBSERVED `weeklyCoach.js:~330-420`)
Performance score 1 to 4 from session adherence, PR density (or block e1RM slope >= 1.5%), and the check-in's `trainingPerformance` verdict. Recovery 1 to 4 (peak-week soreness-only grade 3 is softened to 2, strictly cause-gated, deload thresholds read the RAW grade). Matrix: recovery 4, or recovery >= 3 with performance 4 = deload (`volumeDelta -2`, flag); either dimension 3 = hold (0); both 1 = +3 sets; one 1 = +2; else +1. Sustained over-performance escalation (D15) is gated by `calmMode`. `shouldDeload` in `algorithms.js:701` weights performance 50%, wellness 30%, soreness 20% on the 1-3 per-session slider scale.

### 5.5 Where a volume decision lands (three paths, OBSERVED `CoachOutputScreen.js:1355-1420`)
- Plans with D219 `plan_facts` v2: `resolveCheckinPlan` / `writeCheckinPlan` via `plan/checkinPlacement.js`, set-by-set placement through `prescribeWeek`, may open a new exercise (CHECKIN_STEPS: +3/+2 strong, +1 nothing to apply, hold, withheld, pull back -2).

- Authored plan: `library/authoredCoach.js` (raise lats and side delts by one set each, stall rule, legs question).
- Holds computed at APPLY time (not from the stale card): `loadVolumeIncreaseHolds` blocks increases on muscles under an active capability episode or flagged sore in this check-in; a failed check withholds the increase and toasts a calm retry (D112 R3). `coachApplySafety.js` holds the shared logic.

## Product-decision observations (B)

Each with evidence. Observations only.

1. Three planner generations coexist in production code. `STRUCTURE_ENGINE` (new, live) and `PLANNER_V2` (D219, also `true`) and legacy `planEngine.generatePlan` (live only for style-pool plans). With STRUCTURE_ENGINE on, `saveWithPlannerV2` is unreachable from `generateAndSavePlan` (`planAutoGen.js:1063` vs the early return above it), yet the D219 modules remain load-bearing for check-in placement and week serving on v2-fact plans. The structure engine does not import the recovery model while D219 does (`plan/readiness.js`); the two systems therefore do not use recovery the same way. Decision: when to retire the D219 build path, and whether the engine should consume the corrected recovery model.
2. Stale and inconsistent flag documentation: `structure/release.js` header says OFF; value true. `plan/release.js` says on "(lead, 2026-10-04 ...)" before the engine existed.
3. `CoachReviewScreen` (720 lines) is registered but has no navigation caller, notification route or deep link (grep in `src`). It also implements its own weekly review on `shouldDeload` and `judgeWeek`. Likely dead surface or a lost entry point; the Coach tab's CoachOutput is the current review. Decision: delete, or re-link.
4. "Training blocks" screen is called `MesocycleBuilderScreen`/route `MesocycleBuilder` but contains no builder (history and advice only); the route name leaks via `navigateCrossTab(..., 'MesocycleBuilder')` in Consistency and Plans. Naming mismatch for future work.
5. Block length truth is split: `mesocycle.js` carries 5-week and 6-week experience-based schedules with set multipliers while the block writer and engine use a fixed 6-week block (comment in `mesocycle.js:12-27`). The multiplier schedule's remaining consumers were not traced; risk of two sources of week-phase truth.
6. `blockReview.reviewSections` is exported but unconsumed; the header promises "ONE coherent proposed next block" produced from ledger + learned volume + epoch + intent + coverage. Only parts of it are consumed (`programmeVerdict`, `proposeNextBlock` for notification). Confirm the next-block proposal UI against that promise.
7. The authored plan ships as a named library entry "Mens Physique Off Season - Allan" (`library/offseasonAllan.js`) with person-specific coach rules (for example "Do your legs look flat in your shorts?"). It is a library item every user can see in Plan Library. Whether naming it after an individual is intended for public users is a product call. Also the hard-coded founder programme id in `structure/release.js:26-29` is in the shipped bundle (a personal constant in production code).
8. Duplicated surfaces: volume-per-muscle judgement is displayed on Heatmap, Progress strip, Summary, CoachReview (unified by `volumeJudgement`, good). Recovery appears as Progress pillar + Recovery screen + planner readiness + coach "recoveryFlag" (check-in) + `recoveryState.js` + `recoveryEMA.js`: several recovery notions (estimated model, check-in 1-4 grade, session slider 1-3 soreness, legacy EMA). Founder already pulled recovery off Today (D224).
9. `lib/trialActivation.js` still holds the check-in gate constants and `TRIAL_LENGTH_DAYS` for a product that is fully free (D137). Naming debt; the constants are live (check-in unlock).
10. Fully-free residue in coach code: `weeklyCoach.js` imports `differentialPaywall` and `FULL_ACCESS_FOR_ALL`; `blockAdvisor.buildNextBlockOptions({isPro})` still computes `recommended: isPro && ...` and `getBlockAdvice(..., {isPro})`. Pinned to free-for-all by `NEXT_BLOCK_OPTION_GATING`, but `isPro` still gates the recommendation, so a caller passing `isPro=false` would get no recommendation. The store clamps tier to full access (`useAppStore.js:44-46`), so this is dormant today. Risk if someone re-arms tiers.
11. SCOFF screen is reachable only from the Coach tab (`YouScreen.js:539-541`); the onboarding does not ask (`wellbeing.js` header: "never asks a mandatory question", FQ-1(c)). `scoffPositive` defaults to TRUE when `userProfile == null` (`CoachOutputScreen:1101, 2125`: `userProfile == null || score >= 2`), a conservative fail-closed default. Users who never opened the screen have `scoffScore` null which reads as 0 (not positive) once a profile exists. Coverage of the screen as a safety net depends on discovery.

Training screens that surface weight/food-adjacent coaching (CoachOutput held-decision card) were not confirmed to print it (NOT TRACED the held-decision copy). Constitution says signposting must never be removed or gated; worth verifying the ED-lockout card copy.
13. The logger is a single 8,014-line component. High change risk (live production, paying/real users), and the logger rebuild is layered over by phases (R3, 2B, B/C, D220). Opportunity: split with the same tests as guard (`ActiveWorkoutScreen.verticalLogger.guard.test.js`).

Entry-typed signal (`entry_typed`) is collected and synced, but the consumer ("learner data path, lane LR2") was not found by grep in the reading code of `livePrescription.js` or `personalRecovery.js` in this survey (NOT TRACED fully). If no reader exists yet, the cloud column and flag are infrastructure ahead of use.

Users cannot open an exercise page without having logged it or found it in a picker. (OBSERVED: ExerciseDetail callers are LiftProgress, history and picker related.)

Plan re-generation behaviour differs by plan kind: LIBRARY, authored, hand-built and style-pool plans are never rebuilt (R91) but the weekly check-in still works on them via different paths (`checkinPlacement` for v2 facts, `computeVolumeApply` otherwise, `authoredCoach` for authored). Four apply paths for one "Apply training change" button raises QA surface.

A follow-up noted in the task board commit: "engine-path refusal text when the person's exclusions cause it" (commit 38fd470 `docs/TASKBOARD.md` line). Possible user dead end when exclusions/limits make a kit unbuildable.

Opportunities: the weekly coach output already includes `whyThisWeek`, `limiters`, `heldDecisions`, so a Today-level "this week in one line" reader exists (`lib/home/todayLineArbiter.js`, NOT TRACED); training status (natural/enhanced) is deliberately private and only moves a gain ceiling; no AI anywhere (constitution), consistent with all code read.
