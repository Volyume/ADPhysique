## Document 1 — Beverly International: Classic Physique Nutrition Plan

- **URL:** https://beverlyinternational.com/posted_articles/classic-physique-nutrition-plan-competing-in-mens-physique/ (fetched via r.jina.ai proxy; direct fetch blocked by Cloudflare verification)
- **Coach/system:** Beverly Advisor Team, Beverly International. Originally printed in *No Nonsense* magazine, Vol 20, #4.
- **Client context:** Men's Classic Physique contest preparation, structured in three phases from 16–20 weeks out to contest day.
- **Date:** No Nonsense Vol 20, #4 (publication date not shown on page).

### Phase structure (timing rules, as published)

- "Start dieting 16-20 weeks before contest" (Phase 1, muscle-building focus).
- "Switch to Phase 2 at 8-10 weeks out" depending on conditioning progress.
- "Enter Phase 3 only at 4-6 weeks out if progress stalls."
- "The earlier you start, the more time you will be able to spend in Phase 1."

### Phase 1 (16–20 weeks before contest)

Note printed with plan: reduce portions by 2 oz if under 165 lb. All meats weighed before cooking.

- **Meal #1:** 2 whole eggs + 4 egg whites; 6 oz lean meat; ½ cup oatmeal (measured dry)
- **Meal #2 (choose one):**
  - Option A: protein drink with 2 scoops Ultimate Muscle Protein, 1–2 tbsp healthy fat
  - Option B: 8 oz lean beef/chicken OR 10–12 oz tuna, small apple or orange
- **Meal #3:** 8 oz lean meat (chicken or protein source); 6 oz sweet potato or ⅔ cup cooked brown rice; 2 cups vegetables or salad with 4 tbsp vinegar-oil dressing
- **Meal #4:** same as Meal #2
- **Meal #5:** 10 oz very lean meat; 2 cups vegetables; salad with 2 tbsp vinegar-oil dressing
- **Meal #6 (choose one):**
  - Option A: 2 scoops Ultimate Muscle Protein shake/pudding
  - Option B: 8 egg whites + 2 whole eggs OR 6 oz beef, 1–2 cups vegetables

### Phase 2 (8–10 weeks before contest)

Note printed with plan: reduce meat portions by 1–2 oz if 165 lb or less. All meats weighed before cooking.

- **Meal #1:** 8 oz lean beef or turkey; 3 egg whites, 1 yolk; 1 grapefruit
- **Meal #2:** protein drink: 2 scoops Ultimate Muscle Protein/Muscle Provider; 1 tbsp healthy fat or heavy whipping cream
- **Meal #3:** 8 oz chicken or turkey breast; 2 cups vegetables
- **Meal #4 (choose one):**
  - Option A: protein drink: 2 scoops protein, 1 tbsp healthy fat
  - Option B: 6 oz tuna OR 5 oz chicken, 3 egg whites, 1 whole egg, 1 tomato
- **Meal #5:** 10 oz lean meat; 4 cups salad; 2 tbsp cider vinegar + 1 tbsp olive oil dressing
- **Meal #6:** 5 oz chicken or turkey breast; 6 egg whites; 1 cup omelette vegetables
- **Monday & Thursday addition (replace or add as Meal #7):** 1.5 cups oatmeal (precooked) or cooked rice; 10 oz sweet potato; 1 medium banana; 1 cup vegetables; 1 tbsp butter, almond butter, or oil

### Phase 3 (4–6 weeks before contest — only if progress stalls)

- **Meal #1:** 5 oz lean beef or turkey; 6 egg whites + 1 whole egg; ½ grapefruit
- **Meal #2 (choose one):**
  - Option A: 2 scoops protein, 1 tbsp healthy fat (optional), 4 strawberries (optional), 16 oz water
  - Option B: 6 oz tuna or chicken, 3 egg whites + 1 whole egg, 1 tomato
- **Meal #3:** 8 oz chicken; 4 cups salad; 2 tbsp cider vinegar + 1 tbsp olive oil dressing
- **Meal #4 (choose one):**
  - Option A: 6 oz tuna or chicken, 3 egg whites, 1 whole egg, 1 tomato or 1 cup vegetables
  - Option B: 2 scoops protein (post-training with 12 oz water) OR 2 scoops Ultimate Muscle Protein
- **Meal #5:** 8 oz lean meat; 2 cups vegetables
- **Monday & Thursday addition (Meal #6):** 1.5 cups oatmeal or cooked rice; 10 oz sweet potato; 4 oz banana; 1 cup vegetables; 1 tbsp butter, almond butter, or oil

### Supplement protocols (as published)

- **Phase 1 essential:** 1 Super Pak with Meal #1; 4 Ultra 40 tablets + 4 Mass Aminos with each meal. Optional by priority: Glutamine Select 2–4 scoops during training; Muscle Synergy 8 tablets or 1 scoop twice daily (under 185 lb) / 24 tablets or 3 scoops daily (heavier); Creatine Select 5 scoops daily × 5 days, then 2 scoops training days, 1 scoop non-training days. An "economical rotation" alternates Creatine Select and Muscle Synergy in two-week blocks over weeks 1–8.
- **Phase 2:** Meal #1: 1 Super Pak, 3 EFA Gold. Each meal: 4 Ultra 40; 3 Density; 3 Muscularity; 2 Lean Out. Training: 2–4 scoops Glutamine Select + BCAAs + 10–20 Muscle Mass (5 per scoop). Daily: 3 Quadracarn tablets, 3 times (morning, pre-training, bedtime). Optional: Up-Lift 2 scoops pre-training; Creatine Select 2 scoops daily; Muscle Synergy 2–3 scoops or 16–24 tablets daily.
- **Phase 3:** as Phase 2, plus 5 Density pre-training (optional) and 3 7-Keto MuscLean capsules twice daily (morning and afternoon).

### Template conventions observed

- 6 meals per day in Phase 1 and 2; 5 meals in Phase 3 (compression as calories drop).
- Protein at every meal, mostly weighed in oz pre-cooking; carb-containing meals are stripped out phase by phase rather than portions merely shrinking.
- Built-in substitution structure: several meals offer "Option A (shake) / Option B (whole food)" swaps.
- Scheduled carb-up meal twice weekly (Monday and Thursday) replacing a regular meal in Phases 2–3 — a fixed-day refeed convention rather than a training-day-linked one.
- Portion scaling by bodyweight is a flat rule ("reduce portions by 2 oz if under 165 lbs").

Carb-up rule, verbatim: "Every Monday and Thursday eat the following meal in place of one regular meal: 1 ½ cups rice, 1 large sweet potato, 1 banana, 1 cup vegetables with 1 tablespoon oil, butter, or margarine."

### Phase Two — female (6–8 weeks out)

Supplements: "Super Pak, Lean Out 9 per day, Ultra 40 4 / meal, Muscularity 3 / meal"

- **Meal #1:** 4 egg whites – 1 yolk; 3 oz chicken/turkey/tuna; ¼ grapefruit/cantaloupe or 6 strawberries
- **Meal #2:** 4 oz chicken/turkey/tuna; 1 cup vegetables
- **Meal #3:** 1.5 cups salad + 2 tsp oil + 1 tbsp vinegar; 5 oz tuna; 1 small carrot
- **Meal #4:** 1 egg + 2 egg whites
- **Meal #5:** 5 oz chicken/turkey/fish; 1 cup vegetables; 1 tbsp flax oil

Carb-up rule, verbatim: "Every 3rd day eat the following meal in place of one regular meal: 1 cups rice, 1 small sweet potato, 1 small banana, 1 cup vegetables with 2 tsp. oil, butter, or margarine."

### Final phase — female (final 3 weeks)

Daily supplements: "Ultra 4 – 2 in AM and 2 in PM, Muscularity – 9 between meals #1 and #2; and 9 more between meals #4 and #5".
A 3-day rotating cycle, heavily tuna-based with minimal carbs until Day Three, which includes — Meal #5 (Day Three only): "½ cup Cream of Rice (before cooking), 1 sweet potato, 1 cup green beans with 1 tsp. butter".

### Cardio progression (as published)

- 8 weeks out: "four days per week and thirty minutes per session", interval training twice weekly.
- 6 weeks out: add a third interval day (5 total days at 30 min).
- 4 weeks out: "Add three more weekly sessions of 30–45 minutes each at moderate intensity."
- Final week: "Do no cardio at one week out ... pose, pose, pose instead."

### Template conventions observed

- 5–6 meals per day; men's portions roughly 5–12 oz protein per meal, women's 3–5 oz — same skeleton, scaled portions.
- Fixed-schedule carb-ups: men every Monday/Thursday, women every 3rd day, always as a defined replacement meal with exact foods.
- Carbohydrate is progressively removed approaching the show while protein feedings stay constant; fruit is kept only at Meal #1.
- One liquid meal (protein drink) per day positioned mid-plan.

## Document 5 — 3DMJ (Eric Helms): The Default Diet

- **URL:** https://www.3dmusclejourney.com/blog/the-default-diet (fetched directly, full page)
- **Coach/system:** Eric Helms, Team 3D Muscle Journey.
- **Client context:** drug-free bodybuilders across the whole season — the "default diet" is the athlete's deepest contest-prep intake, which is then modified upward for refeeds and the off-season.
- **Date:** 18 March 2025.

### Core concept (as published)

The Default Diet is a foundational eating structure representing the lowest caloric intake phase (deep contest prep), which gets modified upward for refeeds and off-season phases. The aim is to minimise daily food decisions and reduce food obsession across training phases — in Helms' words, to have "both flexibility, _and_ structure".

### Helms' personal default diet (1,600–1,700 kcal), as published

- **Meal 1:** Quest bar + smoothie (spinach, kale, berries, kiwi, whey)
- **Snack:** Quest bar pre-training
- **Post-workout:** 1.5 scoops whey
- **Meal 2:** 300 g egg whites + 2 whole eggs with vegetables OR tuna + egg scramble OR salmon sashimi with salad
- **Meal 3:** 500 g low-fat Greek yoghurt with sweetener

Macros, verbatim: "50g fat, 100g carbs, 200g of protein".

### Modification rules (as published)

- **Refeeds:** add higher-carb fruits, bread, wraps, sushi with rice, honey.
- **Off-season:** replace lean proteins with whole eggs/oil-packed tuna, use full-fat yoghurt, add discretionary calories/desserts.

### Template conventions observed

- A small number of fixed feedings (3 meals + snack + post-workout shake) with built-in same-slot substitutions (Meal 2 offers three interchangeable options).
- The template scales up from the prep baseline by adding carb foods (refeed) or swapping lean protein for fattier versions (off-season), rather than redesigning the day — off-season and prep deliberately resemble each other.

## Document 6 — Layne Norton: The Ultimate Cutting Diet

- **URL:** https://simplyshredded.com/layne-norton-the-most-effective-cutting-diet.html (direct fetch returned 403; fetched in full via r.jina.ai proxy)
- **Coach/system:** Dr Layne Norton, pro natural bodybuilder/coach. SimplyShredded notes this is an archived article (2010) and that Norton's views may have evolved.
- **Client context:** pre-contest cutting / natural bodybuilding contest prep. This is a rules-based template (how to build the plan) rather than a fixed menu.
- **Date:** 2010 (archived article).

### Calorie baseline (verbatim multipliers)

- Mesomorphs: "bodyweight x 15"
- Ectomorphs: "bodyweight x 16-17"
- Endomorphs: "bodyweight x 13-14"
- Worked example in article: a 200 lb mesomorph = 3,000 kcal baseline.

### Macro rules (verbatim)

- Protein while cutting — mesomorphs: "1.2g/lb – 1.3g/lb"; ectomorphs: "1.4g/lb – 1.6g/lb"; endomorphs: "1.4g/lb – 1.5g/lb".
- Fat — mesomorphs: "17% – 23% of total calories"; ectomorphs: "24%-28% of total calories"; endomorphs: "23%-28% of total calories".
- Carbohydrates: the remaining calories after protein and fat are allocated.

### Meal frequency rules (verbatim)

- Mesomorphs: "eat every 2.5 – 3.5 hours".
- Ectomorphs: "eat every 2 – 3 hours".
- Endomorphs: "eat every 3.5 – 5 hours".

### Nutrient timing — daily carbohydrate distribution (verbatim)

- Pre-workout: "Consume 35% of their total daily carbohydrates in a meal 1.5 to 2 hours before their workout."
- Intra-workout: "a shake composed of 30-40g of whey protein along with dextrose or maltodextrin during their workout. The carbohydrates in the shake should account for about 20% of one's total daily carbohydrate intake."
- Post-workout: "a post workout meal composed of whole food, low GI carbohydrate sources...about 30 minutes after finishing the in workout shake. This low GI carbohydrate should contain about 25% of your total daily carbohydrates."
- Waking meal: "consuming 15% of your daily carbohydrate intake at this meal in the form of low GI carbohydrates."

(That accounts for 95% of daily carbs placed at four slots: waking 15%, pre 35%, intra 20%, post 25%.)

### Fat loss and cardio (verbatim)

"I recommend that one lose approximately 80% of their weight due to calorie restriction and 20% of their weight due to cardio." Target rate: 1–1.5 lb per week.

### Refeed protocol (verbatim rules)

- Frequency by body fat: below 10%: "re-feeds two times per week"; 10–15%: "re-feeding every 6-12 days"; above 15%: "once every week to two weeks".
- Composition: "Keep fat as low as possible during re-feed days"; "Reduce protein intake to 1 g/lb bodyweight"; "Consume as little fructose as possible"; "Increase calories to maintenance level...increase carbs by at least 50-100%".

### Template conventions observed

- Body-type-scaled macro template; meal count emerges from eating-interval rules rather than a fixed number.
- Carbohydrates are a timed budget anchored around training; refeeds are carb-only, scheduled by leanness.

## Document 7 — Renaissance Periodization (RP): diet template structure

RP sells the actual templates, so no single free page prints a full template. Structure was assembled from two RP primary articles plus one third-party review of the purchased product, each labelled below.

- **URL:** https://rpstrength.com/blogs/articles/rp-diet-templates-simplified (fetched directly)

- Original templates, verbatim: "6 total meals per day, including a workout shake". The original templates "specify the number of grams of proteins, carbs, and fats".
- Simplified templates, verbatim: "only 3 main meals per day, no workout shake, and an optional snack"; food listed "only in actual weight"; and "Meals Don't Vary Based on Workouts...Your daily meal plan stays quite static from meal to meal, regardless of time of day or physical activity."

### 7b. RP Strength (primary): "RP Diet Template 3.0 Updates"

- **URL:** https://rpstrength.com/blogs/articles/rp-diet-template-3-0-updates (fetched directly)

## Cross-template conventions (what these systems share)

Drawn only from the documents above:

1. **Meals per day:** 5–6 is the dominant convention (Beverly 5–6; RP classic 6 training/5 rest; Cellucor/Sadik 5 + snack; Mountain Dog-style 6–7 training/6 rest). Helms' 3DMJ default and RP Simplified are deliberate low-decision outliers at 3 meals + snack(s).
2. **Protein at every meal**, dosed either by the ounce (Beverly 5–12 oz; Sadik 7 oz; "3oz lean protein" per RP template meal) or by g/lb rules (Norton 1.2–1.6 g/lb).
3. **Carbs anchored to training:** Norton budgets 95% of carbs across waking/pre/intra/post slots; RP scales "Workout Carbs" to session difficulty and timing; Mountain Dog-style days place starch intra/post-workout; Beverly instead uses fixed-day carb-up meals (Mon/Thu, or every 3rd day for women).
4. **Training-day vs rest-day:** RP drops one meal (the workout shake) on rest days; Mountain Dog swaps starch for fat on off days; Beverly and Sadik keep the day identical and handle variation through scheduled carb-ups/cheat meals.
5. **Substitutions are built into the template:** Beverly's Option A/B meals (shake vs whole food); Helms' three interchangeable Meal 2 options; Beverly's "chicken/turkey/fish" interchangeable protein slots.
6. **Refeed/cheat conventions:** fixed weekly slots (Beverly Mon/Thu carb-up meal; Sadik's Tuesday cheat meal) or leanness-driven frequency with composition rules (Norton: high-carb, low-fat, protein cut to 1 g/lb, minimal fructose).
7. **Phase compression:** as a show nears, carbs are removed meal-by-meal and meal counts may shrink (Beverly Phase 1→3), while the protein skeleton of the day stays constant.
