# Source D — Women's Divisions: Published Days of Eating and Prep Diets

Primary-source research for the Volyume meal library. Angle: bikini, figure, wellness and
women's physique competitors' published full days of eating and prep diets, to balance a
corpus that so far skews male.

Researched: 2026-06-12.
Method: live WebSearch and WebFetch of full documents. Where a host returned 403 or a
verification wall (simplyshredded.com, beverlyinternational.com, whatrunslori.com), the same
page was fetched in full via the r.jina.ai text proxy. web.archive.org is blocked in this
environment and could not be used. All quoted plans are reproduced verbatim as published
(American spellings and US measures retained in quotations). Extraction was performed by an
automated reader over the fetched page text; nothing below has been padded or inferred —
where a source omits calories, timing or rest-day handling, that omission is recorded.

Documents with a complete meal-by-meal day of eating: 7 (plus one partial and one
site-published template, both clearly flagged). Wellness division: no usable written
day of eating found from a named competitor — see Gaps and Failed URLs.

## Document 1 — Megan (Honey We're Home), NPC Bikini, 12 weeks out

- **URL:** https://www.honeywerehome.com/2014/04/12-weeks-out-my-bikini-contest-meal-plan.html
- **Person:** Megan, lifestyle blogger at Honey We're Home, prepping for an NPC bikini contest
- **Division:** Bikini (NPC)
- **Context:** 12 weeks out from her show
- **Date:** April 2014
- **Meals per day:** 5
- **Calorie level:** Not stated
- **Training vs rest day:** Not differentiated in the article
- **Pre/post-workout placement:** Not specified

### Day of eating (verbatim as published)

- **Meal 1 (breakfast):** egg whites and oatmeal made into a pancake, eaten dry without
  peanut butter or syrup
- **Meal 2:** 5 oz boneless, skinless chicken breast + 6 oz sweet potato + 1 cup vegetables
- **Meal 3:** 5 oz boneless, skinless chicken breast + 6 oz sweet potato + 1 cup vegetables
- **Meal 4:** ¾ cup egg whites + ¾ cup brown rice + 1 cup mixed vegetables (cooked in
  nonstick spray, no seasonings)
- **Meal 5:** protein shake with one scoop protein powder + 6–8 oz water + 2 ice cubes

### Notes

Stated restrictions: "No fat in my diet, no sugar, no dairy." Eliminated during this prep:
coffee with creamer, peanut butter, syrup, oils, egg yolks, soy sauce, salt, nuts, milk.
Protein sources: egg whites, chicken breast, whey. Carb sources: oats, sweet potato, brown
rice, vegetables. Fat sources: effectively none by design at this stage of prep.

## Document 4 — Amy Roskelley (Health Beet), NPC Masters Bikini, ~52 days out (with macros)

- **URL:** https://healthbeet.org/current-bikini-competition-diet-macros-and-calories/
- **Person:** Amy Roskelley, age 52, NPC masters bikini competitor and publisher of Health Beet
- **Division:** Bikini (NPC, masters)
- **Context:** 2023 prep diary, logged at 52 and 51 days out
- **Date:** 14 April 2023
- **Meals per day:** 4 (breakfast, lunch, snack, dinner)
- **Calorie level:** Stated per day with macros (rare among these sources)
- **Training vs rest day:** Not differentiated in this post

### Day 1 — 52 days out — "161P / 81C / 25F = 1,208 calories" (verbatim as published)

- **Breakfast:** "1 egg + 1 cup egg whites, 30 g avocado, mushrooms and fat free cheddar
  cheese. 1/3 cup oats with Skinny Syrup caramel sauce"
- **Lunch:** "5 ounces chicken, fat free feta cheese, tomato, lettuce and bolthouse ranch"
- **Snack:** protein waffle with raspberries — 1 scoop vanilla protein powder, 1/2 cup egg
  whites
- **Dinner:** "5 ounces chicken, 6 ounces Shirataki noodles, broccoli, onions, mushrooms and
  soy sauce"

### Day 2 — 51 days out — "166P / 77C / 39F = 1,354 calories" (verbatim as published)

- **Breakfast:** "1 egg, 1 cup egg whites, 1/4 cup fat free cheddar cheese"
- **Lunch:** "5 ounces chicken breast, 2 tablespoon Bolthouse ranch dressing, 3 ounces petite
  carrots, 1 cup chopped broccoli, and 1 cup lettuce"
- **Snack:** protein waffle made with UMP protein powder, 1/2 cup egg whites, chocolate
  sauce, cherry
- **Dinner:** turkey-avocado salad from a restaurant, eaten dry

### Notes

The most quantified source in this set: explicit daily calories (~1,200–1,350 kcal) and
macro splits during a masters bikini cut. Heavy use of volume foods (shirataki, lettuce,
broccoli) and fat-free dairy to keep protein high at low calories. Protein sources: egg
whites, eggs, chicken, turkey, whey/UMP. Carb sources: oats, vegetables, fruit. Fat sources:
avocado, dressing, whole egg.

## Document 6 — Jonelle Baglia, Figure (WNBF pro card), Beverly International No Nonsense

- **URL:** https://beverlyinternational.com/posted_articles/figure-contest-success-in-4-different-countries-how-i-did-it/
  (fetched via r.jina.ai proxy after a direct verification wall)
- **Person:** Jonelle Baglia, Shiloh, Illinois; 5'4", 120–122 lb off-season, 115–117 lb on
  stage; competed in BNBF (2009), INBF (2009, earning a WNBF pro card), Musclemania (2011)
  and NPC (2012)
- **Division:** Figure
- **Context:** Her standard competition-era daily diet; the article does not pin it to a
  specific number of weeks out
- **Date:** No Nonsense Magazine, 2014 collection
- **Meals per day:** 5–6 (meal 6 optional)
- **Calorie level:** Not stated
- **Training vs rest day:** Not differentiated; no carb-up protocol given
- **Pre/post-workout placement:** Meal 2 (UMP shake) taken "after my resistance training
  session or as a meal later in the day"

### Day of eating (verbatim as published)

- **Meal 1:** "4 egg whites scrambled with 2 oz ground chicken breast, ½ cup oatmeal"
- **Meal 2:** "2 scoops UMP mixed with water"
- **Meal 3:** "5 oz lean protein (usually chicken breast or fish) topped on a large salad
  always including tomatoes, cucumbers, sweet peppers, red onion, drizzled with red wine
  vinegar; plus a 4-5 oz sweet potato, lightly salted"
- **Meal 4:** "2-3 oz of tuna (in water) mixed with one chopped dill pickle and 1 tbsp light
  mayo, 1 medium tomato in 4 slices… top each tomato slice with the tuna mix and avocado
  slices. Close to competition I omit the mayo and avocado"
- **Meal 5:** "6 oz lean protein, 2 cups of steamed vegetables, usually broccoli florets
  and/or cauliflower"
- **Meal 6 (optional):** "1 scoop of BI UMP pudding made with water" — only if extra hungry
  before bed

### Notes

Demonstrates an explicit late-prep tightening rule from a figure athlete: fats (mayo,
avocado) removed from meal 4 "close to competition". Protein sources: egg whites, ground
chicken, chicken breast, fish, tuna, UMP. Carb sources: oats, sweet potato, vegetables. Fat
sources: avocado and light mayo, dropped near the show.

## Cross-document observations (grounded only in the sources above)

- **Meal frequency:** 5–6 meals dominates (documents 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 9); Roskelley (doc 4)
  uses 4; Bailey (doc 8) currently 3–4.
- **Stated calories:** Only Roskelley publishes numbers: ~1,208–1,354 kcal at 51–52 days out,
  protein 161–166 g. All other plans omit calories entirely.
- **Protein staples across plans:** egg whites (7 of 9 documents), chicken breast (7 of 9),
  whey/UMP shakes (8 of 9), turkey, salmon, tuna, white fish.
- **Carb staples:** oats (8 of 9) and sweet potato (6 of 9), with rice cakes, brown/white
  rice, quinoa, Ezekiel bread and berries appearing once or twice each.
- **Fat handling:** ranges from deliberate elimination ("No fat in my diet" — doc 1) to
  measured single-tablespoon additions of almond/peanut butter, avocado and olive oil; doc 6
  documents stripping fats out as the show approaches.
- **Carb timing:** docs 2 and 3 place starch early or around training and finish the day on
  protein and vegetables; doc 3 describes carb cycling (carbs raised every third day) late in
  prep.
- **UK specifics:** the one UK plan (doc 5, UKBFF) is metric and off-season; no UK prep-phase
  day of eating was found in written form this session.
