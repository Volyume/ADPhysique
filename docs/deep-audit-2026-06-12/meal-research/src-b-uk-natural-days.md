# Source B — UK and Natural Bodybuilding Competitor Full Days of Eating

Primary-source research for the Volyume meal library. Angle: full days of eating and prep
diet write-ups from UK natural bodybuilding competitors (NPA, BNBF, UKDFBA-adjacent) and
natural coaching organisations (3DMJ).

Research date: 2026-06-12. All content below was fetched on this date from the live URLs
shown (or via the r.jina.ai text proxy where the origin server returned 403). Meal plans
are quoted as extracted from each page. Nothing has been invented or padded; where a
source gives no amounts, that is stated. Failed fetches are listed at the end.

## Document 1 — "Matt", NPA, 8 weeks out (Gym Professor / The Gym Revolution)

- **URL:** https://thegymrevolution.co.uk/blog/2023/11/29/natural-bodybuilding-contest-diet-prep/
  (same article also live at https://www.thegymrevolution.co.uk/blog/diet/8-week-bodybuilding-diet/)
- **Person:** "Matt" (surname not given on page). Works at GymRatZ ("UK's #1 for
  Specialist Gym Equipment"); trains at "Bob's Gym – The Home of Bodybuilding in Bristol".
- **Federation:** NPA (Natural Physique Association) — UK drug-free federation.
- **Context:** "Bodybuilding Diet — 8 Weeks out from Comp. (2009)". Active contest prep,
  8 weeks out. Stats at time of writing: 13 stone (182 lb), 5 ft 9 in. Diet is from 2009;
  republished on the blog 29 November 2023.
- **Meals per day:** 8 eating occasions (7 meals/snacks plus an evening BCAA-and-apple slot).

### Full day of eating (as published, with the page's own macro figures)

| Time | Slot | Foods (verbatim) | Macros (C/P/F) |
|---|---|---|---|

| 7:30am | Breakfast | "100g Porridge Oats, 36g Sci-MX Whey Protein (with water), Daily Multi-Vit" | 63.3g / 36g / 9.6g |
| 10:30am | Snack | "2 Medium Apples, 1 Small Banana, 50g Reflex One Stop All-in-One" | 59g / 26.8g / 5.3g |

**Daily totals (as published):** 2,408 kcal; 293.4g carbs; 191.5g protein; 52.1g fat.

### Notes from the article

- Cardio: "Slow-Jog - 30/40mins Steady State Jog @ approx. 6-7kph pace" first thing,
  plus evening gym sessions (training day shown; post-workout meal at 7pm).
- Hydration: "I will also consume around 5 litres of water a day and approx. 10 teas
  (peppermint, black, fruit). I like my teas".
- On strictness: "It's fair to say that I've been better prepared and more strict at this
  stage of competing previously" — he describes the regimen as "isn't too far from my
  normal daily routine" and his mindset as "more a case of 'eye of the pussy cat', than
  'eye of the tiger.'"
- On supplements: "I must admit that if I'm at home for the day, my supplement usage is
  much less, however, being on the go most of the time, I find it of far greater
  convenience grabbing a shake."
- A promised follow-up ("Closer to the competition (e.g. 4 weeks out), I'll post my
  updated eating habits") could not be located on the site (searched 2026-06-12).
- Protein sources: whey, all-in-one shake, ham, chicken breast, smoked salmon.
  Carb sources: porridge oats, fruit (apples, bananas, grapes, blueberries).
  Fat sources: incidental (salmon, shake, oats) — no added oils.

## Document 2 — Akash Vaghela, BNBF Central, Men's Middleweight, 21-week prep (RNT Fitness)

- **URL:** https://www.rntfitness.co.uk/akashs-2017-bodybuilding-prep-write-up/
- **Person/coach:** Akash Vaghela, founder of RNT Fitness (UK online coaching company).
- **Federation/division:** BNBF Central, Men's Middleweight — finished 2nd (overall champion
  Oladipupo Adeniji won the class). Third contest of his career.
- **Context:** Full 21-week prep diary, 5 March 2017 to show day 30 July 2017.
  Start weight 90.2kg, stage weight 71.7kg (−18.5kg / 40.7 lb).
- **Meals per day:** 6 on training days at the start, cut to 5 from week 9; 5 in late prep.
- **Training vs rest day:** explicit high-day/rest-day carb split throughout; refeeds placed
  on leg days from week 14.

### Week 1 macros (5 March)

- High/training days (4×/week): "Protein: 260g, Fats: 60g, Carbs: 490g"
- Normal/rest days (3×/week): "Protein: 260g, Fats: 60g, Carbs: 360g"

### Week 1 sample high (training) day — verbatim

> M1: 2 Salmon Fillets, 100g Oats with Almond Milk and a drizzle of honey
> M2 (pre workout): 30g Whey, 120g Oats (blended as shake)
> Intra Workout: 30g Highly Branched Cyclic Dextrins, 10g EAA
> M3 (post workout): 40g Whey, 130g Coco Pops
> M4: 150g Chicken, Veggies, 75g Rice, 1tsp E-V Olive Oil
> M5: 150g Chicken, Veggies, 75g Rice, 1tsp E-V Olive Oil
> M6: 150g Chicken, Veggies, 75g Rice, 1tsp E-V Olive Oil

### Week 1 sample rest day — verbatim

> M1: 2 Whole Eggs, 100ml Egg white, 80g Oats with Almond Milk
> M2: 150g Chicken, Veggies, 75g Rice, 1tsp E-V Olive Oil
> M3: 150g Chicken, Veggies, 75g Rice, 1tsp E-V Olive Oil
> M4: 30g Whey, 30g Peanut Butter, 80g Oats
> M5: 150g Lean Lamb Mince, Veggies, 75g Rice

### Macro progression through prep (as published)

- Week 4: high/leg days (×2) 240P/60F/490C; upper/rest days (×5) 240P/60F/275C.
- Week 8: every day 220P/50F/225C ("Protein: 220g (-20g), Fats: 50g (-10g), Carbs: 225g (-50g)").
  Cardio up to 40 min, 6×/week. A birthday meal was accommodated by dropping
  "any additional fats on the day of, and the day after".
- Week 13: 220P/50F/175C.
- Week 14: first refeed, on Friday leg day — "Refeed #1 - Protein: 220g, Fats: 50g,
  Carbs: 340g (+165g)".
- Week 15: steps target raised to 20,000/day (from 10–15k); posing practice 15–30 min/day.
- Week 16: 220P/50F/150C; morning cardio 50 min.
- Week 19 (2 weeks out): 200P/35F/100C; 60 min fasted morning cardio; two higher-carb
  days on leg days at +135g carbs from jasmine rice.

### Week 16 sample day — verbatim

> M1: 140ml egg whites, 1 large egg, 50g smoked salmon, 40g oats
> M2: 133g Chicken, 1 tsp E-V Olive Oil, 38g Rice, Green Veggies
> INTRA: 1 scoop Aminotaur
> PWO: 40g Whey
> M3: 133g Chicken, 1 tsp E-V Olive Oil, 38g Rice, Green Veggies
> M4: 30g Whey, 40g Oats
> M5: 133g Chicken, 1 tsp E-V Olive Oil, 38g Rice, Green Veggies

### Final-week carb-load (no water or sodium manipulation used)

Daily steps from 200P/35F/150C on Wed (11 days out) up to 200P/55F/325C the day before the
show (+25g carb steps; peanut butter reintroduced Tuesday; cardio tapered 50 min → 0).

Carb-loaded Sunday (1 week out) sample day — verbatim:

> M1: 140ml egg whites, 1 large egg, 50g smoked salmon, 60g oats
> M2: 133g Chicken, 1 tsp E-V Olive Oil, 80g Rice, Green Veggies
> During Posing: 1 scoop Aminotaur, 40g Whey
> M3: 133g Chicken, 1 tsp E-V Olive Oil, 80g Rice, Green Veggies
> M4: 30g Whey, 80g Rice
> M5: 133g Chicken, 1 tsp E-V Olive Oil, 60g Rice, Green Veggies

### Show day (30 July, 71.7kg) — verbatim

> 7am: 140ml egg whites, 2 large egg, 50g smoked salmon, 60g oats
> 9.30am: 133g Chicken, 15g Peanut Butter, 80g Rice, Green Veggies
> 12pm: 133g Chicken, 1 tsp E-V Olive Oil, 80g Rice, Green Veggies

Pre-judging (20 min prior): "1 Rice Krispies Treat, 1/4 teaspoon salt, some water".
Between shows (3:30pm): "133g Chicken, 1 tsp E-V Olive Oil, 80g Rice, Green Veggies".
Pre-evening show (20 min prior): "1 Rice Krispies Treat, 1/4 teaspoon salt, some water".
Post-competition: "Steak & chips with a glass of a red and some dark chocolate".

Philosophy quoted: "what built your muscle is what'll keep your muscle"; on late prep,
"the worse you feel, the better you look".

Protein sources: chicken, whey, egg whites/eggs, smoked salmon, salmon fillets, lean lamb
mince. Carb sources: oats, white/jasmine rice, Coco Pops (post-workout), Rice Krispies
treats (show day). Fat sources: extra-virgin olive oil, peanut butter, whole eggs, salmon.

## Document 3 — John Cancea, novice bodybuilder peak week, British Finals qualifier (University of Brighton blog)

- **URL:** https://blogs.brighton.ac.uk/peakweek/
- **Person:** John Cancea (author). Athlete: 20-year-old novice, 83kg, first show of the
  year, "a highly competitive environment where the winner qualifies for the British
  Finals". Federation not named on the page.
- **Context:** Peak week (final 7 days before the show). Published 10 May 2022.
  Blog title: "Nutrition of a peak week for a novice bodybuilder".
- **Meals per day:** 6. Average daily intake across the week: 2,241 kcal (the page notes
  this is ~300 kcal below UK male guidelines, with protein ~80g above recommendations).

### Monday–Wednesday (carb depletion phase) — daily macros 43g C / 186g P / 147g F, 6L water

> Meal 1: 45g dark chocolate (70%), 100g raw chicken, 100g iceberg lettuce
> Meal 2: 42g cashew butter, 100g raw chicken, 100g iceberg lettuce
> Meal 3: 38g olive oil, 100g raw chicken, 100g iceberg lettuce
> Meal 4: 150g raw chicken, 25g olive oil
> Meal 5 & 6: 100g raw chicken, 36g cashew nuts, 100g iceberg lettuce

Meals identical across all three days. Chicken cooked with "3-4 sprays of fry light" or
the prescribed oils. Chicken and rice amounts are given as raw/dry weights.

### Thursday–Friday (carb-load phase) — minimal fat; training limited to light pump work

> Meal 1 & 2: 100g dry rice, 100g raw chicken, 100g iceberg lettuce
> Meal 3: 250g raw potato, 100g raw chicken
> Meal 4: 100g dry rice, 150g raw chicken
> Meal 5 & 6: 100g dry rice, 100g raw chicken

### Saturday (show day)

- "6 litres of water before the first meal", then "four meals of heavily salted chicken".
- With each meal: "4 caps of Peak max by strom sports nutrition" (a diuretic supplement) —
  recorded here exactly as the source states; this is a practice report, not a
  recommendation.
- Backstage: "salt and vinegar crisps and a small portion of sweets such as Haribo",
  minimal water.
- Post-competition rehydration with electrolytes is emphasised "to prevent acute organ
  damage".

## Document 4 — Eric Helms, Team 3DMJ, "The Default Diet" (personal prep low day)

- **URL:** https://www.3dmusclejourney.com/blog/the-default-diet
- **Person/coach:** Eric Helms PhD — coach and Chief Science Officer, 3D Muscle Journey
  (natural bodybuilding coaching organisation); drug-free competitor.
- **Context:** Published 18 March 2025. The "default diet" concept: keep the same eating
  structure across off-season and contest prep, changing only portions and specific foods.
  The day below is Helms' own low day (deep prep intake).
- **Meals per day:** 3 main meals plus a pre-training snack and post-workout shake.

### Helms' low day — verbatim

> Meal 1: Quest bar, Smoothie – 1 cup mixed spinach and kale, 1 cup frozen mixed berries,
> 1 kiwi fruit, 1 scoop of whey, as much water as will not overflow.
>
> Snack – quest bar before training
>
> PWO – 1.5 scoops whey
>
> Meal 2: Omelette – 300g egg whites, 2 whole eggs, laughing cow cheese wedge, spinach,
> mushrooms, tomato, bell pepper OR replace egg whites with can of tuna mixed with 2 whole
> eggs and veggies scrambled up together OR salmon sashimi with cabbage and mixed green salad.
>
> Meal 3: 500g low-fat unsweetened Greek yogurt with sweetener
>
> That puts me right around 50g fat, 100g carbs, 200g of protein and 1600-1700kcals.

### Phase modifications (as published)

- Refeeds: adds carb sources — banana, popcorn, toast, nigiri sushi, honey.
- Off-season: switches "to whole eggs, and tuna in olive oil" and "full fat Greek
  yogurt", and adds "frozen yogurt, gelato, or slow churned ice cream for dessert".
- The fuller two-avatar example diets (≈1,200 kcal low day for smaller athletes,
  ≈1,800 kcal for larger) are referenced but sit in the paid 3DMJ Vault course
  (https://www.3dmjvault.com/courses/default-diet), not in the free article.

## Cross-document observations (for meal library use)

- Meals per day: 5–6 is the norm in UK prep (Documents 1, 2, 3, 5, 6); 3DMJ/Helms runs
  3 meals + 2 shakes/snacks on very low calories.
- Repeated-meal pattern: UK preps lean heavily on one repeated template meal —
  chicken + rice + green veg + measured olive oil (Vaghela ×3 daily; Cancea ×4–6 daily;
  Galton from Meal 2 onward).
- Pre/post-workout: whey + fast carbs post-workout (whey + Coco Pops; Recover Ex;
  whey + oats blended pre-workout). Intra-workout aminos appear in two documents
  (EAA/HBCD; Aminotaur).
- Training vs rest day: handled by carb manipulation only — protein and fat held constant
  (Vaghela explicitly; refeeds placed on leg days).
- UK-specific foods observed in plans: porridge oats, white/jasmine/brown rice, chicken
  breast, smoked salmon, lean lamb mince, ham, cottage cheese, eggs/egg whites, Coco
  Pops, Rice Krispies treats, salt and vinegar crisps and Haribo (show day), punnet of
  grapes, dark chocolate 70%, cashew butter, flax oil, extra-virgin olive oil.
  Notably absent from every plan fetched: Weetabix, Quark, Skyr, Fage, rice cakes —
  do not attribute these to any of the above sources.
- Show-day practice (two documents): salted food + simple sugars shortly before stage
  (Rice Krispies treat + ¼ tsp salt; crisps + Haribo backstage).
