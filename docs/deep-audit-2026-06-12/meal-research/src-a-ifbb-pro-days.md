## Document 1 — Fouad Abiad (off-season / muscle gain), Hosstile

- **URL:** https://hosstile.com/blogs/nutrition/full-day-of-eating-like-a-bodybuilder-with-fouad-abiad
- **Person:** Fouad Abiad, retired IFBB Pro (Men's Open bodybuilding)
- **Division:** Men's Open (IFBB Pro)
- **Context:** Off-season / "to pack on muscle" — article frames the day as eating in a calorie surplus to gain size. Not contest prep.
- **Date:** Published 2020-08-16/17 (page metadata `datePublished: 2020-08-16 16:41:06 -0400`)
- **Fetch method:** direct curl with browser user agent (WebFetch returned 403)

### Complete day of eating (verbatim from article)

> **Upon Rising:**

>
> **Meal 1:**

> - No sugar added ketchup
>
> **Pre-Workout & Intra-Workout Supplements:**
> - 1 scoop of HOSSTILITY pre workout 20 minutes before training
> - 2 scoops INTRA[R3] intra-workout during training
>
> **Meal 3 (post workout meal):**
> - 6 ounces of chicken breast
> - 1 cup of asparagus
>
> **Meal 4:**
> - 6 ounces of steak
> - Frank's Red Hot
> - 95 grams cream of rice
>
> **Meal 5:**
> - 6 ounces of chicken breast
> - 95 grams cream of rice
> - 2 cups of spinach
> - 1 cucumber
> - 1 handful of cherry tomatoes
> - 1 red bell pepper
> - Lemon
> - White vinegar
> - 1 tablespoon of extra virgin olive oil
>
> **Meal 6:**
> - 6 ounces of chicken breast
> - 200 grams cream of rice
> - 2 tablespoons of natural peanut butter

### Structure notes (as stated in the article)

- **Meals per day:** 6, plus an upon-rising amino/hydration drink. Article's general advice: "Eating six meals per day, spacing them out every two to three hours".
- **Pre/post-workout placement:** Meal 2 is explicitly the pre-workout meal; Meal 3 is explicitly the post-workout meal. Fasted cardio in the morning with EAAs. Pre-workout drink 20 minutes before training; intra-workout drink during training.
- **Protein sources:** Greek yoghurt, whey isolate, whole eggs, chicken breast (×3 meals), steak.
- **Carb sources:** granola, Ezekiel (sprouted) bread, cream of rice (×3 meals, 95 g/95 g/200 g). Article also names "white and brown rice, and oatmeal" as recommended sources and advises "a mix of both fast and slow digesting carbohydrates".
- **Fat handling:** small, deliberate additions — 1 tsp butter at the pre-workout meal, 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil in the salad meal, 2 tbsp natural peanut butter in the final meal. Egg yolks in Meal 2.
- **Hydration:** "Aim to consume a minimum of 16 cups (1 gallon) of water, spread out over the course of each day."
- **Training-day vs rest-day:** not stated.

## Document 5 — Derek Lunsford (off-season, 212 era), Fitness Volt

- **URL:** https://fitnessvolt.com/derek-lunsford-off-season-diet/
- **Person:** Derek Lunsford, then reigning 212 Olympia champion
- **Division:** 212 Bodybuilding
- **Context:** **Off-season**, in preparation for the 2022 Olympia. Filmed on a **rest day** (no training). Per-meal targets stated rather than full gram amounts for every item.
- **Date:** Published/updated 2022-06-01 (page metadata `datePublished: 2022-06-01T11:31:51-04:00`)
- **Author:** Doug Murray, Fitness Volt (written breakdown of Lunsford's own YouTube video)

### Complete day of eating (verbatim from article)

Stated per-meal target: "At each and every meal, Lunsford's goal is 50 grams of protein and 75 grams of carbohydrates." (The Meal One heading in the article reads "50 grams of Protein, 60 grams of Carbohydrates".)

> **Meal One — 50 grams of Protein, 60 grams of Carbohydrates**
> Breakfast: egg whites, mushrooms, steak, and potatoes.
> "We're going to make egg whites, with mushrooms and three ounces of Icon Meals streak [steak]. About 200 grams of egg whites, and I'm going to cut up some potatoes for him and air fry it."
> After the meal: two scoops of Evogen Evolog and Evovite, taken "two to three times a day", plus "one to two probiotics" — always with a meal to prevent nausea. He then does a cardio session before finishing the meal.
>
> **Meal Two — 50 grams of Protein, 75 grams of Carbohydrates**
> - Eight ounces of Icon Meals shredded chicken
> - Rice: "Normally I do about six ounces of rice, but we are going to do eight ounces. So, my carbs got pumped up a little bit this week, I was doing 200 grams of carbs a day, moved to about 300 [grams] and then 400 grams of carbs."
>
> **Meal Three — 50 grams of Protein, 75 grams of Carbohydrates**
> - Protein shake (chocolate Evogen as base) with added cream of rice, made with almond milk.
> "The only time I do protein shakes is post-workout, and sometimes I'll have a second shake in the day, but I like to eat whole food. Chicken, beef, salmon, whatever."
>
> **Meal Four — 50 Grams of Protein, 60 Grams of Carbohydrates**
> - "Eight ounces of ground chicken… It's taco seasoning and we have rice to go along with it. This looks to me about eight or nine ounces of rice, like earlier."
>
> **Meal Five — 50 grams of Protein, 80 grams of Carbohydrates**
> - Steak with white rice. "Steak and rice, it's a staple. I think you need to eat at least one red meat meal a day in the off-season, maybe two."
>
> **Meal Six — 50 grams of Protein, 75 grams of Carbohydrates**
> - Shake made with almond milk and two scoops of Evogen Isoject. The article notes he "chose to consume less carbohydrates for his final meal of the day."
>
> Lunsford's own summary: "We did two whole food meals for meals one and two. Did a shake for the third meal. Did the same, did two more whole food meals and a shake before bed."

### Structure notes (as stated in the article)

- **Meals per day:** 6 (on this rest day he says: "Today, I'm not training, I'm resting. So, I'll eat five meals for sure and late tonight I'll eat a sixth meal.").
- **Pre/post-workout placement:** "The only time I do protein shakes is post-workout" — on training days the shake sits post-workout; here shakes were meals three and six (before bed).
- **Protein sources:** egg whites (≈200 g), steak (3 oz at breakfast; steak again at meal five), shredded chicken (8 oz), ground chicken (8 oz), whey (Evogen Isoject, 2 scoops). Red meat once or twice daily in the off-season, by his stated rule.
- **Carb sources:** potatoes (air-fried), white rice (6–9 oz servings), cream of rice (in shake). Weekly carb intake being ramped: 200 g/day → ~300 g → 400 g.
- **Fat handling:** no added fats mentioned; almond milk in shakes.
- **Cheat meals (stated):** "like twice a week I'm having cheat meals… either Sushi or burgers or Persian food."
- **Training-day vs rest-day (stated):** rest day = five meals certain plus a late sixth; shakes shift to non-post-workout slots.

## Cross-document patterns (only what the documents themselves state)

- **Meal frequency:** 5–6 whole-food meals is universal across all seven days (plus shakes/snacks). Abiad's article recommends meals every 2–3 hours; Bumstead (prep) eats roughly every 2 hours after his post-workout meal.
- **Pre/post-workout structure:** where labelled, a solid pre-workout meal sits 1–2 meals into the day and a post-workout feeding follows training (whole-food meal for Abiad/Walker; shake-first-then-meal for Bumstead; shake + fruit for Lunsford in prep).
- **Protein sources across documents:** chicken breast (all 7), white fish/cod (Bumstead prep, Lunsford prep), salmon (Bumstead prep), bison (Lunsford prep, Bumstead off-season), steak/beef (Abiad, Lunsford ×2, Bumstead off-season, Walker, Hadzovic), egg whites/whole eggs (Abiad, Bumstead, Lunsford off-season, Hadzovic), shrimp (Lunsford prep), Greek yoghurt (Abiad), whey isolate shakes (Abiad, Bumstead, Lunsford ×2, Hadzovic).
- **Carb sources:** white/jasmine rice dominates every plan; cream of rice (Abiad, Lunsford off-season, Walker), oats (Bumstead prep, Hadzovic), potatoes (Lunsford ×2, Bumstead off-season), Ezekiel bread (Abiad, Bumstead off-season, Hadzovic), fruit (Bumstead off-season, Lunsford prep, Walker).
- **Fat handling:** consistently the most manipulated lever — slashed to near-elimination in deep prep (Bumstead: 42 g/day, only one egg yolk + salmon), modest in earlier prep (Hadzovic deliberately raised fats slightly; Walker keeps peanut butter/avocado on a high day 5 weeks out), liberal in the off-season (Bumstead: 115 g/day with avocado, olive oil, almond butter).
- **High/low carb cycling in prep:** stated by Lunsford ("Today is a high-carb day… double carbs"), Walker ("high day"), and Bumstead ("a few high carb days recently… right around 300 grams of carbs").
- **Rest-day differences, where stated:** Lunsford (off-season): five meals certain plus a late sixth, shakes move out of the post-workout slot; Bumstead (off-season): skips his usual green shake on rest days; Hadzovic: keeps 5 g creatine on rest days.
