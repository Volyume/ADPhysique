# Requirement note — basic supplement guidance (founder, 2026-06-12)

Founder steer (verbatim intent): "Not sure if it would be for us — worth
researching. We don't want to spam them with info, and we don't want them
spending a fortune, but things like creatine have benefit for everyone. A
section on this might be worth it."

## The founder's framing → design principles
1. **Research first** — including the honest possibility that the answer is
   "keep it minimal" or "don't build it." This is a question, not a mandate.
2. **No spam** — if built, it's a small, calm, optional, educational section;
   never pushy, never recurring nags, never content-marketing filler.
3. **Anti-spend by default** — the guidance should SAVE users money: the short
   list with real evidence (creatine monohydrate is the archetype) and an explicit
   "you don't need the rest." This aligns with the transparent-coach trust moat:
   a coach with nothing to sell who tells you what NOT to buy.
4. **Evidence-based only** — ISSN positions / peer-reviewed consensus, not
   industry claims.

## Hard lines (non-negotiable)
- **Never PED-adjacent.** The old-coach spreadsheet has a PEDs tab; Volyume will
  never touch this territory in any form.
- **ED-safety:** no fat burners, appetite suppressants, diuretics, or any
  weight-loss-marketed product — ever. Section behaviour under ED/wellbeing
  flags must be considered (likely suppressed or neutral).
- **Age:** consider under-18 guidance carefully (the app has age data on the Pro
  path only).
- **No selling, no affiliate links, no brand promotion** (flag any commercial
  option only as a founder PROPOSAL — default is none; trust is the product).
- UK regulatory context (health-claims rules), medical-advice disclaimers
  consistent with the existing CoachOutput credential/disclaimer pattern.
- Deterministic, offline, British English, no AI.

## Open questions for the research
- The short evidence-backed list for the general gym population vs physique
  competitors (creatine, caffeine, vitamin D in a UK winter, omega-3, protein
  powder-as-food; what else clears the bar — and what explicitly does NOT,
  e.g. glutamine, BCAAs alongside adequate protein).
- Eddie-specific: batch-testing / Informed Sport for drug-tested federations;
  timing/dosing detail as progressive disclosure.
- Besa-specific: the "do I actually need anything?" answer, cost framing
  (£/month), and gym-anxiety-safe tone.
- Placement: where it lives (You-tab education row? a row on the generated meal
  plan like the coach spreadsheet's intra-workout line items? methodology-style
  page?), Free vs Pro, and how it stays one-time/reference rather than spam.
- What competitors/coaches do (RP, MacroFactor's abstention, Examine's model)
  and what users praise/complain about.
