# Citation Re-Validation — ext-04, ext-05, ext-07 (2026-06-12)

**Scope:** full adversarial re-verification of every material claim in
`external/ext-04-gamification-habit-science.md`,
`external/ext-05-gym-psychology-coaching-comms.md` and
`external/ext-07-virality-social-wordofmouth.md`, all three of which carry a
PROVENANCE NOTICE (produced in an environment that blocked page fetches; claims
previously unvalidated). Style follows `docs/COACHING_VOICE_CITATION_AUDIT.md`.

**Method:** every load-bearing claim was checked against actually-fetched
primary sources (PubMed/PMC records, journal pages, official company blogs and
press releases, SEC-filed shareholder letters) or, where the publisher blocked
fetching, against at least two independent search-indexed records. Nothing is
marked VERIFIED below without a fetched or search-confirmed primary record;
where the only support is a vendor blog repeating an unsourced number, the
verdict says so. A small number of trivial product-description claims
(e.g. "Habitica is an RPG habit app") were not fetched and are marked
UNVERIFIABLE (trivial) rather than silently passed.

**Verdict vocabulary:**
- **VERIFIED** — fetched source confirms the claim as written (minor wording slack allowed).
- **CORRECTED** — the underlying source/finding is real, but the number, attribution, journal, mechanism or direction in the doc is wrong; the corrected fact is given.
- **UNSUPPORTED** — no reliable source found; the claim circulates only in unsourced tertiary blogs, or the cited source does not contain it.
- **FABRICATED** — the cited source demonstrably does not contain the figure and no source for it exists anywhere that could be found.
- **UNVERIFIABLE** — could not be confirmed or refuted (paywall, vendor-only data, vague attribution); what was tried is stated.

**Headline:** roughly 42% of material claims survived verification as written.
The academic spine of ext-05 and the social-accountability spine of ext-07
(the 83-study meta-analysis, the Strava kudos study, Duolingo Friend Streak,
Matthews/Dominican, social-support overload, BJHP shame study) are **real and
held up almost exactly**. The quantified Duolingo retention folklore in ext-04
(churn 47→28, Streak Freeze −21%, widget +60%, 17.19 vs 11.62 days) **did not
survive**: none of it traces to Duolingo, and one figure is fabricated against
its own citation. ext-05 has a fabric of real papers with a persistent pattern
of wrong numbers, wrong journals and, twice, reversed findings.

### The worst failures (in order of severity)

1. **G8 — "17.19 days vs 11.62 days (+48%) with streak freezes" — FABRICATED.**
   Attributed to a Trophy blog post that does not contain the figures (fetched
   and checked); no source anywhere on the indexed web contains them.
2. **P1 — "~47% of UK adults uncomfortable joining a gym (PureGym 2025/26)" —
   CORRECTED to 12%.** The real report (fetched) says 12% find the gym
   intimidating. The headline number used to justify the gymtimidation thesis
   is nearly 4× the real one.
3. **G13 — "Duolingo churn 47% (2020) → 28% (2024), streaks the single most
   effective lever" — UNSUPPORTED.** Exists only in uncited third-party blogs
   (StriveCloud's version says 28% by *late 2023*, no source); Duolingo has
   never disclosed churn in these terms in any shareholder letter found.
4. **G12 — "iOS widget → +60% commitment" — UNSUPPORTED.** The Smashing
   Magazine (Feb 2026) article cites orizon.co, which states it with no source.
   No Duolingo primary exists.
5. **G7 — "Streak Freeze reduced churn 21% among at-risk users" — UNSUPPORTED.**
   Same unsourced-blog chain; not on the cited Trophy page (fetched).
6. **G18/V27 — PMC10807424 cited for motivation *crowding-out* and for
   "leaderboards harm the least active" — CORRECTED.** The fetched paper
   (Huang, Chen & Zhou, Front. Psychol.) found the **opposite**: financial
   reward and social recognition *crowded in* intrinsic motivation, and it says
   nothing about leaderboards harming less-active users.
7. **P43 — Health Communication citation — CORRECTED.** The cited DOI is Sun,
   Huang & Jiang (2023), a WeRun survey arguing fitness-app comparison of
   *activity* can **improve** body satisfaction — cited in support of the
   opposite claim. (The "female undergraduates shown fitness images" finding is
   Tiggemann & Zaccardo 2015, uncited.)
8. **P14 — "identified regulation is the most stable long-term predictor" —
   CORRECTED (reversed).** Teixeira et al. 2012 (the real 66-study review)
   concludes identified regulation predicts *short-term adoption* and
   **intrinsic motivation predicts long-term adherence**.

## 6. What is new or was missed (to June 2026)

1. **Strava paywalled Year in Sport (Dec 2025) — verified.** ext-07 predicted
   this dynamic before the doc could have known the outcome; the backlash was
   severe and multi-outlet (Slashdot, road.cc), mirroring Garmin's Connect+
   backlash. The "decide the free/Pro line before launch and never move it"
   rule is now one of the best-evidenced recommendations in the corpus.
2. **Fuente-Vidal et al., JMIR mHealth (Jan 2026).** Fitness-app engagement
   falls 69.3% by end of month 1 and 80.6% by month 3; paying users retain
   roughly twice as long (154 vs 81 days); intrinsic motivation and muscle-gain
   goals predict persistence. This sharpens the D0–D30 case for D1/D2 far
   better than the gym-folklore stats the docs leaned on.
3. **Sheen et al., BJHP (Oct 2025) — verified.** Machine-assisted topic
   analysis of ~14k posts: shame at logging, irritation at nagging
   notifications, disappointment at app-set targets → disengagement. Direct
   evidence for the notification budget, autonomy-supportive copy and
   adherence-neutral posture.
4. **Xu et al. 2025 umbrella review (Int J Psychology):** SDT–physical-activity
   association evidence judged *inconclusive* across systematic reviews. SDT
   remains a sound design language; stop presenting it as settled empirical
   law in internal docs.
5. **Duolingo scale update:** DAU surpassed 50M (Q3 2025 press release); the
   streak-share disclosure remains "~70% of DAU >7-day streak" (Q3 FY22/FY23
   letters). Friend Streak remains the only published, quantified shared-streak
   mechanic (22% daily-completion lift).
6. **PureGym UK Fitness Report 2025/26:** gymtimidation 12% overall but
   *rising* (+4pts), women 17% vs men 5% and widening, women 25–34 worst.
   The Besa thesis survives at honest magnitude — the barrier is concentrated
   exactly in Volyume's beginner persona, even though the headline prevalence
   in ext-05 was wrong.
7. **The 2026 PTM gymtimidation paper and the 2026 "From immersion to burnout"
   paper both exist** — the corpus's instinct (streak/gamification pressure is
   a real psychological hazard to design around) is supported, just not by the
   specific mechanisms it invented.
8. **Hevy passed 10M users (App Store: "Join +10 million users!")**, still
   organic-led — the artefact-virality benchmark in ext-07 is, if anything,
   stronger than when the doc was written.

## 7. Delta verdicts — the five features built on these docs

### (a) D2 — programme-arc strip + phase-completion celebration + surfaced streak repair — **KEEP**

None of the broken claims is load-bearing here. What actually supports D2 is
all verified: Lally 2010 (missing one day does not derail habit formation —
the exact rationale for making the silent repair visible), the
abstinence-violation/perfectionism literature reflected in P23/G15, Sheen 2025
(shame and silent failure drive abandonment), Fuente-Vidal 2026 (the cliff is
months 1–3 — a visible "Week 3 of 10" arc targets it directly), and the
verified Wrapped/YiS evidence that narrative arcs with endings are the
artefacts people value. The phase-completion celebration inherits the verified
Wrapped mechanics (V28, G23). **Action:** none structural. Re-point the
streak-repair rationale at Lally + Sheen instead of any Duolingo repair
economics (which were never verified).

### (b) D1 — milestone ladder (4/12/26/52 weekly-streak milestones, early-win emphasis) — **KEEP (re-base the evidence)**

The ladder's stated justification leaned partly on Trophy's "33.42% vs 20.46%
day-1 achievement retention" — that is the vendor's own platform data
(CORRECTED, G19) and must not appear in any internal doc or pitch as industry
evidence. The feature survives without it: early mastery experiences are the
best-verified self-efficacy lever (Bandura, canonical; Stojanovic 2021 fetched
— self-efficacy↔automaticity is a measured positive feedback loop), Lally puts
median habit automaticity at ~66 days (validating the 4- and 12-week rungs as
the critical span), and the weekly (not daily) cadence is exactly what the
verified harm literature (G15, P38) and watchOS 11's rest-day retrofit (G28)
point to. The 26/52 rungs are cheap and harmless. **Action:** strip the Trophy
stat and the "80%+ imposter syndrome" line (G38) from any copy/justification;
replace with PureGym 2025/26 verified figures if a gymtimidation hook is
needed.

### (c) Partner system rebuild (PartnerScreen, derived-signals-only, cheer + shared-streak pushes, Progress tile + Consistency row + WorkoutSummary beat) — **KEEP**

This is the best-evidenced feature in the entire programme. Its four pillars
all verified exactly: the 83-study meta-analysis (V24 — comparison is the harm
mechanism, so removing the comparator is the right architecture); Franken 2023
kudos study (V13 — acknowledgement within reciprocal ties lifts real activity;
upward comparison does not → one-tap reciprocal cheer, no metrics); Duolingo
Friend Streak (V21 — 22% daily-completion lift, monotonic in partners; the
only quantified shared-streak mechanic in the market); Matthews (V22 — a known
person seeing weekly progress: 76% vs 43%). The support-overload paper (P28,
verified) independently validates the blueprint's own choices: pushes
opt-in/budget-capped, support invoked not ambient, the never-breaking shared
streak (vs Duolingo's punitive break-and-unpair). **Action:** two wording
fixes only — (1) ban the "+D14 retention" shorthand anywhere it survives
(Duolingo published no D14 number); (2) phrase Matthews as "+33 percentage
points (76% vs 43%)", not "95%" (the Future.co figure is marketing-grade and
the corpus has already self-corrected on it — keep it dead).

### (d) Notification budget + ghost-prevention (missed check-in) — **KEEP (one amendment)**

Strongly supported by verified evidence: Sheen 2025 (notification irritation
and shame are documented abandonment drivers — the budget is the defence),
support-overload 2025 (P28), autonomy-supportive coaching evidence (Amorose
2007 verified; Zhang 2025 verified; controlling language harms), and Lally
(one missed opportunity is recoverable — the entire basis for
"pick up where you left off" framing over "you broke it"). The
ghost-prevention concept matches the verified BJHP finding that silent
disengagement follows shame. **Amendment:** the supporting docs cite
unverified Duolingo notification folklore (cadence-by-streak-length, G11) and
coaching-industry vendor stats (ext-06's "95% goal-achievement when committing
to someone" — UNSUPPORTED). Remove those props; cap-and-tone decisions should
cite Sheen 2025 + Li 2025. No design change required — the built design is
already more conservative than the bad citations would have licensed.

### (e) Free share artefacts strategy (PR/milestone/recap cards as virality engine) — **AMEND**

Direction confirmed, projected magnitude not. What held: Wrapped 2020 drove a
measured +21% weekly download lift (V28) and Wrapped 2025 hit 200M engaged in
~24h with ~500M shares (G23); Hevy reached 2M downloads on $15k and now 10M+
users substantially via shared artefacts and routine links (V4); the act of
posting, not the likes received, carries the identity value (V1, corrected to
Liu 2021 — this validates share cards even for users with tiny audiences); and
Strava's December 2025 paywall backlash (V9) hard-confirms the rule already
adopted in founder decisions: **all share artefacts free, decided before
launch, never re-gated.** What failed: the quantified conversion chain — 86%
YiS view-through (UNSUPPORTED), 25% January re-install spike (UNSUPPORTED),
30% vs 5% deep-link install rates (vendor, unsourced), Boostcamp year-in-review
precedent (not at the citation), viral coefficient ≈1 (rhetoric). **Amend as
follows:** (1) keep building PR/milestone/block/annual cards — cost is low and
the verified upside is real; (2) reset internal expectations: treat artefacts
as a compounding brand/acquisition surface, not a quantified "engine" — no
forecast should inherit the dead numbers; (3) instrument it ourselves (share
events → store-listing visits) rather than borrowing fabricated benchmarks;
(4) the programme/plan share link (ext-07 Rank 2) survives on Hevy's verified
mechanic but its 30%-install claim must be dropped from any business case.

## 8. Pattern notes

1. **The unsourced-blog laundering chain is the dominant failure mode** in
   ext-04: a number is invented or garbled on a growth-marketing blog
   (orizon.co, StriveCloud, trypropel), gets repeated by a more reputable
   outlet (Smashing Magazine, Feb 2026), and arrives in the research doc
   wearing borrowed credibility. Every Duolingo retention number that did not
   come from Duolingo's blog, shareholder letters or a named PM on the record
   failed verification.
2. **ext-05's failure mode is subtler and more dangerous:** real papers, fetched
   and confirmed to exist, cited for numbers they don't contain (54%/58.3%;
   ≤0.5% vs 0.5–1%; d=0.31 pinned to a pilot RCT) or for findings they reverse
   (crowding-in cited as crowding-out; a body-satisfaction-*improvement* paper
   cited for harm; identified-vs-intrinsic regulation swapped). Every one of
   these would have passed a does-the-DOI-resolve check. Only reading the
   abstract catches them.
3. **The social-accountability evidence base is clean.** Everything NEW-002 §0
   claimed to have verified (83-study meta, kudos study, Friend Streak,
   Matthews, Apple mechanics) re-verified exactly, despite being produced
   under degraded tooling. The partner rebuild rests on the most solid ground
   in the corpus.
4. **Verified harm literature consistently outperforms the verified
   gamification-win literature.** The strongest fetched evidence in all three
   docs concerns what *hurts* (comparison, shame, notification nagging,
   support overload, streak pressure). Volyume's restraint-first design
   choices are better supported than its growth-mechanic choices — which is
   the right way round for a product with an ED safety system.
