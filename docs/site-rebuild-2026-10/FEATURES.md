Corrections to FACTS.md section 3 found on this pass
1. **Pre-workout and post-workout meal slots are NOT reachable.** FACTS 3.4 lists them. They exist only if `userProfile.mealPlanPeriWorkout` is true, and no screen sets it (`src/lib/food/mealSlots.js:7-8,135`; grep of screens/components finds readers only). Do not advertise them.
2. **The lock-screen rest countdown with +15s and Skip buttons is Android only.** FACTS 3.1 states it without a platform. `presentRestTimerNotification`, `dismissRestTimerNotification` and the whole foreground-service path return immediately off Android (`src/lib/notifications/activeWorkout.js:266-269,296-300`; `src/lib/notifications/restForeground.js:52,98,151,169`). iPhone gets the end-of-rest alert only (`src/lib/notifications/restEnd.js:41`, "Fires on BOTH platforms"). The iPhone Live Activity is parked (`src/lib/__tests__/liveActivity.wiring.test.js:1-26`).
3. **Lifting weights are kg only.** FACTS 3.10 says "weight units kg, stone or pounds"; that is body weight only. Settings > Units says "Gym weights stay in kg" (`src/screens/SettingsUnitsScreen.js:55`, `:17`); the store pins `units: 'kg'` (`src/store/useAppStore.js:2250`).
4. The in-app name for the photo progress score is **Volyume Score** (`src/screens/AthleteProfileScreen.js:474`, `src/components/ProgressScanHistoryCard.js:189`). FACTS used "progress score".
5. There is no cardio logging (no running, cycling or rowing-machine entries; the library is strength, bodyweight, carries and timed holds) and no exercise demonstration media (no `exercise-demos` reader in `src/`). Do not imply either.

## Wording cautions (features that may be named, with limits)

C1. **Body weight is never the hook** (CLAIMS-STANDARDS section 5, ban on foregrounding the scale). Body metrics, the Today weigh-in box, the athlete-profile weight tile and weigh-ins in the check-in may appear only in passing as "one of several things the coach reads". Do not make weight-tracking a headline.
C2. **Volyume Score**: say "a score from your own photos, with a confidence level, worked out on your phone, which can decline to score". Not a body-fat measurement, not medical. Keep the in-app band label "Leanness" off the page. Photos as a feature are allowed; before/after body imagery in ads is not.
C3. **Injuries & limitations**: say only "your plan is built around how you train". Name no population, condition or diagnosis, and use no medical framing (marketing readiness matrix is all NO, CLAIMS-STANDARDS 9A).
C4. **Weekly decision**: "by default, your coach suggests and you tap to apply". Coached mode applies changes on opening the decision (safety holds still wait). Do not write "nothing changes until you tap".
C5. **Meal builder / dietary needs**: no allergy-safety claim. Allergens are a filter on suggestions, not a guarantee.
C6. **Set videos privacy**: use the founder's line only, "private on your device only, unless you choose to share them" (`WhatsNewSheet.js:160-163`).
C7. **Recovery** is an estimate; always say "estimated", never "measures".
C8. **Cycle tracking**: optional and shown only for a female profile; Article 9 data. Mention only as an optional setting if at all.
C9. **Nutrition targets**: say "worked out from your details and goal, with the reasoning shown". Give no floor figures and no "safe" claim.
C10. **Community auto-posting**: if described, say each finished session can be posted after you join, you choose who sees it before your profile is created, and you can turn it off. Never "private by default". Do not claim Community is moderated beyond reporting and blocking.
C11. **Lifting units**: gym weights are kg only. Do not imply pounds for lifting; do not target imperial-lifting audiences.
C12. **Coach handover report**: say "for your coach". The in-app line adds "or GP"; leave the clinical framing off the page.
C13. **Foods**: "over 29,000 foods, built around UK products", offline once the first-launch import has finished. The Open Food Facts part is a dated snapshot (2026-07-05); do not say "always up to date".
C14. **Vitamins and minerals** are shown where the food data has them (display only, never read by the coach). Do not say "micronutrient tracking".
C15. **Gym challenge**: advertise only if the founder confirms one is running. On 2026-10-20 production held none (`supabase/migrate_203_free_for_life_on_completion.sql` header). Never put "free for life" on the homepage as a general promise.
C16. **Platform words**: lock-screen rest countdown, widgets, "Set as wallpaper", "Make rest alerts exact" are Android only. Google sign-in is Android only until the iOS client id is filled (re-checked at `ae60488`: still empty, button still hidden on iPhone, placeholder URL scheme removed from `app.json`). Apple sign-in is on both. Do not say "widgets" or "live countdown" for iPhone.
C17. **No training-app claims the code does not support**: no cardio logging, no demonstration videos, no chat assistant, no Apple Health or Health Connect link, no restaurant or takeaway database, no photograph-a-meal logging.
