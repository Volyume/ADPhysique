## Headline findings

1. NO-AI IS MARKETABLE. Duolingo's "AI-first" pivot cost it 400k+ TikTok
   followers in weeks (May 2025, well-corroborated) and its energy system
   drew guilt-mechanic backlash. Industry commentary: "AI became a business
   model before it became a benefit." MacroFactor already markets "real
   nutrition data, not LLM guessing" as a trust signal. Hevy's "AI" is
   rules-based auto-progression; Fitbod's "AI" ignores real-time wearable
   data. A deterministic, explainable, falsifiable claim ("same inputs, same
   output, no LLM, no randomness") is a position AI-branded competitors
   cannot copy without abandoning their branding.
2. THE BARCODE PAYWALL IS STILL THE CATEGORY'S MOST RESENTED DECISION.
   MyFitnessPal doubled down on AI tiers (Premium+ $24.99/mo, Cal AI
   acquisition, Meal Scan) while the 2022 barcode paywall still drives
   switching. "Barcode scanning, always free" is direct ASO ammunition.
3. UK FOOD DATA IS WEAK EVEN AT THE TOP. Cronometer's own docs admit UK
   branded coverage lags the US despite the Nutritionix integration; its UK
   anchor is ~750 McCance & Widdowson foods. A UK-first app can own this.
4. TRIAL LENGTH: RevenueCat 2026 (primary): 17-32 day trials convert 42.5%
   median vs 25.5% for <=4-day; the industry is nevertheless moving to short
   trials. Volyume's 14-day cardless trial is in the better-converting band.
   Health & Fitness leads all categories on realised LTV ($35.64/payer at 1
   year) and trial-to-paid (37.7%). Western Europe LTV comparable to NA.
5. WEAR OS 7 (Google I/O, May 2026, primary): new standardised Wear Widgets
   (2x1/2x2, Jetpack Glance) + a standardised Workout Tracker experience.
   Early widget partners named are Spotify/WhatsApp/Peloton/Todoist — NO
   lifting logger has claimed the format yet (first-mover gap). Counter-
   weight: watchOS holds ~54% European smartwatch share and dominates the UK,
   so phone widgets reach more UK Android users than Wear OS does.
6. CALM DESIGN IS A RETENTION STRATEGY, NOT JUST ETHICS. Guilt streaks are a
   recognised failure mode; graceful-exit design correlates with better
   long-term retention (directional; specific stats unverified). Peer-
   reviewed 2026 work (JMIR, primary) maps ED-app design pitfalls and calls
   for certification that does not yet exist — Volyume's hard floors are
   ahead of where the field says the market should be.
7. GDPR TIGHTENING (2026-2027): stricter Article 9 application expected;
   Germany's DiGA reform signals more EU health-app scrutiny. EU-Dublin
   residency + fail-closed consent is a widening moat worth surfacing in
   store data-safety copy.
8. FREE-TIER GENEROSITY SCALES: Boostcamp (11,000+ programs free, logger
   free) proves the model Volyume already runs; its shareable "year in
   review" stats mechanic is a low-risk growth feature precedent.
9. DISTRIBUTION: long-tail ASO ("powerlifting log", "workout logger no
   ads") + Apple Custom Product Pages (now 70/app) targeting query-specific
   screenshot sets; nano/micro UK coach partnerships out-convert macro
   influencers on CPA (directional); transparent founder posts outperform
   promotional posts on Reddit (general pattern, not fitness-verified).
10. PREMIUM COACH-BRAND APPS (RP $225-300/yr, JuggernautAI $350/yr) prove a
    segment pays for authority-led coaching — an argument for UK coach
    partnerships rather than an AI arms race.

## Competitor snapshot (mid-2026, prices as reported)

- Hevy: $2.99/mo, $23.99/yr, $74.99 lifetime; Hevy Trainer auto-progression
  (Feb 2026) + HevyGPT plan builder. Free tier thinner than Volyume's.
- Strong: $4.99/mo / $29.99/yr; Apple Watch live sync (v6.2.1 Mar 2026);
  progress charts paywalled.
- MacroFactor: $5.99-11.99/mo; DB-backed photo logging (Apr 2025), no price
  rise since launch; anti-LLM trust positioning.
- MyFitnessPal: Free / $19.99mo Premium / $24.99mo Premium+; Cal AI
  acquisition Mar 2026; barcode still paywalled.
- Cronometer: UK coverage improved via Nutritionix, still lags US.
- Boostcamp: programs free; Pro = extra coach programs only.
- Fitbod: 4.81 stars across 272k US ratings; Apple-ecosystem-led.
- RP Hypertrophy / JuggernautAI: $225-350/yr authority-led.
- KeyLifts: durable 5/3/1 niche utility, regular updates.
- Alpha Progression: AI-adjusted plans, multi-gym flexibility.
