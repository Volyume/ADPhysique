# Communities revamp (2026-09-10, founder prompt)

Founder brief (in chat, 2026-09-10), verbatim substance:
- Community was spec'd wrong from the start: it assumed sharing of
  user-created plans and routines. Volyume already builds individualised
  plans (coaching system, plan builder, plan library), so plan sharing is
  the wrong foundation and stays removed.
- The purpose is CONNECTING: people at your gym, people in your age group,
  people who train the same way or compete in the same discipline (for
  example men's physique athletes), and any similar dimension. You can see
  people in your category or gym: how often they have trained, whether they
  have had a PR, whether they are progressing.
- Friends: create groups with friends, see and encourage them; inside a
  group people can share a great workout they have done.
- Investigate first; do not build from the founder's word alone. Deep
  research through the lowest adequate agent tier per task (Haiku or
  Sonnet; never Fable or Opus for what a lower tier can do).
- Completely revamp the presentation: slicker, no stacked cards, an elite
  community service that is a differentiator, and one product with the
  rest of Volyume. Audit look and feel: a static orange button appeared in
  Community although earlier tasks removed them (D148: colour is hierarchy).
- Look at how the best community products do it: not only gym or workout
  apps, the best of the best communities anywhere.
- Token preservation on every task. Founder addition (chat, same day):
  Fable does the design ruling and the integration judgement itself;
  big reads and research go to the lowest adequate tier.
- Founder addition (chat, same day): research the LOOK AND FEEL and
  ARRANGEMENT of the best community products as much as their features.
  The current surface "does not look elite": text too big in places,
  things stacked together too much. Slick is the aim.
- Founder addition (chat, same day): the app must never explain
  Community as programme or routine sharing anywhere. Residue was seen
  on the routines tab ("share programmes") and the Train page (a link
  saying Community is for programmes). Audit every explanation site.
