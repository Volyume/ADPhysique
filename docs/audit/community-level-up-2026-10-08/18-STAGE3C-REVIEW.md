## Checked against the brief, nothing to report

1. Safety. `announceTraining(true)` refuses with no profile, a minor, the switch off, or the consistency gate closed (calm mode, open ED flag), in that order; a clear is sent when this device raised the marker even if the switch went off since. `logFinishedSessionToChallenges` applies the same gate and sends only the key and the day. `publishAmbientItems` returns before the milestone for sharing off, gated, and no profile (the summary's own early exit), and the milestone takes the session item's already-gated audience (minor forced to followers, unreadable `me` treated as a minor). PresenceBand defaults `gated = true` and both screens fail closed until the gate answers; the Challenge band is withheld under `gated`. Bands show sessions and counts only; challenge rows show "N sessions" only.
2. Privacy panel (D212). The consistency and age-group switches and "Show when I am training" are hidden for a minor; a minor's audience is text only, no chips. No setter fires without a tap. `saveShowTrainingNow` writes the mirror only after the server accepted; `changeShowTraining` and `changeBand` revert on failure; `saveBandToggle` reverts on `rules_outdated` and is the Training profile's own logic moved unchanged.
3. Finish with no workout id: `logFinishedSessionToChallenges` returns early on a falsy key; all three logger calls are `.catch(() => {})` and never block the finish.
4. Delete permissions: author or admin only, with the house destructive confirm; report reaches the ReportSheet. The visible "..." under the bubble gives screen-reader users the same action as long-press.
5. Composer: the group ceiling of 500 is passed through; the counter appears from 450.
6. Date maths: `addLocalCalendarDays` uses `setDate`, so DST is safe; every choice is inside the server's 31-day cap even with F3.
7. Law: no amber on PresenceBand or ChallengeSheet (guard pinned at 0); colours and type from tokens; band row `minHeight: touchTarget.minimum`; chips carry `radio` roles inside `radiogroup`; SectionHeader carries the header role.
8. Copy: British English throughout, no em dash, no banned words; every new line is calm and plain ("It ends for everyone in the group. Nobody loses their sessions.", "Some of that wording is not allowed in Community. Please reword it.").
