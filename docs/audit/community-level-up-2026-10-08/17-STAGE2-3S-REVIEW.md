## Appendix: checked and found sound

Server, migrate_191 (read in full):
- Calm and ED gate. `_community_training_now` returns NULL for a withheld viewer and filters each listed person through `_community_consistency_withheld` (migrate_180 helper reused, not re-implemented). `community_hub_summary` and `community_group_get` call it only for members; a non-member or gated viewer gets JSON null. `community_challenge_board` returns NULL for a gated caller and drops gated members from the rows and from `group_total`. The recipients RPC drops gated members. A gated person's figures never reach anyone else.
- Minors. `community_set_show_training_now` refuses (`forbidden`). The readers require `is_minor = false` (presence, board). Chat and challenges are members only, and groups exclude minors, so nothing reaches a minor beyond what group pages already allow.
- Chat. Members only (`_community_group_role` is `state = 'member'` only: migrate_165:736, so an invited or requested row cannot read or send). 500 characters, checked in the function and by a CHECK. `_community_clean_text` is the DM filter. The rate rail is 20/60 an hour, with `_community_require_rules`. Blocked (either way) and muted authors are hidden on read and in the unread count. Delete by the author or an admin; anyone else gets not_found.
- The recipients RPC excludes the sender, non-active profiles, blocks both ways and muters. EXECUTE is revoked from PUBLIC, anon and authenticated and granted to service_role. Acceptance asserts it, and the harness C7 proves `42501`.
- No weight, calorie, bodyweight or measurement anywhere. The acceptance block pins the exact column list of the three new tables. Board payload: counts, handle, display name, avatar.
- Additive and idempotent. The header carries purpose, status UNAPPLIED, safe to re-run and rollback. Part 0 pre-flight. The re-issued bodies differ only by marked additions (spot-checked group_get and list_mine).

Edge:
- community-notify fan-out. The author must match `actorId`. The message must be under 10 minutes old. Recipients are re-filtered by UUID and capped at 200. Per recipient: the `community_message` toggle (a read error skips), quiet hours, the ED flag (fails closed), and the 15-minute collapse while unread. The body is "New messages in {group}", with no content and no handle. `group_message` is in `MUTE_SILENCED_KINDS`. The comment push's `post_id` is now the comment's target post (the Stage 1 SF1 follow-through, verified at the comment branch).
