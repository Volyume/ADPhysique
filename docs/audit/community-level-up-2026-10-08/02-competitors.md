## 1. Per-app sections

Fixed order in each: Where / Feed / Graph / Groups and challenges / Messaging / Profile / Notifications / Look and feel / Safety / Have-that-we-lack / Noise.

### 1.1 Hevy (closest rival; 17M+ athletes)
- **Where:** social is the default landing tab (a logbook-first complaint, https://prpath.app/blog/strong-vs-hevy-2026.html). Feed sits beside logging; workouts post on finish. P:10.
- **Feed:** Home (followed) and Discover (others) tabs; post = title, duration, volume, PR count, photos, likes, comments with replies. Ranking of Discover not documented (UNVERIFIED). https://www.hevyapp.com/features/discovery-feed/ ; https://www.hevyapp.com/features/content-feed/
- **Graph:** one-way follow, mutual indicator, contact import and invite links, suggested-athletes carousel (hide toggle exists). Default public profile, request/accept if private. https://www.hevyapp.com/features/social-features/ ; https://www.hevyapp.com/help/how-to-make-a-profile-private/
- **Groups/challenges:** none. Leaderboards vs people you follow across up to 38 exercises; 1:1 comparison with a named user. https://www.hevyapp.com/features/gym-leaderboard/ ; https://www.hevyapp.com/features/workout-comparison/ . Gym tagging announced for later (July 2026 link above).
- **Messaging:** none found.
- **Profile:** workout count, followers, 3-month activity graph, saved routines, media. https://www.hevyapp.com/features/user-profiles/
- **Notifications:** live in-workout PR notice (https://www.hevyapp.com/features/live-pr/); per-person workout notifications opt-in; monthly achievement celebrations.
- **Look and feel:** July 2026 native rebuild, Liquid Glass and Material 3, refined logging animations (July 2026 link). Share cards in light/dark/transparent (https://www.hevyapp.com/features/shareable/). Screenshot-level density UNVERIFIED.
- **Safety:** block/report per profile; default-public; photo/measurement persistence leak noted in P:10 (UNVERIFIED primary source).
- **Has that we lack (check against Volyume build before acting):** hevy.com no-install routine preview; Discover feed as a separate tab; two-tier share (plan without weights vs full log); transparent-background share cards.
- **Noise:** social as forced default tab; suggested-athletes carousel; Strava auto-forward duplication.

### 1.2 Strong
- **Where/feed/graph/groups/messaging/profile:** no feed, no follow graph, no groups; sharing is a one-off share sheet for workouts and routines. 2026 comparison pieces call social "minimal" (https://setgraph.app/articles/best-strong-app-alternatives-(2025), competitor marketing, treat with caution). P:10.
- **Look and feel:** the reference for restraint, a pure logger; no social chrome (UNVERIFIED visually).
- **Has that we lack:** nothing social. Its lesson is that a large user base stays without a feed: social must be optional and quiet.
- **Noise:** n/a.

### 1.3 JEFIT
- **Where:** "Discover" tab holds feed, groups, challenges; Play listing advertises posting progress, gym milestones and PBs (https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=je.fit).
- **Feed:** "Motivational Feed" (https://www.jefit.com/download/motivation); progress-picture voting inside challenges.
- **Graph:** friends, groups, gym-based community (P:10).
- **Groups/challenges:** seasonal quarterly group challenges, points for logging, sharing progress pictures for votes, inviting friends; top teams win a year of Elite (https://www.jefit.com/blog/how-to-join-group-challenges ; undated post). "My JEFIT Group" chat from Discover (https://www.jefit.com/blog/group-chats-are-the-best-tool-for-learning-something-new).
- **Messaging:** group chat; DMs UNVERIFIED.
- **Profile:** "Companions" collectible feature from Year in Review campaign (https://www.jefit.com/wp/jefit-news-product-updates/jefit-year-in-review-2025-a-look-back-at-your-strength-journey/).
- **Safety:** photo-vote mechanic is the anti-pattern for body-image safety.
- **Has that we lack:** seasonal cadence of challenges; points for logging; group chat tied to a group.
- **Noise:** photo-voting; prize-for-invites referral loops; ad-heavy UI (P:10).

### 1.4 Strava (benchmark for graph and clubs)
- **Where:** Feed tab plus Groups tab (clubs, challenges, events) plus Progress tab. Strength now first-class (May 2026, link in section 0) so the logger and the feed are one product.
- **Feed:** each activity is a card; kudos (one tap), comments; "kudos bomb" shake on group activities; indiscriminate kudos viewed as etiquette failure. https://communityhub.strava.com/insider-journal-9/10-ways-to-get-more-kudos-1573 ; https://roadmancycling.com/podcast/ep-2174-the-etiquette-of-strava . Feed ordering is partly algorithmic (UNVERIFIED current).
- **Graph:** follow with approve for private; contacts; clubs. P:11.
- **Groups/challenges/events:** clubs with owner/admin roles, join approval, weekly leaderboards (top-10 for clubs of 500 or fewer), Events tab, organiser hub, waivers; invite-only group challenges (up to 24 friends; Most Activity, Fastest Effort, Longest Single Activity); Local Legends (most efforts in 90 days, rewards frequency not speed). https://endurance.biz/2026/industry-news/strava-targets-leaderboard-accuracy-and-rolls-out-navigation-and-club-event-updates/ ; https://support.strava.com/hc/en-us/articles/216918347-Clubs-on-Strava ; https://support.strava.com/en-us/articles/15401751-local-legends ; https://www.coachmag.co.uk/fitness-apps/8942/strava-group-challenges
- **Messaging:** group-chat-style club posts; DMs not core (UNVERIFIED current).
- **Profile:** activity totals, clubs, trophies, Weekly Streak Stickers (2026).
- **Notifications:** kudos/comments/follow; club activity.
- **Look and feel:** activity-as-card with map or media hero, big numerals, orange accent; strength shares via five dedicated formats (not seen; UNVERIFIED).
- **Safety:** privacy zones are opt-in; 2018 heatmap leak; leaderboard integrity needs active cleaning (3.9M activities removed, endurance.biz link above). Public-by-default activity visibility.
- **Has that we lack:** clubs with organiser tooling and events; frequency-based "Local Legend" style recognition; weekly streak stickers as lightweight share objects; strength share formats.
- **Noise:** segment/route/map machinery; speed-based KOMs; paid tier gating of social.

### 1.5 Fitbod

### 1.6 Boostcamp
- Community tab with feed of completed workouts and followed friends (P:12; no new 2026 source found). Programme ratings attach to the programme, not a profile.

### 1.7 Ladder
- **Where:** the team IS the product: quiz, join one of about 23 coach-led teams, new weekly plan (https://www.sensai.fit/blog/ladder-app-review-2026), team chatroom with coach and members, coach video replies (https://apps.apple.com/app/id1502936453).
- **Graph:** team membership only, no follow graph. Cold start solved by being placed into a populated team on day one.
- **Messaging:** team chat; paid tiers add 1:1.
- **Safety:** coach moderation; AI sentiment tooling for coaches (Fortune, Dec 2024, https://www.fortune.com/2024/12/10/strength-training-ladder-app-users-growth) is out of bounds for Volyume.
- **Has that we lack:** day-one membership in a populated group; chat anchored to the week's plan.
- **Noise:** paid-coach funnel; AI.

### 1.8 Garmin Connect
- Redesign with daily snapshots, calendar, leaderboards and newsfeed; weekly step challenges against similar users; group challenges (6 types); contacts/Facebook/Google search to connect (https://www.wareable.com/garmin/garmin-updates-its-connect-mobile-app-1851 ; https://www.garmin.com/en-US/blog/general/connect-3-7-release/). No 2026 change found. P:11 (follower-system rebuild reported mid-rollout in September; not re-verified).
- **Has that we lack:** auto-matched weekly challenge vs similar users (needs care: matching on body data is off-limits for Volyume).
- **Noise:** stale-sync dropping from ranked view (P:10).

### 1.9 WHOOP
- **Where:** Community tab lists Teams; opening a team shows chat first then swipe to leaderboard; chat only on member-created teams, admins can disable; leaderboards on strain, recovery, sleep (https://www.whoop.com/thelocker/community-feature-app-team-chat/ , 2021 post, current state UNVERIFIED).
- **Graph:** Teams only; users ask for follow (link in section 0).
- **Safety:** recovery and sleep leaderboards rank body-state data; comparable to what Volyume must avoid for weight/food.
- **Has that we lack:** chat and leaderboard as two panes of one team object.
- **Noise:** physiological-score ranking.

### 1.10 Peloton
- **Where:** Community tab with Here Now row; in-class leaderboard.
- **Feed/graph:** Tags (up to 10 self-declared identity tags, filter leaderboard by tag) https://support.onepeloton.com/s/article/Peloton-Software-Understanding-Peloton-Tags ; High Five, rate-limited to one per minute, out-of-class version via Here Now (links in section 0 and P:10 doc).
- **Safety:** harassment via repeated High Fives from strangers documented (https://www.yahoo.com/lifestyle/high-five-creeps-peloton-stopped-102700958.html); leaderboard shows real-time output ranking.
- **Has that we lack:** self-chosen identity tags; "who is training now" presence; milestone prompts (25 workouts, 10-day streak) nudging a friend to react.
- **Noise:** live rank pressure.

### 1.11 MyFitnessPal
- Forums, groups, Challenges board (monthly running challenge, 100-day daily weigh-in challenge running to September 2026), friend news feed that long-time users say they miss (https://community.myfitnesspal.com/en/categories/challenges ; https://community.myfitnesspal.com/en/groups). Friends can view diaries (UNVERIFIED current).
- **Safety:** daily weigh-in and calorie challenges are the exact pattern Volyume must not build (ED-safety).
- **Has that we lack:** topic forums with a long searchable archive.
- **Noise:** weigh-in challenges, diary sharing, forum-as-separate-app feel.

### 1.12 Gymshark Training
- Community is mostly outside the app: Gymshark66 progress posted to Instagram/TikTok with hashtag; entrants keep accounts public (https://support.gymshark.com/en/articles/13224296-gymshark66). In-app "trending in the community" and shared plans per site copy (https://training.gymshark.com); an in-app Feed/PBs/Challenges tab seen online is a designer's concept, NOT a shipped feature.
- **Has that we lack:** a brand-wide habit challenge with a fixed start date that drives a whole-user-base moment.
- **Noise:** hashtag/public-account entry rules (privacy and moderation hazard).

### 1.13 Fitbit / Google Health
- 2026 contraction (section 0): social stripped to friends plus a weekly leaderboard; badges deleted; groups, DMs, feeds, usernames and avatars gone; children cannot have friends.
- **Lesson:** a big platform judged a broad social surface not worth moderating and kept only friends plus one leaderboard. Evidence that small and focused can be a deliberate end state.

### 1.14 Caliber
- Free "Workout Groups": private groups where workouts and PBs are shared (https://apps.apple.com/ID/app/id1482405410); coaching tier has chat and weekly async video check-in (P:12). No 2026 change found.
- **Has that we lack:** weekly structured check-in; private small groups free.

### 1.15 Extras
- **Tonal:** public profile, follow, feed of friends' workouts, reactions, per-field privacy toggles (name, location, streak, Strength Score, achievements, leaderboard status), see which programmes friends joined (https://tonal.com/blogs/all/tonal-social-features/). The field-by-field "what to show" panel is the cleanest privacy UI found.
- **Nike Run Club:** async Cheers plus live audio cheers (P:10 doc, Facebook post link there). Zwift: Ride On with club limits (P:10).

### 2b. Ranked patterns (value x fit with Volyume constraints)
Constraints applied: no photos of people without moderation, no AI, no bodyweight or calorie ranking, calm mode and open ED flag withhold, preset icons only. "Fit" is my judgement from the sources above; this document does not read the Volyume code, so mark each as a candidate to verify against the current build.
1. **One-tap, low-cost reaction with etiquette guard** (Strava kudos, Peloton rate limit 1/min): highest value, trivially fits; add a per-minute cap and no blanket "react to all" outside a group moment.
2. **Auto-post of the finished workout as the core feed object** (Hevy, Strava, Tonal): feed is a by-product of logging, not a separate publishing chore.
3. **Small private groups with chat anchored to a shared object** (Caliber private groups, WHOOP team chat+leaderboard, Ladder team): fits; moderation load small because invite-only.
4. **Consistency-based recognition, not strength-ranked** (Strava Local Legend frequency, Weekly Streak Stickers, Peloton milestone prompts): fits ED-safety; never bodyweight.
5. **Per-field profile privacy panel** (Tonal): fits GDPR; let the user tick which stats show.
6. **Plan sharing with two tiers: plan-only vs full-log copy** (Hevy, Boostcamp one-tap save): fits; no-install web preview (hevy.com) is a growth loop but needs a GDPR/EU-hosting review first.
7. **Live PR moment inside the logger** (Hevy): reinforces "social is part of the logger".
8. **Presence: "friends training now"** (Peloton Here Now): high engagement; gate behind explicit opt-in and withhold in calm mode.
9. **Seasonal, fixed-start habit challenge counting sessions, not weight** (Gymshark66, JEFIT quarterly): fits if the metric is sessions logged; keep it in-app (do not push to public hashtags).
10. **Self-chosen identity tags** (Peloton): fits the discipline/physique cohort ask; curated list only, no free text.
11. **Followed-people leaderboards on lifts, with fairness rules** (Hevy; its own July 2026 note admits single vs double weight logging skews fairness): fits if relative to self-follow set and withholds under ED flag.
12. **Club organiser tooling and events** (Strava 2026): high value for gym groups; larger build.
13. **Day-one populated group** (Ladder teams): solves cold start; needs seeded, moderated, preset-named groups (editorial effort, no AI).
14. **Milestone nudges to friends** (Peloton): turns a PR into a prompted reaction.
15. **Gym tagging of workouts** (Hevy roadmap): UNVERIFIED how it will look; location sensitivity, EU residency review needed.

### 2d. What makes social feel "part of" the logger
Evidence-based, with sources:
1. The post is generated by the act of logging, not authored (Hevy, Strava strength, Tonal).
2. The reward moment is in the logger itself (Hevy live PR; Peloton in-class notifications), then reappears in the feed.
3. The social object is the workout or plan, so reactions, comparisons, copy-this-workout and plan sharing all act on the same data (Hevy Copy Workout vs Share Routine; Boostcamp save; Tonal "friends joined this programme").
4. Groups are anchored to a shared object (team plan, leaderboard), not a free-form forum (Ladder, WHOOP; MyFitnessPal forums feel "cold" and separate per user posts above).
5. Full opt-out stays easy: Hevy's forced default tab is a documented complaint, Strong's no-social model still thrives, Google Health cut social down. Volyume should keep logging the home of the product.
6. Small and focused beats broad: Google Health's 2026 cut and Strava's 2026 decision to add strength to the main feed rather than a new product both point the same way.

## 3. Noise: do NOT copy
Photo-voting (JEFIT), weigh-in or calorie challenges (MyFitnessPal), physiological-score ranking (WHOOP), public-hashtag entry rules (Gymshark66), live rank pressure (Peloton), AI coach tooling (Ladder, Boostcamp Pro, Fitbod), segment/heatmap location machinery (Strava, 2018 leak P:11), referral-for-prizes loops (JEFIT), paid-tier social gating, forced social default tab (Hevy).
