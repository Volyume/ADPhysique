## 2. Findings (lead-verified where marked; the full evidence is `05-R3-BODY-METRICS-READ.md`, numbered BM-1 to BM-51)

### 2.1 Untrue lines and broken paths (severity 1)

- **BM-1 (verified in code).** Editing any weigh-in logged from Home cannot save: `BodyMetricsScreen.js:975` passes `{ weightKg: data.body_weight }` while the validator sets `data.weightKg` (`bodyMetricValidate.js:113`), so `updateMorningWeightById` throws on an undefined weight (`database.js:8017`) and the person reads "Couldn't save. Try again." Every merged Home row is such a row. The measurements warning at `:971` tests `data[m.key]` against keys that are `chestCm`-style, so it never fires. `BodyMetricsScreen.editDelete.guard.test.js:65` pins the defect.
- **BM-2.** "Entry deleted." and the row comes back: a form entry is written through to `morning_weights`, the delete tombstones only `body_metric_log`, and the merge re-lists the day's morning weight. The confirm promises removal from history.
- **BM-3.** After fourteen days without a weigh-in the Progress Body row says "No weigh-ins logged yet" to a person with forty.
- **BM-4.** Body metrics prints a months-old "Weekly change" as current (no date bound on its EWMA), one tap from a root that says there are none.
- **BM-5 (verified).** The screen loads fifty entries (`:750`, `:781`) and judges "All N weeks" and the 6-month and year chips against that set (`:275`).
- **BM-6.** A direction word from two weigh-ins, and wrong (80 then 82 kg prints "holding steady").
- **BM-7.** Four numbers for one body weight; "average" is the mean of the smoothed series, not of the weigh-ins.
- **BM-8.** The robust smoother damps gains to about a quarter of a 0.5 kg a week gain on the takeaway, with no raw dots beside it any more (COMP-024's premise no longer holds on this screen).
- **BM-9.** The delta badge draws a down arrow for zero, compares with the previous entry of any age, and prints pounds beside a stone hero.
- **BM-10 (verified).** "Weight steady." beside "Losing weight": recomposition's flat rule is 0.15 kg per entry over eight entries (`recompReframe.js:33-34`), the chip's is 0.2 kg a week over 42 days (`BodyMetricsScreen.js:209-211`).
- **BM-11.** "Barbell Bench Press up 6 kg" is an estimated one-rep max gain in kilograms labelled with the person's units (a pounds user reads kilograms as pounds), with no "estimated" and no span.
- **BM-12.** "Average intake 1625 kcal over the last 1 day" misstates a seven-day window in which one day was logged.
- **BM-13.** The maintenance caption prints weigh-in days divided by seven as "weeks", "High confidence" at the memo's minimum, and "Firming up" is unreachable.
- **BM-14 (verified).** Body metrics resolves the maintenance estimate with different inputs from every other surface: `rowToEntry` carries no `body_fat_source` (`:99-122`), so `bodyFatSource` is always null (`:623`) while the hook passes the stored source (`useWeightTrend.js:90`); the formula context differs, the screen can show "build it" while the coach holds a current figure, and it can persist a revalidation marker. Engine-adjacent: the lead traces and fixes.
- **BM-15.** The Progress Body row's fallback says the maintenance calories "are worked out" whether or not a figure exists.
- **BM-16, BM-17.** The ED-branch sentence claims stability when the rate is unknown, "a few weeks" at seven weigh-ins, and "slightly" at any size; the calm sentence says "kept in Body metrics" to a person with no weigh-ins. ED-safety copy: section 4.
- **BM-18 (verified).** The first visit writes the onboarding weight as a weigh-in dated today (`:752-772`), through to the morning series, and the marker note is never shown.
- **BM-19 to BM-21.** Same state, different withholds on one screen (section 4).

### 2.2 What a lay reader cannot say back (severity 2)

The chip ("Maintaining" what?), "All 2 weeks" under a lit "3M", "1M 3M 6M Y", "View data", a card called "Weight trend" that prints a weight, "Weekly change" with no referent and no noise band, "Effective maintenance" with a paragraph whose "This" has no referent, three unit systems for a stone user on one screen, a form whose unit labels vanish on typing, "RECOMPOSITION", "Body fat down 1%" (percentage points), six definitions of "flat", five "enough data" thresholds with no denominator, a body-fat figure with no method or date (BM-22 to BM-33).

### 2.3 Value (severity 3)

"Is my weight doing what the plan wants?" is not answered on the screen; "what changed this week?" and "how much is normal?" are missing; no "weighed N of 7 mornings" denominator and no "so far"; the history stops at twelve rows with no way further; the measurements card vanishes whenever the newest row is a weight-only weigh-in; no plausibility guard (28.4 kg and a date in 2027 are accepted) while Home's quick gate has no floor at all; an evening entry silently replaces the morning weight; notes are never shown; the better chart (COMP-004, smoothed over raw) is dead code that nothing renders (BM-34 to BM-45).

### 2.4 Looks (severity 4)

Amber on six elements, four of them facts; the photos door above the weight; the primary action below the fold; two equal weight numerals of different meaning; a 120 dp chart with a padded axis that flattens the signal; twelve rows with twenty-four bordered controls; a hard-coded 10 px delta; italic grey captions carrying the explanations; ungrouped numbers (BM-46 to BM-51).

## 3. The design (lead, hands-on; ruled under D33)

The screen's question: "Is my weight doing what the plan wants, and what changed this week?" Order, top to bottom:

1. `BackHeader` "Body metrics".
2. **This week** (the object, one `Card`):
   - Line 1: `type.h2` the TREND WEIGHT now with `type.bodyStrong` "trend weight" (with a single weigh-in the label reads "first weigh-in", review N11) and `type.bodySm` "weighed 4 of 5 mornings so far this week". The trend weight is the ONE smoothed figure on the screen: `computeEWMA` (alpha 0.28), the same figure the Progress root prints, over the real trailing 90 days with the 14-day boundary the root uses (D97-22, D97-25; the hook's reading becomes the shared one). The robust smoother stays the engine's and the coach's; no display surface calls two smoothers. "Trend weight" gets the (i): "A smoothed average of your recent weigh-ins, so one heavy or light morning moves it only a little."
   - Line 2, the verdict: the weekly coach's own sentence when its output is within 14 days ("Moving at the planned rate." and the five others, `weightTrend.js` `coachVerdictInsight`), exactly the Progress Body row's words; otherwise the direction over the last two weeks in plain words with its rate ("Down 0.6 kg over the last 2 weeks, about 0.3 kg a week."; a rate inside the one steady rule, 0.2 kg a week, reads "Holding steady over the last 2 weeks, about 0.09 kg a week.": the first draft's example sat inside the rule and was corrected at the build, D214 addendum 7); from 7 weigh-ins over 7 or more days, else "Not enough weigh-ins yet to show which way your weight is going: 3 of 7." (census 0.3, D214 addendum 9: never "for a direction"); the window named is the one the weigh-ins cover, "over the last 8 days" until they span a fortnight (lane 7 review S3)
   - Line 3, this week against last: "This week's average 82.3 kg, 0.2 kg below last week's (5 weigh-ins against 6)." Monday-anchored local weeks (`dayKey.js`), the plain average of the week's weigh-ins; "so far" on the open week; omitted until last week has two weigh-ins.
   - Line 4, the noise line: "Day to day your weight usually moves within 0.4 kg." from the person's own weigh-ins (the median absolute day-to-day change over the last 28 days, a pure display derivation in `weightTrend.js`; shown from 10 weigh-ins). It is what stops a 0.3 kg morning being read as a change.
   - No chip, no arrow, no delta badge: direction lives in line 2 with its window, change in line 3 with its referent.
3. **The action row**: "Log weight" (primary, the ONE amber on the screen) and "Add measurements" (secondary). The form: units printed as labels that never vanish ("12" [st] "7" [lbs], the app's one spelling), the date through the date picker with no future dates, a plausibility check before saving ("That is 54 kg below your last weigh-in of 82.4 kg. Save it anyway?"; the rule: more than 5% or 5 kg from the last weigh-in; the same check on Home's quick entry, which also gains the form's 20 kg floor), a note field whose text shows in the history, body fat with its method asked and glossed (`coachGlossary.bodyFatMethod`) ONLY ONCE THE FOUNDER CONFIRMS (D214 addendum 7, open question 1: a measured method stored from this form moves the FFM floor to the typed figure through `computeFFMFloor`, as the setup wizard's choice does, where the old form stored 'manual'; the build keeps 'manual' and asks no method until the founder rules), measurement sites with "how to measure" behind one (i). A later entry on a day that already has a weigh-in says so before saving: "This replaces today's 7:02 weigh-in of 82.4 kg."
4. **Trend** (one card): title "Trend, last 3 months" with chips in words "1 month · 3 months · 6 months · 1 year" (the selected chip is the screen's second control; its selected state uses the chip's own style); the chart draws the smoothed trend line in ink (`textPrimary`) over the weigh-ins as faint dots (`textMuted`), a two-entry `LegendRow` ("Trend · Weigh-ins"), a y-axis fitted to the window's data plus the typical swing (never a fixed 1 kg pad), date labels at the start, middle and end with weekly ticks (carrying the year when the window crosses one, so "1 year" never reads "17 Sep to 17 Sep", review S2), 160 dp tall; the data is read for the window shown (a date-range read, never the newest fifty rows), so "6 months" and "1 year" are true. The takeaway under it: "4 Sep to 17 Sep: your weigh-ins averaged 82.3 kg; the trend held steady, about 0.05 kg a week." ("held steady" needs BOTH a rate inside the steady rule and a total movement under half a kilo, so a year that drifted 2 kg reads "moved down 2 kg, about 0.04 kg a week"; D214 addendum 7) When the log is shorter than the window: "Everything you have logged, 4 Sep to 17 Sep: ...". The direction word follows the same 7-weigh-in rule; the dead-band is the one steady rule with the half-kilo amount floor, said in the (i) (the first draft said the typical swing; corrected at the build, D214 addendum 7). The "View data" button goes; the history list is the chart's text alternative and the chart carries one spoken summary (AX-02).
5. **Maintenance calories** (the term every reader knows and the coach's own diet-break line already uses; the first build's title "Calories that hold your weight" was rejected by the founder on 2026-10-02 as not plain English, D214 addendum 8; the (i) reads "Maintenance calories are the calories you eat in a day to stay the same weight. This is an estimate worked out from your weight and food logs, not a measurement. Your coaching calls it effective maintenance. It needs 14 weigh-ins and food logged on 5 of the last 7 days.", census 0.1b): current: "About 2,450 kcal a day, estimated from 23 weigh-ins and food logged on 6 of the last 7 days." (the memo's own counts, no span claimed, review S5; "weigh-ins" and "food logged on N of the last 7 days", census 0.5) (the memo contract's own windows: the weeks the weigh-ins span, and a seven-day food window, so the first draft's "18 logged food days over the last 8 weeks" could never be true; corrected at the build, D214 addendum 7); being rechecked: "Earlier estimate, about 2,450 kcal a day, being rechecked against your newer weigh-ins and food."; held (older than 14 days with nothing newer): said as what it is; not ready: "Not ready yet. It needs 14 weigh-ins (you have 9) and food logged on 5 of the last 7 days (you have 3)." (the true counts, what the estimate needs and what there is, never an instruction to the person; a lapsed series reads "It needs a weigh-in from the last 14 days"); both thresholds met with no memo yet: "Not ready yet. It has 40 weigh-ins and food logged on 7 of the last 7 days; the first estimate is worked out the next time your coaching runs." (the coaching run learns it, review S4). The number is grouped and called "estimated" on its own line (D201). The inputs are the SAME as the hook's and the coach's, built by one helper (`src/lib/maintenanceInputs.js`, the lead's) that every caller uses, with the stored body-fat source and the date of birth; the screen never persists a revalidation marker. The intake line reads "Over the last 7 days you logged food on 3 days, averaging 1,625 kcal." or is omitted when no day was logged.
6. **Recomposition** stays, on ONE definition of "steady": the trend's rate over the last 6 weeks within 0.2 kg a week (the retired chip's rule, now the only one; read off the same 90-day-seeded smoother as the trend weight, never a fresh smoother started at the window's first morning, review N6), "Weight steady over the last 6 weeks. (each figure dated, rule 7.0.4; the share card's hero unit reads "kg added to your estimated one-rep max", census 0.14; body fat reads from one figure to the other, never "1 point" and never "1%", founder order 2026-10-02; "lbs" is the app's one spelling, `units.js`) in the person's units, the share image unchanged in content; suppressed under calm and an open flag as now.
7. **Body fat and measurements**: the latest value of each with its date from ANY entry (never gated on the newest row being a full entry), the change against the previous entry carrying that site with both dates ("Down 2 cm from 88 cm on 26 Aug."; body fat "Down from 19% on 26 Aug." beside its own value), the body-fat method shown; "Add measurements" is the door.
8. **History**: weekly groups "Week of 15 Sep · average 82.3 kg · 5 weigh-ins" (the same averages as the This week card; under calm mode or an open flag the header keeps the count only, "Week of 15 Sep · 5 weigh-ins", review N1), rows "Tue 16 Sep · 82.5 kg" with the note beneath when there is one, each row tappable to open the edit form (built inline in the row rather than as a bottom sheet, since a sheet over the keyboard is the pattern the keyboard history argued against, D214 addendum 7; edit and delete live inside it, one control per row, no bordered icon pair); "Show earlier weeks" loads the next eight weeks; an edit on a Home-logged row saves (BM-1) and the sheet shows only the fields that row can hold (weight, date, note) unless the person adds measurements, which makes it the day's full entry; a delete removes the day's weigh-in from history AND from the trend (the morning series), the confirm saying exactly that; a day holds one weigh-in (the latest replaces the earlier, said at save time).
9. **The Progress root Body row** (`pillars.js`, the lead): evidence "Trend 82.4 kg, +0.1 kg a week over the last 2 weeks" (the referent and window named; the plan's rate stays off the row, Q6 = B); the lapsed state "No weigh-in in the last 14 days; the last was 3 weeks ago." in place of "No weigh-ins logged yet" (BM-3); the maintenance sentence only when a figure exists (BM-15).
10. **Day zero**: no fabricated weigh-in. The onboarding weight shows as "Starting weight from setup: 82 kg, 3 Aug" in the This week card's slot with "Your trend starts with your first morning weigh-in."; the history is empty; the seed row is never written (existing seed rows keep their real date and show their note); a weigh-in typed through the form on the setup day is a weigh-in the person made, never the seed (review S1).
11. **States**: loading skeleton in each card's slot; a failed read says so under its own card ("Couldn't load your trend just now.") and logs; a stone user reads stones and pounds everywhere on the screen except the chart axis, which stays in kilograms with its one-line note (A1, pinned), rates in "lbs a week"; one spelling ("12 st 13.5 lbs", the app's `formatBodyWeight` and `units.js`; the first draft's "lb" was corrected to the app's spelling at the build, D214 addendum 7).
12. **Colour and type**: amber on "Log weight" only; the trend line, dots, chips' unselected states, history and every number in ink; no italic captions; the micro size only on the chart axis; grouped numbers.

What this design does not touch: the engine's smoothers and gates (`robustTrend.js`, the rapid-loss and ED gates, the canonical `morning_weights` series as the gate's input), the memo contract (`effectiveMaintenance.js`), D166 (bodyweight's prominence on Progress), Q2 and Q6, the calm interstitial.
