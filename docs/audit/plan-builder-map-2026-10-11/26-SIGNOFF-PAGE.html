What happens when you approve

Once you approve and the last two stages are finished (the checks on later changes, and moving existing plans), the new builder is switched on for everyone in the next build you run. People already on a plan the old builder made move to the new one at their next session, keeping their exercises wherever the rules allow. Hand-built plans, library plans and your own plans are never changed. Reply "approved" in chat, or name any plan here you want changed first.
