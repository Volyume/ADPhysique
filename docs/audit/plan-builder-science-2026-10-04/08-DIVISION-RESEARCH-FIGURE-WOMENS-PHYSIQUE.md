## 1. Judging criteria

### 1A. Figure

| Body | Quoted criteria | URL |
|---|---|---|
| NPC Worldwide rules (hosted on IFBB Pro) | "A small degree of muscularity with separation but no visible striations"; "Toned muscle with shapely lines and overall firmness but not excessively lean"; "Overall healthy appearance, including skin tone and makeup"; "Overall stage presentation". No muscle group is named on that page. | https://www.ifbbpro.com/npc-worldwide/rules/ |
| OCB | "Judging for Figure is based on symmetry, muscularity and conditioning." "Leanness and muscle development are expected in figure, but NOT to the same degree as presented in Women's Physique." "Balanced muscle proportion from top to bottom." "Looking for an 'X' frame with capped shoulders, wide lats, small waist, full round, glutes, sweeping quads". "Lean, visible separation/cuts in quads, hamstrings, abdominals, and back." "Not grainy or overly striated muscles, with the possible exception of the shoulders". Marked down: "deeply drawn-in faces or stringy-looking muscularity (an emaciated look)"; "less shapely muscularity due to muscle glycogen depletion". | https://ocbonline.com/figure/ |
| WNBF (2025 judging deck, PDF) | Two rounds: Symmetry, Muscle Tone. "Figure competitors should be more muscular than a bikini competitor". "Bodybuilding muscularity does not fit this category and is scored down". | https://wnbf.no/documents/2025%20Updated%20Official%20WNBF%20Judging%20Rules%20EDITED.pdf (slide text extracted with pdftotext) |
| Coach explainer (Heather Owen, not a federation) | "Shoulders, back, quads and glutes are very important muscle groups to give the "x" type of shape." Judges want separation but not striations. | https://getfitgofigure.com/divisions-explained-womens-bikini-figure-physique-bodybuilding/ |

## 5. What the sources say the division does NOT do

- Figure: no visible striations; not excessively lean (https://www.ifbbpro.com/npc-worldwide/rules/). Bodybuilding muscularity "is scored down" (WNBF PDF above). No deeply drawn-in faces or stringy muscle (https://ocbonline.com/figure/). Gillon trains biceps with lower volume than other upper-body muscles because they "grow very readily" (https://www.kaged.com/blogs/cydney-gillon-figure-trainer/cgfigure-training). A very developed upper body with no leg muscle is not wanted: "You don't want a very developed upper body and no leg muscle" (https://getfitgofigure.com/10-training-tips-for-the-figure-competitor/). Figure glutes are smaller than Wellness in the Frontiers paper's description (URL in section 2A).
- Women's Physique: not described as ripped, shredded, peeled, striated, dry, diced, hard, vascular, grainy, massive, thick, dense; "Excessive muscularity should be scored down" (https://www.ifbbpro.com/npc-worldwide/rules/). OCB says the opposite on size ("the most muscularity of the four categories") but still marks down stringy, depleted muscle (https://ocbonline.com/womens-physique/).
- Training: the Frontiers WP medians show chest (2) and abs (2) near zero in that sample (n=3; not a rule). W1 lists no direct abs, calf or glute work (TotalShape URL above). No source I fetched states that either division omits a named muscle group from training; that claim is NOT FOUND.
