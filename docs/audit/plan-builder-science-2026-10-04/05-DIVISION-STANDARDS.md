# 05 Division standards: baseline plus emphasis

Purpose: per-category weekly set ranges, exposures and session lists for the plan builder, under the founder rule (full bodybuilding baseline for every category, division emphasis ADDED, never subtracted).

Evidence grading. OBSERVED = read in a search result this session. RECALLED = standard coaching knowledge not re-fetched this session; verify before quoting to users. Search coverage was standard mode, one pass; the official NPC men's physique and classic physique criteria pages were NOT retrieved (see Gaps).

## Sources used

- S1 RP volume landmarks (MEV/MAV/MRV framework): https://rpstrength.com/training-volume-landmarks-muscle-growth/ (OBSERVED in results; per-muscle numbers from third-party summary https://arvo.guru/fr/tools/volume-calculator: chest MEV ~8, MAV 12-20, MRV 22+; back MEV ~8, MAV 12-20, MRV 25+).
- S2 Third-party summaries agree MAV is about 12-20 sets/muscle/week and MEV about 4-10 (same arvo page, https://coachway.io/articles/dr-mike-israetel-how-many-sets-for-coaches/). OBSERVED.
- S3 Men's physique: upper-body focus, V-taper, shoulder-to-waist ratio, calves support the look; board shorts cover legs: https://anbfnatural.com/mens-physique-guidelines/ (OBSERVED, a natural federation, indicative of NPC).
- S4 Classic physique: X-frame, more muscle than men's physique, less than open: https://anbfnatural.com/classic-physique-guidelines/ and https://www.ironmanmagazine.com/y-is-an-x-frame-mandatory/ (X-frame is the V-taper plus bigger quads and hamstrings). OBSERVED.
- S5 Bikini: foundation of muscle, full round glutes with slight hamstring separation, small delt roundness, conditioned core, no figure-level density: https://npcnewsonline.com/?p=215473 and https://fitnessvolt.com/tyler-manion-judging-criteria-bikini/ (proportion top to bottom). OBSERVED.
- S6 Wellness: lower-body (glute and thigh) emphasis, more leg muscle than bikini: https://fitnessvolt.com/tyler-manion-explains-wellness-judging-criteria and https://cleanhealth.edu.au/blog/training/determining-the-right-division-for-your-female-physique-athlete/ (titles OBSERVED; detail RECALLED).
- S7 Open bodybuilding: symmetry and proportion, shoulder caps against arms and calves, chest against quads, classic X frame: ironmanmagazine.com result above. OBSERVED.
- S8 Figure: more muscle and V-taper than bikini, "figure-style muscular density" per S5 negative criteria. Partly OBSERVED (via S5), rest RECALLED.
- S9 Helms Muscle and Strength Pyramids (Nutrition/Training), 3-4 days and 2x/week frequency per muscle as default: https://muscleandstrengthpyramids.com RECALLED.
- S10 Schoenfeld 2016 frequency meta-analysis (2x/week beats 1x when volume is equated) and Schoenfeld 2017 dose-response (more sets, more growth, roughly 10+ weekly sets best): RECALLED, search PubMed "Schoenfeld 2017 dose-response resistance training volume".

Take-away used throughout: the app's 10-20 direct sets/week is the MAV band of S1/S2 and matches S10. Intermediate baselines below sit inside it.

## 1. Baseline and emphasis tables

Baseline column is identical across all categories by founder rule (it is what every category keeps). Where a muscle's baseline is lower (small or heavily indirect-worked muscles), it is noted. Emphasis is ADDED sets on top, in the stated amount. "Direct sets/week" means sets where the muscle is the prime mover.

Baseline (all categories), intermediate, growth:

| Muscle | Baseline direct sets/wk | Note |
|---|---|---|
| chest | 10-16 | S1: MAV 12-20 |
| back | 12-18 | rows plus vertical pulls, both counted |
| side_delts | 8-16 | recovers fast, tolerates high frequency (RECALLED, RP) |
| rear_delts | 6-12 | also gets back indirect |
| front_delts | 0-6 | pressing already covers it; direct only optional |
| traps | 4-10 | shrugs; also rows indirect |
| biceps | 8-14 | small muscle, 1-2 exercises/session |
| triceps | 8-14 | pressing gives large indirect share |
| forearms | 0-8 | grip work indirect; direct optional for baseline |
| quads | 10-16 | |
| hamstrings | 8-14 | include hip hinge AND knee flexion |

| adductors | 4-8 | often via adductor machine or wide squat |

Emphasis per division. none = baseline only; moderate = about +2-4 sets/wk; high = about +4-8 sets/wk (still inside the S1 MRV, so total stays at or below about 22).

## 2. Exposures and session lists

Rule check for all lists: 2-3 exercises per session for big muscles (chest, back, quads, hamstrings, glutes), 1-2 for small. Sets per exercise 2-4. Two exposures per muscle per week is the mainstream default (S9, S10); 3 for side/rear delts, calves, abs and any emphasised small muscle. A muscle at 12 sets over 2 sessions = 2 exercises x 3 sets per session, within the cap. A muscle at 18 sets needs 3 exposures or 3 exercises x 3 sets over 2 sessions.

### 2.1 Exposure pattern by day count (mainstream)
| Days | Split | Big-muscle exposures | Small-muscle exposures |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | full body A/B/A (alternating) | 2-3 | 2-3 |
| 4 | upper/lower x2 | 2 | 2 |
| 5 | upper/lower + push/pull/legs, or PPL + upper/lower | 2 (emphasis muscle 3) | 2-3 |
| 6 | PPL x2 | 2 | 2-4 |

### 2.2 Session lists. Same template is used for general and bodybuilding; divisions swap a few slots toward the emphasised muscles (additions marked +).

**3 days (full body A, B, A / B, A, B)**
- Full A: chest, back (row), quads, hamstrings (hinge), side_delts, triceps, calves, abs.
- Full B: chest, back (vertical pull), glutes/quads, hamstrings (curl), rear_delts, biceps, side_delts, traps.
- Per week with three sessions: big muscles 2-3 sessions at 2 exercises x 2-3 sets gives 8-18 sets; ok.
- Division additions: mens_physique + side_delts, lats, abs in every session; classic + lats, side_delts; bikini + glutes in every session; wellness + glutes and quads each session; figure + side_delts and back; bodybuilding adds weakest muscle a third exposure.

**4 days (Upper A, Lower A, Upper B, Lower B)**
- Upper A (strength/heavier): chest x2-3, back x2-3, side_delts, biceps, triceps.
- Lower A: quads x2-3, hamstrings x2, glutes x1-2, calves, abs.
- Upper B (volume): back x2-3, chest x2-3, side_delts, rear_delts, traps, biceps, triceps, forearms optional.
- Lower B: hamstrings x2-3, glutes x2, quads x2, adductors, calves, abs.
- Check: chest 2 sessions x 2-3 exercises x 2-3 sets = 8-18, in baseline. Same for the others.
- Division swaps: mens_physique +1 side_delt exercise and +1 lat exercise in both uppers, +abs; classic same plus a third quad exercise in Lower A; bikini +1 glute exercise in each lower (hip thrust, RDL, split squat); wellness +1 glute and +1 quad exercise per lower; figure +1 side_delt and +1 back exercise per upper.

**5 days (Push, Pull, Legs, Upper, Lower)**
- Push: chest x3, side_delts x2, triceps x2.
- Pull: back x3, rear_delts, biceps x2, traps.
- Legs: quads x2-3, hamstrings x2, glutes x1-2, calves.
- Upper: chest x2, back x2, side_delts, rear_delts, biceps, triceps.
- Lower: hamstrings x2, glutes x2, quads x1-2, adductors, calves, abs.
- Check: chest, back, quads, hamstrings, glutes each 2 exposures; side_delts 3 (Push, Upper, plus a finisher in Pull or Lower); calves and abs 2-3.
- Division: the extra day goes to the emphasised muscle only on top of the above (e.g. bikini/wellness: swap Upper for a second glute-focused Lower with chest/back still trained in Push/Pull; NEVER by removing upper-body exposures).

**6 days (Push A, Pull A, Legs A, Push B, Pull B, Legs B)**
- Push A/B: chest x2-3, side_delts x2, triceps x2 (B swaps to incline, cable lateral raises, overhead triceps).
- Pull A/B: back x3, rear_delts, biceps x2, traps (A row-focused, B vertical-focused).
- Legs A: quads-focused (squat variation x2, leg press/extension, calves, abs). Legs B: hamstring/glute-focused (RDL, curls, hip thrust, adductors, calves).
- Each muscle 2 exposures, side_delts and rear_delts get a third via finishers. Totals reach the upper end of baseline; emphasis muscles up to MRV-ish 20-22.

### 2.3 Weekly-range feasibility by category
All categories: every muscle in 1.x tables reaches baseline via the 4-day template above at 2 exposures x 2-3 exercises (cap respected). Emphasis adds are delivered by (a) +1 exercise in an existing session (still within 3-per-session cap for big muscles, 2 for small), or (b) a third exposure at 5-6 days. At 3 days, high emphasis can only exceed baseline for one muscle group per session; the plan should add exposure (finishers) rather than remove other muscles.

## 3. What mainstream coaching NEVER does

1. Cuts a muscle to one exercise or one weekly exposure for a physique division. Even men's physique prep guides keep full legs because the X/V-frame needs them (S3, S4 and https://www.ironmanmagazine.com/y-is-an-x-frame-mandatory/).
2. Holds legs at maintenance in a men's physique off-season. Off-season is a growth phase; board shorts hide legs on stage only (S3). Leg size reads in posing and proportion.
3. Drops chest, arms or core in bikini or wellness. Bikini needs a muscular foundation and "proportional top to bottom" (S5).
4. Prescribes only isolation for glutes or only hip thrusts without squat or hinge patterns for bikini or wellness (RECALLED, mainstream glute programmes).
5. Trains any muscle under MEV (about 4-8 direct sets, S1/S2) during a growth phase and calls it progress.
6. Pushes any muscle past MRV (about 22-26 sets) to "bring up" a lagging part; coaches add exposures and exercise variety within MAV first (S1).
7. Treats stage conditioning limits (e.g. bikini avoiding "figure-style density", S5) as a reason to under-train in the off-season. Size is controlled by diet and phase, not by deleting muscle groups (RECALLED).
8. Lets a hidden or "not judged" muscle go untrained (e.g. hamstrings in men's physique). Injury balance and posture matter (S10, S9).
9. More than 3 exercises per session for one big muscle; mainstream caps because of diminishing returns per session (the app's own science.js constants, and Helms S9).
