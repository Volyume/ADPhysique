Lead rulings for this lane (D33, recorded by the lead):

1. Credits (lead ruling): the catalogue's `credits` follow one curated rule, not the corpus's secondary roles, because the corpus credits rear-delt flyes to back, squats to hamstrings and hip thrusts to quads, which makes the planner give back 4 direct sets a week. The rule, by the role an exercise fills (every equipment variant of the role gets it): flat and incline presses { triceps: 0.5, front_delts: 0.5 }; overhead press { triceps: 0.5, side_delts: 0.5 }; vertical pulls { biceps: 0.5 } (the wide-grip pulldown also rear_delts: 0.5); rows { biceps: 0.5, rear_delts: 0.5, traps: 0.5 }; squats, hack squat and leg press { glutes: 0.5, adductors: 0.5 }; Romanian deadlift and other hinges { glutes: 0.5 }; hip thrust { hamstrings: 0.5 }; lunges and split squats listed under glutes { quads: 0.5 }; every isolation exercise {}. An exercise counts 1 for the muscle its catalogue role trains (drop or ignore the `direct: 0.5` idea). These are exactly the credits in src/lib/plan/__tests__/fixtures/choices.js; a test pins that resolveCatalogue's full-gym output carries the same credits for those names.
2. Test: a Walking Lunge planned under glutes is served under glutes.
