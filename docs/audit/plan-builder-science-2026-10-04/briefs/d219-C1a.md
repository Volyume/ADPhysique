# D219 lane C1a: the planner's fixed-structure mode (register build ruling 5, founder Q1)

Read first: d219-build-common.md; design ../00-AUDIT-AND-PLAN.md sections 8, 4.2 to 4.9 and 4.14; register D219 "Build rulings, 2026-10-04" item 5 ("a LIBRARY or KIT plan keeps its authored sessions and exercises, and the new planner sets its sets, climb and order around them (a fixed-structure mode)"). The planner: src/lib/plan/planner.js (buildPlan, evaluateFamily, toPlan) and src/lib/plan/allocate.js (allocatePeakWeek, slotChoice, the floor stages). Lead rulings already in it that must keep holding: a focus muscle's sets are never cut for time or the D45 ceilings; a focus muscle's isolation exercise may take 4 sets; no session mixes the halves of the body (does not apply to authored sessions: they are the person's); the week-5 peak equals what prescribe() serves.

Build (the API is the lead's; keep it exactly):
1. buildPlan accepts `fixedSessions: [{ name, routineId, exercises: [{ exerciseId, name, muscle, kind, credits }] }]`. With it: the only family is those sessions in that order (key 'fixed'); each muscle's exposures are exactly the sessions whose exercises train it (no session-count search); every authored exercise is kept and opened at its floor (2 sets) in stage 1, the exercises a session lists for a muscle are that session's choices for it (allocate.js gains `sessionChoices[sessionIndex][muscle]`, used by slotChoice and slotsAllowed in place of the catalogue list; the per-muscle allowance does not cut an authored exercise); then the floor stages and the greedy place the sets inside every cap as today; the rotation order, the light caps, the readiness check and its fixes run as today, except fixes that add or remove a session for a muscle (the structure is the person's).
2. toPlan keeps each exercise's exerciseId and the session's routineId, so the caller can write the targets and facts onto the existing rows.
3. Without fixedSessions, every output is byte-identical (the whole planner suite green, unedited).
4. Tests, each failing first: every authored exercise kept in its session; no exercise above its cap in any served week (prescribe over the output, 6 weeks); a focus muscle's sets kept; determinism; an authored 5-day bro split and a 3-day full-body library plan both build.

Do not touch anything outside src/lib/plan/planner.js, src/lib/plan/allocate.js and their tests. Lane R3 runs at the same time (algorithms.js, sessionAdjustments.js, src/lib/recovery/**, planAutoGen.js). STOP and report on any ambiguity.
