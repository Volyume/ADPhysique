# 06 Division research: Men's Physique and Classic Physique

Research date: 2026-10-10. Method: every claim below was read on a page fetched this session; the URL is given inline. Where a fact could not be fetched, it says NOT FOUND. Search snippets were used only to locate pages, never as evidence.

Evidence grades used throughout:
- PRIMARY = words or programme by the athlete or coach (own site, own quoted video).
- SECONDARY = a magazine or site reporting or reconstructing the athlete's routine (named, but not their own document).
- UNATTRIBUTED = no named coach or athlete (kept only to show the tally; excluded from "named source" counts).

Important limits, stated up front:
- Almost no PRIMARY programme documents were reachable (Muscle and Strength returned HTTP 403 on every fetch; Sommerfeld's own programme page only advertises a paid PDF). Most splits below are SECONDARY reconstructions and several are contest-prep, not off-season. Each row says which.
- ifbbpro.com was never returned by search or fetched. The "IFBB Pro League" criteria below are NOT FOUND; the IFBB (federation, ifbb.com) rule PDFs and the NPC pages were used instead.

## 1. Judging criteria

### 1.1 Men's Physique

**NPC** (https://npcnewsonline.com/official-npc-mens-physique-division/), "Contest Format (Judging)":
- "Judges will be looking for fit competitors who display proper shape and symmetry" combined with "muscularity and overall condition."
- "This is not a bodybuilding contest so extreme muscularity will be marked down."
- "Judges will also be looking for the competitor with the best stage presence and poise."
- The page's judging text does not mention legs or lower body. Board shorts appear only in General Rules: "They must be one inch below the navel." "They must extend to two inches above the kneecap and be tapered." "Spandex is prohibited."

**IFBB federation, Section 9 Men's Physique 2017** (https://ifbb.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/Mens-Physique-2017.pdf, text extracted from the PDF):
- Art. 10.1: assessment "should begin at the head and extend downwards, taking the whole physique into account."
- "Judges will be looking for fit contestants who display proper shape and body proportions, combined with balanced muscularity and overall condition. The judges are reminded that extreme muscularity and definition should be marked down."
- Appendix 2 (legs, explicit): "With the exception of the upper legs, which are covered by board shorts, the assessment should take the whole physique into account."
- Appendix 2: "The judges should favour competitors with a harmonious, proportional, classical male physique, good posture, correct anatomical structure (... straight legs, not bandy or knock- kneed). Vertical proportions (legs to upper body length) and horizontal proportions (hips and waist to shoulder width) are ones of the key factors."
- Art. 6.1 shorts: "The shorts will cover the whole upper leg, down the upper point of the knee (patella)." No tight lycra.
- The 2021 edition (https://ifbb.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Mens-Physique-Rules-2021-.pdf) carries identical Appendix 2 wording ("With the exception of the upper legs, which are covered by board shorts...") and the same "extreme muscularity and definition should be marked down" line. It also introduces a separate "Muscular Men's Physique" category "for competitors with slightly bigger muscularity".

**2Bros / UK pro-qualifier federation** (https://www.2bpevents.com/division-rules/):
- "fit contestants who display proper shape and symmetry combined with muscularity and overall condition." "This is not a bodybuilding contest so extreme muscularity will be marked down."
- Shorts: "Shorts must be just above the knee in length and can be one inch below the belly button." "no spandex and no logos".
- The page sets no separate leg criteria and names no muscle groups.

**Observed disagreement on shorts length:** NPC "two inches above the kneecap"; 2Bros "just above the knee"; IFBB "down to the upper point of the knee". All three cover the quads; none scores them.

### 1.2 Classic Physique

**NPC** (https://npcnewsonline.com/classic-physique/):
- "Judges shall score competitors according to the 'total package', which is a balance of size, symmetry, and muscularity."
- "During the call-outs, judges shall have the opportunity to compare competitors against each other."
- Mandatory poses: Front Double Biceps, Side Chest, Back Double Biceps, Abdominals and Thighs, Favorite Classic Pose (no Most Muscular).
- Trunks: "black trunks made from cloth fabric." The page has no sentence on physique ideal, legs, waist or shoulders.

**IFBB federation Classic Physique rules 2018** (https://ifbb.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/Classic-Physique-Rules-2018.pdf, text extracted):
- Art. 7.1: judges assess "the degree of proportion, symmetry, muscle size and quality (density, separation, definition) as well as skin tone."
- Survey order: "starting from the head, and looking at every part of the physique in a downward sequence... looking for muscular bulk, balanced development, muscular density and definition."
- "The downward survey should take in the head, neck, shoulders, chest, all of the arm muscles, front of the trunk for pectorals, pec-delt tie-in, abdominals, waist, thighs, legs and calves and feet." Back poses add "the upper and lower trapezius, teres and infraspinatus, erector spinae, the gluteus group, the leg biceps group at the back of the thighs and calves and feet."
- Art. 10.2: "The judge should favour competitors with a harmonious, classical physique... correct anatomical structure (including body framework, broad shoulders, high chest, correct spinal curves, limbs and trunk in good proportion, straight legs...)."
- Attire is gymnastic shorts "allowing to display the upper legs" (so legs are fully visible and named in the survey, unlike Men's Physique).

**2Bros** (https://www.2bpevents.com/division-rules/): judging "scored 100%", five mandatory poses as above; "Abdominal and Thighs" is the only mandatory pose involving the legs; "You are NOT to wear board shorts or Bodybuilding posing trunks." No muscle groups named.

NOT FOUND: any ifbbpro.com page for either division; any Classic Physique height/weight table (not requested, not fetched).

## 6. Gaps and cautions for the plan builder

- No source in either division is a controlled off-season template with weekly sets per muscle stated by the coach; the survey (section 4.1) is the only multi-athlete quantity.
- 3-day and 4-day structures: NOT FOUND for Classic; 3-day NOT FOUND for Men's Physique; 4-day Men's Physique rests on a single coach programme (Hanson).
- Several pages disagree with themselves (Dr Muscle for Buendia: 5 or 6 days; Fitness Volt for Cook: "6 days" for a 3-day rotation; Fitness Volt Bumstead prose against table). Day-level tables were preferred over prose.
- Dates: several pages are 2020 to 2023; Hendrickson, Buendia and Terry day-lists come from the Jacked Gorilla and Dr Workout aggregators, and Muscle and Strength states (via search only, not fetched) that its Hendrickson routine combines separate videos, so treat M1 as indicative of structure, not a current plan.
- ED-safety note: nothing in this file touches calorie floors or ED gating; it is training-structure evidence only.
