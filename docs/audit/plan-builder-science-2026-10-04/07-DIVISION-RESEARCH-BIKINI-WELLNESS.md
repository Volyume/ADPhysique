### 3.3 Differences, plainly stated (evidence, then inference)

- Observed: Bikini splits contain 2 to 3 upper sessions with shoulder and back work in nearly every case; Wellness splits contain 1 to 2 upper sessions, with 3 to 4 lower sessions at 5 to 6 days.
- Observed: only Wellness sources separate quads onto their own day (W1, W2, W4); Bikini sources rarely do (B1 does, quads on D1).
- Inference: the number of lower-body sessions is the main structural difference between the two divisions in this sample.

## 5. What the sources say the division does NOT do

### Bikini
- No heavy quad building: "Bikini division emphasizes glutes, shoulders, and overall shape-not quad mass." https://rpstrength.com/blogs/podcasts/the-science-of-training-female-bodybuilders-division-specific-strategies
- Not much upper back: "Most bikini girls shouldn't do TOO much upper back work (judges don't want much development there)." https://nextlevelbikiniprep.com/5-keys-bikini-competition-training/
- Not much adduction, oblique or direct ab work before lean: "You really can't afford to do a lot of hip ADDuction"; "Avoid side bends/side planks, and torso rotation." Same URL.
- No dedicated arm day: "If you have a dedicated arm day, you're overtraining them." https://fivestarrphysique.com/bodybuilding-contest-prep/upper-body-balance-for-bikini/
- No pec focus: "no competitor has ever been told to work on their pecs." Same URL.
- Not striated, dense or separated: NPC "Should NOT display" list (section 1.1), https://npcnewsonline.com/bikini-rules/
- Not equal-effort upper body: Contreras advises against giving upper body muscles "the same effort you hammer the glutes". https://bretcontreras.com/how-to-train-for-a-bikini-competition-its-all-about-glutes/
- Not a counter-claim: OCB asks for "some shoulder development and back muscularity" and "some development with some fullness in the quadriceps", so a fully minimal quad or back approach is not universal. https://ocbonline.com/bikini/

### Wellness
- No big shoulders or wide back: "Judges do NOT want big shoulders or a wide back."; "Do NOT flare your lats. That's a figure cue, not a wellness cue."; "Wellness is not a shredded division." https://ryanvltn.com/blog/wellness-division-bodybuilding
- No dense upper body: "Dense/overly muscular upper body- should be more on par with bikini/slightly bigger." https://www.ifbbproleague.com.au/uploads/b/994ea660-d8aa-11ed-9014-378d597f6cc4/Judging%20Criteria%20-%20Wellness.pdf
- No quad-dominant legs or etched hamstrings: "Balanced look to the side leg-not overly quad dominant."; "Overly etched in or developed hamstrings." Same URL.
- No heavy chest work in most programmes: survey median 1 set (section 4.1); chest absent from W2, W3, W4.
- Stay under the division line: "it's essential to avoid excessive muscle mass that could push you into different divisions." https://www.roxstarfitness.com/the-ultimate-beginners-guide-to-wellness-physique-competitions/
- No striations: NPC "no striations" in glutes, shoulders and quads. https://npcnewsonline.com/npc-and-ifbb-professional-league-wellness-rules/537583/

## 6. Limits of this research

- Sample sizes are small and mixed in quality; only B1, B3, B7, W1, W2, W3 are named people with a full visible split. Five or six of the 13 splits are blog or commercial pages.
- No fetched source gave weekly direct-set targets per muscle for a recommended plan; the only set figures are the 2025 survey medians and one athlete's listed sets.
- UKBFF, PCA, WNBF criteria, Revelia, Findley, Helms, Nunes, Teixeira and Team Elite Physique splits: NOT FOUND as readable pages this session.
- Do not treat the "modal" rows as evidence-weighted: they are counts of pages (section 3).
