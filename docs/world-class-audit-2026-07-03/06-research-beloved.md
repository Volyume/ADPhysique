# Track 6: research — what makes apps beloved (calm/ED-safe transferable)

TOP 12 BY FIT (full evidence in agent report; strongest sources: Apple case study on
Gentler Streak; peer-reviewed PMC8485346 + PMC5700836 on MFP red-number ED harm;
Strava kudos causal study sciencedirect S0378873322000909; RevenueCat/Sensor Tower):
1. Activity-Path BAND replaces chain-streak: training-load-aware corridor, rest day
   reads "on track" never "broken" (Gentler Streak — same audience, proven).
2. "No red numbers" now EVIDENCE-BACKED (peer-reviewed) — cite in design rules.
3. Deterministic "wrapped" seasonal/annual recap (Gentler Streak reflective register,
   not Spotify competitive) — pure data viz, no AI.
4. Hevy-style shareable workout card export (free tier, opt-in, no feed).
5. Streak-freeze reframed as PLANNED REST within the programme (Duolingo +48% streak
   longevity mechanic minus the guilt).
6. Identity copy over compliance copy: "6 weeks training three times a week" not
   "6-day streak" (Bem self-perception).
7. RPE/RIR progressive disclosure gated by logged-set history (beginners can't
   self-rate for 3-6 months; Metric/StrengthLog gate it).
8. Sub-90-second install→first-logged-set benchmark (Hevy ~90s, Strong minimal).
9. Daily deterministic one-line "how's today looking" brief on open (Oura ritual,
   rules-based: "deload week, lighter targets today").
10. MacroFactor-style fast-track calibration import + honest "targets sharpen over
    weeks" expectation-setting.
11. Non-comparative "someone noticed your consistency" affirmation (kudos effect
    without comparison; partner feature is the natural carrier).
12. Distinct "planned rest" vs "missed" day states in all consistency visuals
    ("null day" pattern).

top retention) prove calm loops are commercially load-bearing. Fitbod's lesson:
retention risk lives at workouts 2-7 continuity, not day-1 polish. Churn predictor:
<3 sessions in first 14 days = 3-4x churn — instrument THAT metric.
