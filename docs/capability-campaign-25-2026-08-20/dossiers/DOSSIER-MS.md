# EVIDENCE DOSSIER: multiple sclerosis

profileId: multiple_sclerosis

## 1. Target population or problem
Adults with multiple sclerosis, all courses; the strongest literature of
the wheelchair-relevant conditions surveyed (multiple 2024-2026
meta-analyses, 12-13 RCTs each; R5 section 7).

## 2. Purpose
Question selection (balance, grip, impact), fatigue-honest framing,
family pointers, cited education. No MS-labelled programming.

## 3. Variability
"Everyone's MS is different, and you know best how your MS affects what
you can do" (MS Trust, verbatim). Week-to-week variability is the
defining product fact; the episode machinery and free session length are
the designed answers.

## 4. Sources (live-verified 2026-08-21)
- MS Trust, Starting to exercise with MS,
  https://mstrust.org.uk/information-support/exercise-ms/starting-exercise (T2).
- Journal of Clinical Medicine 2024 13(5):1378 systematic review,
  https://doi.org/10.3390/jcm13051378 (T3; DOI recorded from R5).
- McManaman et al. 2025, PM&R, walking-speed meta-analysis (T3; R5).

## 5. Supported strength-training principles
Resistance training is consistently supported across recent
meta-analyses for strength and function; a two-to-three-times-weekly
pattern appears alongside general activity guidance (R5).

## 6. Unsupported conclusions
No single "MS exercise" exists; parameters do not template. Heat
mitigation specifics (pre-cooling etc.) are charity practice, not traced
primary evidence (CR-8).

## 7. Movement and setup considerations
Supported/machine bases for variable balance; cooler environments noted
as a personal preference line only.

## 8. Exercise-library requirements
Balance-supported coverage (registry: 264), grip-light coverage (142),
seated coverage (241).

## 9. Movement metadata requirements
balance_demand, grip_demand, impact; all live.

## 10. Generalisable parameters
Only that strength work is supported and personal; no dose is carried.

## 11. Parameters that remain individual
Energy, heat response, balance, session length, week shape.

## 12. Clinical boundary
"Do not push through fatigue" is clinical framing (CR-7): the app never
encodes it as an autoregulation rule; the fatigueNote states energy is
personal and shorter weeks are never judged, which is product framing,
not symptom instruction. Heat mitigation stays out (CR-8). Relapse
handling belongs to the MS team.

## 13. Claim and language risks
Never course-modifying claims; never fatigue-management claims.

## 14. Accessibility considerations
None MS-specific beyond standing app law.

## 15. Routine implications
Families: Steady-Base Full Body, Supported Machine Builder, Seated Full
Body, Dumbbell & Band Foundations. No MS-labelled plan before EXPERT +
LEG-23.

## 16. Check-in implications
Generic conditional question suffices; energy variability rides 33.12
levers, never new questionnaires.

## 17. Reintroduction implications
Generic conservative model; flare re-start (saved shape) is the
designed repeated-episode path.

## 18. Review date and version
2026-08-21, v1.

SUPPORTED, AUTOMATED TESTED. Not DEVICE TESTED, not EXPERT REVIEWED,
not USER VALIDATED, not MARKETING READY.
