# R1 — Progress-Photo Tools in Physique / Bodybuilding Apps: Competitor Teardown

Research date: 2026-07-03. Author: research agent (evidence-led, competitor apps are closed-source).

**Evidence tags:** `[OBSERVED]` = seen in a demo/screenshot/video/review; `[DOCUMENTED]` = from official docs/help/store listing; `[INFERRED]` = reasoned, not confirmed. Confidence is stated honestly — most competitor internals cannot be verified without a live account, so a lot is `[DOCUMENTED]` (their marketing claim) rather than `[OBSERVED]`.

Two families are covered:
- **A. Coaching-platform photo galleries** (coach reviews client photos): Trainerize, TrueCoach, Everfit, Hevy.
- **B. Dedicated body-progress photo apps** (solo user): Metamorph, Progress Pics "Then & Now", BodyShapr, "Progress", PhotoJourney/My Body Tracker, LocalOne Gym Pics, GainFrame, BodySpace.

---

## A1. ABC Trainerize

Market-leading coaching platform; the photo tool is a client-facing feature inside a broader coaching app.

**1. Capture flow** — Client (or coach) snaps photos in-app; the app prompts for **front, side, and back** each time a set is logged. `[DOCUMENTED]` The mobile camera shows **guiding lines** to help repeat framing. `[DOCUMENTED]` (Trainerize help + blog.)

**2. Dating / labelling / metadata** — Photos are **auto date-stamped** and each capture is a "set" tagged by pose (front/side/back). `[DOCUMENTED]` Body stats (weight, body-fat, measurements) are logged separately and can be shown alongside. `[DOCUMENTED]`

**3. Organisation** — Sets are ordered **newest-to-oldest**; browsable as a dated timeline plus built-in graphs/reports. `[DOCUMENTED]`

**4. COMPARISON experience** — The headline interaction: on mobile, **rotate the phone to landscape and two selected photos snap into a side-by-side view**; the user screenshots it for a marketing image. `[DOCUMENTED]` Comparison is **two-up side-by-side only** — no before/after slider, no aligned overlay, no time-lapse. A long-standing top feature request is manual photo selection for the side-by-side and doing it on **desktop** (currently mobile-only), which trainers find limiting because they work on a computer. `[OBSERVED]` (Trainerize Idea Forum threads.)

**5. Consistency aids** — Camera **guiding lines**; blog content coaches "same pose each time." No ghost-overlay of the previous shot at capture. `[DOCUMENTED]` / `[INFERRED]` (no overlay found).

**6. Privacy model** — Cloud-hosted (coach and client both see photos); no stated on-device-only mode, no face-blur. Photos live in the coaching account. `[INFERRED]` (help docs don't detail storage/encryption).

**7. What RETAINS vs what CHURNS** — Retains: the landscape side-by-side is fast and "instant marketing asset." `[OBSERVED]` Churns: coaches complain they **can't save client photos in high quality or straight to phone**, and **can't compare on desktop** — "many trainers do all client work on the computer and it doesn't make sense to open the mobile version to compare." `[OBSERVED]` General reliability gripes about lost logged data also surface. `[OBSERVED]`

**8. ED / body-image concerns** — None surfaced specific to the photo tool; framing is explicitly **marketing/transformation-oriented** (before/after as "your best fitness marketing tool"), which is the opposite of ED-safe positioning. `[OBSERVED]`

## A2. TrueCoach

Coach-client platform; photos are part of client check-ins.

**1. Capture flow** — Client taps a camera icon and either **takes a photo in-app or picks from camera roll**; uploads **front, side, back** poses for coach review. `[DOCUMENTED]`

**2. Dating / labelling / metadata** — Each photo **set is dated**; a date picker (swipe left/right by month) lets clients **back-date older photos**. **One set per calendar date** — "Once you have uploaded a progress photo set for the day, you will not be able to upload another … for the same date." `[DOCUMENTED]` Body metrics (weight, body-fat, circumferences) tracked separately and graphed. `[DOCUMENTED]`

**3. Organisation** — Toggle between the comparison view and per-set view via an **arrow icon top-right**; dated history. `[DOCUMENTED]`

**4. COMPARISON experience** — Once ≥2 sets exist, a **"then and now" view** shows earliest vs latest side-by-side. `[DOCUMENTED]` It is a **two-up side-by-side**; no slider, overlay, or time-lapse found. Coach dashboard has its own Progress Pictures tab to compare sets and view metric graphs. `[DOCUMENTED]`

**5. Consistency aids** — Pose labels only. No documented camera overlay/ghost or on-capture alignment guide. `[INFERRED]`

**6. Privacy model** — Cloud; photos shared with the coach by design. No on-device-only mode, encryption detail, or face-blur documented. `[INFERRED]`

**7. RETAINS vs CHURNS** — Retains: simple "then and now" makes progress legible to clients. Churns: reviewers note check-ins are **limited to progress updates rather than structured review forms**, and photo **quality/export** complaints echo Trainerize. `[OBSERVED]`

**8. ED / body-image concerns** — None specific; but the coach-visibility model means a client's body is under continuous external review, which is a body-checking amplifier by design. `[INFERRED]`

## A3. Everfit

Coaching platform with a comparatively richer photo comparator.

**1. Capture flow** — Client or coach uploads via **"+ Add"**; drag/drop or file-select, **up to 10 photos at once**. `[DOCUMENTED]` (More of an upload flow than an in-app guided camera.)

**2. Dating / labelling / metadata** — Tag each image **front / left / right / back**; enter **weight and body-fat** at upload (edits apply to the whole batch). Organised chronologically with metrics shown top-right. `[DOCUMENTED]`

**3. Organisation** — Chronological library; **"View All"** browses the full set. `[DOCUMENTED]`

**4. COMPARISON experience** — **Side-by-side comparison with drag-and-drop arrangement**, plus **per-photo zoom** (hover zoom icon), **aspect-ratio adjustment**, pan while zoomed, and a **reset-to-100%** button. `[DOCUMENTED]` No slider; **no ghost/aligned overlay** — competitors note Kahunas layers photos and "Everfit doesn't match" that. `[OBSERVED]` Separately, Everfit added a **Responses Comparison** to view any two check-in forms side-by-side (week 1 vs week 12). `[DOCUMENTED]`

**5. Consistency aids** — Pose tags; zoom/pan for like-for-like inspection. No capture-time overlay. `[INFERRED]`

**6. Privacy model** — Cloud; client and coach share access. No on-device-only/encryption/face-blur detail documented. `[INFERRED]`

**7. RETAINS vs CHURNS** — Retains: zoom + ratio control makes the side-by-side genuinely usable for coaches; batch upload saves time. `[OBSERVED]` Churns: **no overlay** is the noted gap vs rivals. `[OBSERVED]`

**8. ED / body-image concerns** — None specific; same external-review dynamic as other coaching tools. `[INFERRED]`

## A4. Hevy (workout logger, photos as a secondary feature)

**1. Capture flow** — Profile > Measures > **+** > "Add Picture" to take a photo or upload from library. `[DOCUMENTED]`

**2. Dating / labelling / metadata** — **Auto-timestamped**; library shows the upload date per photo. No pose tags in-app (the guide only *recommends* front/side/back). `[DOCUMENTED]`

**3. Organisation** — Dated library via "See All." `[DOCUMENTED]`

**4. COMPARISON experience** — Tap any photo → option to **position it side-by-side with another** from the library. `[DOCUMENTED]` Two-up only; no slider/overlay/time-lapse.

**5. Consistency aids** — Written guidance only: "same poses each time," consistent lighting/angle/time of day. **No built-in overlay or pose guide.** `[DOCUMENTED]`

**6. Privacy model** — **Progress photos are always private even on a public profile**: "Even if your profile is public, progress photos are private to track visual improvements over time." Only workout media is social. A **Share** button generates an external image on demand. `[DOCUMENTED]` (Cloud-stored, but access-private.)

**7. RETAINS vs CHURNS** — Retains: strong privacy default; photos tie to a beloved logger. Churns: photos are explicitly a **secondary feature**, so power users of physique tracking outgrow it. `[OBSERVED]`

**8. ED / body-image concerns** — None specific; private-by-default is a mild protective factor. `[INFERRED]`

## B1. Metamorph — "Progress Pic Photos" (dedicated, privacy-first)

Frequently ranked the strongest dedicated iPhone progress-photo app of 2025-26.

**1. Capture flow** — In-app camera; take face/body/fitness/weight-loss series. `[DOCUMENTED]`

**2. Dating / labelling / metadata** — Automatic sequence dating within a photo series. `[DOCUMENTED]`

**3. Organisation** — Photo series (per body area), reminders (daily/weekly/custom). `[DOCUMENTED]`

**4. COMPARISON experience** — **Time-lapse video** from a series, plus **before/after** exports. `[DOCUMENTED]` (Slider not confirmed; headline output is the time-lapse.)

**5. Consistency aids** — Headline feature: **ghost overlay at capture** — "an alignment overlay shows a ghost of your previous shot so you can match your position, distance, and angle before you capture anything." `[DOCUMENTED]` This is the single most-praised mechanic across dedicated apps.

**6. Privacy model** — **On-device / user's iCloud only**: "Your photos stay on your device or iCloud — the developer does not store, or even have access to your photos … does not collect any data." `[DOCUMENTED]`

**7. RETAINS vs CHURNS** — Retains: **privacy-first + alignment overlay + reliable reminders**. `[OBSERVED]` Churns/limits: reviewers note it is **"a photo storage and alignment tool, not an analysis tool"** — deliberately **no AI, no body-fat estimate, no physique score.** For an ED-safe app that "limitation" is actually the safe design. `[OBSERVED]`

**8. ED / body-image concerns** — None surfaced; the deliberate absence of scoring/rating is protective. `[INFERRED]`

## B2. Progress Pics "Then & Now" (dedicated, on-device)

Small but feature-dense recent entrant; 5.0★ on a low rating count.

**1. Capture flow** — In-app camera with **voice-activated timer**, **body-silhouette templates**, **pinch-to-zoom while shooting**, and **crop persistence**. `[DOCUMENTED]`

**2. Dating / labelling / metadata** — **Auto date-stamp**; tracks up to **10 metrics** (weight, body-fat, mood, cost, custom measurements); **Apple Health** sync. `[DOCUMENTED]`

**3. Organisation** — Calendar with **streaks/stats**, metric charts over time. `[DOCUMENTED]`

**4. COMPARISON experience** — The most complete of the set: **time-lapse video**, **side-by-side with metric overlays**, AND an **interactive slider between two photos**. `[DOCUMENTED]`

**5. Consistency aids** — **Ghost overlays** at capture + **body silhouette templates** + voice timer for hands-free identical framing. `[DOCUMENTED]`

**6. Privacy model** — **On-device / personal iCloud only**: "Your photos never leave your device. No accounts. No sign-ups. No ads. No data tracking. Ever." Plus **album-level Face ID/Touch ID lock** that **auto-locks when the app closes**. `[DOCUMENTED]`

**7. RETAINS vs CHURNS** — Retains (verbatim reviews): *"this actually lets me compare them side by side and the ghost overlay helps me take the photo from the same spot"* (Zetochi, ★★★★★); *"clean and simple app without all the bloat … keeps all your photos on your phone, so no privacy concerns"* (Quizim, ★★★★★); *"Simple, private and useful … album level lock — no need to flood your photo roll"* (Iampr, ★★★★★). `[OBSERVED]` Churn risk: tiny install base / unproven longevity. `[INFERRED]`

**8. ED / body-image concerns** — None in reviews; note the **mood metric + streaks** are a double edge — streaks can nudge daily body-checking. `[INFERRED]`

## PATTERNS ACROSS THIS SET (what the good ones share)

1. **Pose triad is universal.** Every serious tool structures photos as **front / side / back** (Everfit adds left/right). If VOLYUME logs photos, this is the expected schema. `[DOCUMENTED]`
2. **Auto-dating is table stakes.** Nobody makes the user type a date; the date is the spine of the whole feature. Most also allow **back-dating** older photos into the timeline (TrueCoach). `[DOCUMENTED]`
3. **The ghost overlay at capture is the single most-loved mechanic** in dedicated apps (Metamorph, Then & Now, PhotoJourney) and its **absence** is the top complaint against coaching platforms. It's a *consistency* aid, not a *comparison* aid — and users explicitly credit it in reviews. `[OBSERVED]`
4. **Comparison ladder, roughly in order of sophistication:** two-up side-by-side (everyone) → **before/after slider** (BodyShapr, Then & Now) → **aligned/ghost overlay** (Kahunas, capture-time overlays) → **time-lapse video** (Metamorph, Then & Now, BodyShapr, PhotoJourney). Coaching platforms stop at side-by-side; dedicated apps go further. `[DOCUMENTED]`
5. **Privacy is a headline selling point for dedicated apps**, phrased almost identically: on-device / personal iCloud, **no accounts, no data collection**, often **album-level biometric lock** and **separation from the main camera roll**. This is a *marketing win*, not just compliance. `[OBSERVED]`
6. **One-time purchase reads as trustworthy; subscriptions read as "predatory"** in this niche's reviews. `[OBSERVED]`
7. **Metrics ride alongside photos** (weight, body-fat, custom measurements, sometimes mood + streaks). Useful, but streaks/daily reminders quietly push toward daily body-checking. `[OBSERVED]`
8. **Coaching platforms frame photos as marketing/transformation assets**; dedicated privacy apps frame them as a **private personal record.** Opposite tones. `[OBSERVED]`

## WHAT TO STEAL / WHAT TO AVOID (for a calm, ED-safe, on-device physique app)

**STEAL**
- **Front / side / back pose tagging** with auto-date and optional back-dating — the universally understood schema. `[DOCUMENTED]`
- **Ghost overlay at capture** (fade the previous same-pose shot on the viewfinder). It is the highest-love, most-cited feature and it is a *consistency/quality* aid, not a body-scrutiny aid — the safest thing to take. Pair with a light silhouette guide and a hands-free voice/self-timer (Then & Now) so the user isn't fussing at the mirror. `[OBSERVED]`
- **True on-device privacy** with **album-level biometric lock that auto-locks on backgrounding** and **keeps photos out of the main camera roll** (Then & Now). This aligns perfectly with the SQLCipher/on-device-truth architecture and EU-Dublin residency posture, and it's a genuine differentiator vs cloud coaching tools. `[DOCUMENTED]`
- **Time-lapse and simple two-up side-by-side** as the *comparison* surface — but see the ED guardrails below on how to gate/present it.
- **One-time / included-in-Pro** rather than a nagging separate subscription — matches the niche's trust signals. `[OBSERVED]`

**AVOID (hard lines for an ED-safe app)**
- **No AI physique scoring / body-fat-from-photo / "what changed" analysis** (GainFrame pattern). This is number-chasing, it violates the "no AI, deterministic engine" constitution, and it's a body-image hazard. `[OBSERVED]`
- **No community/social gallery or feedback on photos** (BodySpace). External appraisal of one's body is the highest-risk model here. `[INFERRED]`
- **No "before/after marketing asset" framing** (Trainerize/TrueCoach). Clinical sources are explicit: **progress photos are a form of body checking**, and **before/after comparison "reduces recovery to what you look like"** and acts as a **maintenance factor** for disordered patterns — it "strengthens cognitive distortions around shape, weight and self-worth." Don't celebrate transformation exports; don't auto-generate shareable before/afters. `[OBSERVED]` (First Steps ED; Aster Springs; Equip.)
- **Don't gamify frequency.** Streaks + daily reminders on photos nudge daily body-checking. Prefer **low-frequency defaults** (e.g. every 2–4 weeks), user-set, never a streak, never a shame nudge. `[INFERRED]` from the body-checking literature.
- **Suppress photo prompts/comparison under an open ED flag** — consistent with VOLYUME's existing rule that weight/food-adjacent notifications suppress under an open ED flag. `[INFERRED]` (maps to CLAUDE.md ED-safety mandate.)
- **Offer non-appearance progress markers alongside** (strength PBs, consistency, how clothes/lifts feel, mood) so visual change is never the only lens — directly the alternative recommended by ED clinicians. `[DOCUMENTED]` (First Steps ED.)
- **No face-blur was found in any competitor** — a genuine gap. An **optional face-blur / crop-to-torso** default would be a calm, protective differentiator worth considering (surface as a founder decision, per no-parking rule). `[INFERRED]`
